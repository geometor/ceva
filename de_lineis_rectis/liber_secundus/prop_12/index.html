Proposition XII: Cubic Area Ratio on Three Parabola Tangents¶

Proposition XII · Theorem XI (pp. 48–49, Tab. 6, Fig. 50)

In the same parabolic tangent triangle with concurrency point :g:`H`, the ratio of the two sub-triangles $\triangle FHA$ and $\triangle DHC$ equals the **triplicate (cubic)* ratio of the tangent segments FE to ED:*

$$\triangle FHA : \triangle DHC = \overline{FE}^3 : \overline{ED}^3.$$

Scholium: Notes the immediate extension to area ratios of all remaining sub-triangles around H.
