Proposition XX: Parallel Segments Cut by a Transversal¶

Proposition XX · Theorem XVII (p. 58, Tab. 8, Fig. 67)

If from the extremity of any straight line we draw two lines to the endpoints of another line parallel to the first, and then from the other extremity of the first parallel another line be drawn cutting the two former lines between the parallels: the ratio of the parallels will be compounded from two ratios, of which one is formed from the portions of the cutting line, and the other from the segment and the whole straight line intercepted between the parallels upon which the cutting line itself terminates, so that all homologous terms are coterminous.

Let there be two parallel lines DC, EK, and from the same endpoint D let us draw two lines to the endpoints of the other parallel, which are DK, DE; moreover from the other endpoint C let another straight line CIB be drawn cutting DK at I and DE at B. I say that the ratio of DC to EK is compounded from the two ratios CI to IB and BD to DE. Since BC cuts DC, one of the parallels, if produced it will also meet the other parallel KE produced at A. Since therefore DC to EK is compounded of the ratios of straight lines DC to AE and AE to EK, and as DC to AE, so DB to BE because of the parallels, DC to EK will be compounded of the ratios DB to BE and AE to EK. Yet in the First Element whose weights are A, K, D and center B, the ratio of straight line AE to EK, namely of weight K to A, is compounded of the ratios of weights K to I to A, namely of straight lines DI to IK and AB to BI, that is, of straight lines IC to CA and AB to BI. Therefore the ratio DC to EK will be compounded of the ratios DB to BE, IC to CA, and AB to BI. And since IC to CA arises from the ratios IC to CB and CB to CA, and as CB to CA, so DB to DE, DC to EK will be compounded of the ratios DB to BE, IC to CB, DB to DE, and AB to BI. Yet AB to BI is compounded of AB to BC and BC to BI, and AB is to BC as EB to BD; therefore the aforesaid ratio DC to EK will be compounded of DB to BE, IC to CB, BD to DE, EB to BD, and BC to BI, or of the same taken perturbedly—that is, of DB to BE to BD to DE and IC to CB to BI—nay, of the two ratios BD to DE and IC to BI, which was to be proved.

Corollary.

It is manifest that, given the two ratios BD to DE and IC to BI, the ratio DC to EK compounded from them is also given.
