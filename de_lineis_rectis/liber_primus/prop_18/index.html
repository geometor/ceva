Proposition XVIII: First Pyramid Ratio of Element IV¶

Proposition XVIII · Theorem VIII (p. 26, Tab. 2, Fig. 20)

With the same figure of the Fourth Element posited, let lines EA, FC be drawn moreover; I say that GF to GE is as the pyramid whose base is triangle FDC, and altitude AB, to the pyramid whose base is triangle ADE and altitude CB.

Compounded is the proportion of straight line GF to GE, namely that of weight E to F, of the ratios of heavy bodies E to C, C to A, and A to F, namely of straight lines CD to DE, AB to BC, and FD to DA, or of the same taken perturbedly, namely from CD to DE, FD to DA, and AB to BC, that is, from the ratios of triangle FDC to triangle EDA, and from straight line AB to BC; but from the same ratios is compounded the pyramid whose base is triangle FDC, and altitude AB, to the pyramid whose base is triangle ADE and altitude BC; therefore as the former pyramid to the latter, so GF to GE, which &c.

Corollary.

It is clear that, if straight line AB be equal to straight line BC, triangle FDC is to triangle ADE as straight line GF to GE.
