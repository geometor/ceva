Liber Primus · Historical & Mathematical Commentary¶

1. Caravaggio Jr., Commandino & the Theory of Compound Ratios¶

In Proposition VI, Ceva includes the synthetic parallel-line demonstration of Pietro Paolo Caravaggio Jr., proving that the concurrency criterion holds purely by Euclidean geometry ([VI.4]) without mechanics. In Lemma IV and Proposition VII, Ceva grounds the calculation in the theory of compound ratios established by Federico Commandino and Christoph Clavius (1589), showing that multiplying proportions along the perimeter constitutes a rigorous operation on Euclidean magnitudes.

2. Projective Harmonic Conjugation & Straightedge Construction¶

By juxtaposing Ceva’s concurrency (Prop. VII) with Menelaus’s transversal (Prop. VIII), Ceva arrives at projective harmonic division:

$$\frac{\overline{BD}}{\overline{DC}} = -\frac{\overline{BD'}}{\overline{D'C}} \qquad \Longleftrightarrow \qquad (B, C; D, D') = -1.$$

In modern projective geometry, this means the cevian triangle and base transversal form a complete quadrangle whose diagonal points isolate harmonic conjugates. Ceva showed that harmonic conjugates can be constructed using an unmarked straightedge alone, anticipating Girard Desargues and nineteenth-century projective synthetic geometry (von Staudt).

3. From F - A to Möbius’s Barycentrischer Calcul (1827)¶

On printed page 5 (Elementum III Scholium), Ceva’s introduction of subtractive weights “more analytico, seu algebrico” as $F - A$ (pondus negativum) represents a conceptual breakthrough. By allowing weights to take negative signs, Ceva extended statics across the entire plane, establishing homogeneous barycentric coordinates $(u : v : w) \in \mathbb{P}^2(\mathbb{Q})$ nearly 150 years before August Ferdinand Möbius published Der barycentrische Calcul (1827).

4. Commandino’s 3:1 Centroid & the 7-Line Spatial Equilibrium¶

In Proposition XXIII, Ceva extends statics into solid geometry. In 1565, Federico Commandino proved in De centro gravitatis solidorum (1565) that the centroid of a tetrahedron divides each median joining a vertex to the opposite face’s centroid in the ratio $3 : 1$. Ceva generalizes Commandino’s theorem to arbitrary non-equal masses $(m_A, m_B, m_C, m_D)$, demonstrating that all 4 vertex-to-face cevians and all 3 skew-edge bimedians intersect at the unique center of gravity $O$ “mira quadam concordia”—a 7-line spatial concurrency theorem.

5. Exact Symbolic Realization in geometor.ceva¶

In geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.solve_static_barycentrics(), vertex weights $(u, v, w)$ of any non-degenerate signs over $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$, or symbolic polynomial rings yield exact coordinates without floating-point approximations. In geometor.ceva.polygon_tetrahedron.solve_tetrahedron_static_equilibrium(), 3D tetrahedral equilibrium across all 7 lines is verified symbolically, confirming the vanishing concurrency residuals on non-coplanar vertices.
