Proposition XXIV: Five Concurrent Lines in a Quadrangular Pyramid¶

Proposition XXIV · Theorem XIII

Let there be a pyramid whose vertex is E, and whose base is any quadrilateral CBAD; and within triangle BEC, three straight lines being drawn intersecting one another at the same point N, such as are the lines BNK, CNO, ENP, so that the figure of the Second Element is made; let AO, DK be joined, and in triangles BEA, CED let two figures of the same Element be completed, BOEFARQ and CKEGDML; and with lines AG, DF joined intersecting each other at H, let EH be produced to I, and let two lines IP, RM be joined so that they meet each other at point S. Finally, let the lines ES, QM, HP, LR, NI be conceived within the pyramid; I say that these are cut at the same point, such as T. Now to demonstrate this, let us suspend, as was done in the preceding, from the angles of the same pyramid five weights ✠, V, X, Z, ℞, of which any two to the remaining three, or two to two, or two to one, or one to one, or one to four, will be reciprocally among themselves as the lengths of those balance-beams from which they hang.

(Tab. 3, Fig. 29) Let it be made as DG to GE, so weight ℞ to ✠; as CK to KE, so ℞ to Z; and as AF to FE, so ℞ to V. Now since in each of the four figures of the Second Element which on the surface of the pyramid, the base excepted,

we have constructed, we have adapted three weights according to the laws of the Second Element, BR will be to RA as weight V to ✠; CP to PB as ✠ to Z; and DM to MC as Z to X. Wherefore since DI to IA, that is, weight V to X, is compounded of the ratios of weights V to ✠, ✠ to Z, and Z to X, namely of straight lines BR to RA, CP to PB, and DM to MC, ARBPCMDIAS will be a figure of the Fifth Element, whose heavy bodies are V, ✠, Z, X; thus since their center is at S, and heavy body ℞ is at E, on line ES will be the center of gravity of all the heavy bodies V, ✠, Z, X, ℞. Again, because at R is the center of heavy bodies V, ✠, and at L the center of heavy bodies ℞, Z, X, the center of all heavy bodies V, ✠, Z, X, ℞ will likewise be on balance-beam RL in the same former position; and since this is unique, and by the same reason exists likewise on balance-beams NI, QM, HP, it is necessary that all the said lines ES, LR, NI, QM, HP intersect one another at the same point, such as T; and since this is at once the center of the same balance-beams, it is established that RT to TL is as the three heavy bodies Z, ℞, X to the two V, ✠; IT to TN as the three heavy bodies ✠, ℞, Z to the two V, X; MT to TQ as the three ✠, V, ℞ to the two Z, X; HT to TP as the two ✠, Z to the three V, ℞, X; and finally ET to TS as the four V, ✠, Z, X to ℞; and the remaining ratios are manifest from the Second and Fifth Elements; therefore the proposition is clear.
