Unit I · Historical & Mathematical Commentary¶

1. Al-Mu’taman ibn Hūd (11th Century) vs. Ceva’s Static Revolution (1678)¶

For three centuries Giovanni Ceva was universally regarded as the sole discoverer of the concurrency criterion $\frac{AF}{FB}\cdot\frac{BD}{DC}\cdot\frac{CE}{EA} = 1$. In 1985, historian Jan P. Hogendijk (1995) discovered that Yusuf al-Mu'taman ibn Hud (d. 1085), Hudid King of Zaragoza, had already proved the theorem synthetically in his Arabic Kitāb al-Istikmāl (Book of Perfection) by iterated applications of Menelaus's transversal theorem.

Because Kitāb al-Istikmāl was never translated into medieval Latin, Ceva’s 1678 discovery in Milan was completely independent—and methodologically far more radical. Where al-Mu’taman worked inside classical Greek proportion theory, Ceva invented barycentric statics: assigning algebraic masses $(u : v : w)$ to points so that incidence theorems reduce to associative vector addition over $\mathbb{Q}$.

2. From A † F and F - A (1678) to Möbius’s Barycentrischer Calcul (1827)¶

Two features of Ceva’s opening pages mark a watershed between Renaissance Archimedeanism (Commandino, Valerio) and modern projective-affine algebra:

  • Typography in Transition (`A † F` and `F - A`): In Lombardy in 1678, Ludovico Monza's press used the obelus † for addition alongside - for subtraction. Ceva’s willingness on page 5 to treat a physical weight “more analytico, seu algebrico” as $F - A$ liberated static moments from the Greek requirement of strictly positive magnitudes. Physically, a negative weight acts as an upward buoyant pull (or pulley tension); algebraically, it yields homogeneous coordinates $(u : v : w) \in \mathbb{P}^2(\mathbb{Q})$ across the entire projective plane, directly anticipating August Ferdinand Möbius (1827).

  • Exact Symbolic Realization in ``geometor.ceva``: In geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.solve_static_barycentrics(), weights $(u, v, w)$ of any non-degenerate signs over $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$, or symbolic polynomial rings produce exact Point2D coordinates without a single floating-point operation or square root, while geometor.ceva.polygon_tetrahedron.verify_skew_quadrilateral_ceva() confirms the vanishing $3\times 3$ coplanarity determinant of Elementum V Corollarium on non-planar 3D vertices.
