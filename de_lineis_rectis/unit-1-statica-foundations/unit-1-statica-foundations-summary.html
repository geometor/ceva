Unit I · Executive Geometric Summary¶

Core Theses of the Frontmatter & Liber Primus Opening (1678, pp. 1–7)¶

  1. The Principle of *Statica Constructio*: Rather than introducing clever auxiliary parallels or circles ad hoc for each incidence theorem, Giovanni Ceva places exact non-zero point masses $(u, v, w)$ at the vertices $(A, B, C)$ of a geometric configuration. Because addition of weighted points is commutative and associative and the global center of gravity $P = \frac{uA + vB + wC}{u + v + w}$ is unique (Axioma I), grouping $(B, C)$ first at fulcrum $D$, $(C, A)$ at $E$, or $(A, B)$ at $F$ forces $AD$, $BE$, and $CF$ to concur at $P$.

  2. Signed / Negative Weights (1678 $\to$ 1827): On printed page 5 (Elementum III Scholium), Ceva extends Archimedes’ physical lever to exterior concurrency by writing subtractive weights “more analytico, seu algebrico” as $F - A$ (pondus negativum). Whenever one vertex carries a negative sign (e.g., $(-u, +v, +w)$ with $u + v + w \neq 0$), exactly two side fulcrums fall externally on extended sides, producing two negative directed ratios and one positive ratio whose product remains $(-)(+)(-) = +1$.

  3. 3D Non-Planar Skew Quadrilateral Coplanarity: In Elementum V Corollarium (pp. 6–7), Ceva proves that four weights $(m_A, m_B, m_C, m_D)$ on a non-planar 3D quadrilateral determine four edge fulcrums $E_{AB}, E_{BC}, E_{CD}, E_{DA}$ in cyclic product $1$ whose opposite joins $E_{AB}E_{CD}$ and $E_{BC}E_{DA}$ intersect at the spatial center $O = \frac{m_A A + m_B B + m_C C + m_D D}{m_A + m_B + m_C + m_D}$, forcing all four spatial fulcrums to be coplanar.
