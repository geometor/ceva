Proposition XII: Hyperbolic Conoid Inscribed in a Conical Frustum¶

Proposition XII · Theorem XII (pp. 76–77, Tab. 10, Fig. 79)

If a frustum of a cone intercepted between two parallel planes encloses a portion of a hyperbolic conoid so that both solids stand on the same base and touch each other along the circumference of this base,

the portion of the conoid will be equal to the frustum of the cone minus that cone whose altitude is common with the portion, and whose base is the common circle.

Around the same axis CF let there be conceived a portion of a hyperbolic conoid and a frustum of a cone, and let their generating figures be hyperbola ACE and trapezium ABDE, so that the two sides AB, DE touch the hyperbola at points A, E, and side BD parallel to AE at point C; then, having joined lines AC, AD, let us conceive a cone whose triangle through axis AC is BAD. I say that portion ACE of the conoid is equal to frustum ABDE of the cone minus cone BAD.

Let any point O be taken on axis CF, and through it let a plane be drawn parallel to base AE, by which plane let those three conceived solids be cut, and let the sections be circles IN, LH, IP.

(margin: Conics III.16) Since as the square of tangent CB is to that of tangent BA, so is rectangle HIL to square IA, by alternation square BC will be to rectangle HIL as square BA to AI, or as square BC to IK; therefore square BC has the same ratio to rectangle HIL as to square IK, and hence square IK will equal rectangle HIL. But (margin: Elements II.6) rectangle HIL together with square LO equals square IO; therefore square IO will be equal to the two squares LO, IK taken together; wherefore circle IN, whose radius is IO, will also be equal to the two circles IP, LH, whose radii are IL, LO.

f. 0094 · p. 77

Now, taking the same rule AE, all the circles AE, IN, BD, etc. of frustum ABDE of the cone are equal to all the circles AE, LH, etc. of the portion of the conoid, together with all the circles IP, BD, etc. of cone BAD; from which it follows that portion ACE of the hyperbolic conoid, together with cone BAD, is equal to frustum ABDE; and subtracting cone BAD in common, portion ACE of the conoid will be equal to frustum ABDE minus cone BAD, which was to be demonstrated.
