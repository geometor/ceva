Statica Prolegomena · Executive Geometric Summary¶

Core Theses of the Frontmatter & Methodological Prooemium (1678)¶

  1. The Principle of *Statica Constructio*: Rather than introducing clever auxiliary parallels or circles ad hoc for each incidence theorem, Giovanni Ceva replaces geometric straight lines with exact non-zero point masses placed at vertices. Because addition of weighted points is commutative and associative and the global center of gravity is unique (Axioma I), grouping base loads into composite fulcrums forces cevian lines to concur at the global center of gravity.

  2. The Epistemological Break from Cavalieri’s Indivisibles: Ceva confesses that in his youth he was obstinately absorbed in attempts at circle quadrature until reading Bonaventura Cavalieri's Geometria Indivisibilibus Continuorum Nova (1635). The theory of indivisibles cured him of naive quadrature illusions, leading him to seek a systematic, mechanical foundation for linear geometry.

  3. The Typography of Mechanics: Milanese printer Ludovico Monza establishes a distinctive typographic convention for static operations: the obelus or dagger † denotes additive weight combination (A † F = $A + F$), while the minus sign - denotes subtractive or analytic negative weights (F - A = $F - A$).
