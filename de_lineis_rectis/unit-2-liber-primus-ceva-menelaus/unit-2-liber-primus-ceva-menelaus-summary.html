Unit II · Executive Geometric Summary¶

Core Results of Liber Primus, Props. VI–XXII (1678, pp. 7–35)¶

  1. Equivalence of Static & Synthetic Proofs (*Props.* VI–VII): Pietro Paolo Caravaggio Jr.'s parallel-line construction through vertex $A$ (Prop. VI) translates Ceva’s static weight cancellation into three pairs of similar triangles ([VI.4]), establishing both necessity and sufficiency (reductio / coincidence of the third foot $F^* = F$ by [V.9]).

  2. Ceva–Menelaus Duality & Straightedge Harmonic Conjugation (*Prop.* VIII): Pairing concurrent cevians $AD, BE, CF$ ($\frac{AF}{FB}\frac{BD}{DC}\frac{CE}{EA} = +1$) with the transversal $EF$ cutting $BC$ at $D'$ ($\frac{AF}{FB}\frac{BD'}{D'C}\frac{CE}{EA} = -1$) yields $\frac{BD}{DC} + \frac{BD'}{D'C} = 0$, proving $(B, C; D, D') = -1$ (construct_cevian_menelaus_harmonic()).

  3. Exact Rational Constellation on Heronian $\triangle(13, 14, 15)$: Choosing $A=(5,12), B=(0,0), C=(14,0)$ ($a=14, b=15, c=13, s=21, \Delta=84, r=4$) places every classical center inside $\mathbb{Q}^2$ with zero radicals:
