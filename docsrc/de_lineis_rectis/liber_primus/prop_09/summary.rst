Proposition IX: Fifteen Combinatorial Cases of Element II — Summary
===================================================================

.. folio:: 0030
   :page: p. 13

*Proposition IX* (pp. 13–15, `Tab. 1, Fig. 5`) solves all $\binom{6}{2} = 15$ inverse ratio problems of Element II (Ceva's interior triangle), demonstrating the algorithmic completeness of the three-weight static method.
