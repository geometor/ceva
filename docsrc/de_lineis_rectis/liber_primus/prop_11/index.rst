Proposition XI: Side Ratio via Triangles and Rectangles
=======================================================

.. folio:: 0034
   :page: p. 17

**Proposition XI · Theorem III** (`Tab. 1, Fig. 5`)

*The figure of the Second Element* :g:`EDABCKF` *being again set out, I say that* :g:`AE` *to* :g:`AC` *is compounded of the ratios of triangles* :g:`CFB` *to* :g:`DFE`, *and of rectangles* :g:`DEK` *to* :g:`BCK`.*

The ratio :g:`EA` to :g:`AC` is compounded of the ratios :g:`AE` to :g:`AD`, :g:`AD` to :g:`AB`, and :g:`AB` to :g:`AC`; and likewise the ratio :g:`AE` to :g:`AD`, namely of weight :g:`D` to :g:`E`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`D` to :g:`C` to :g:`E`, 
namely of the straight lines :g:`CF` to :g:`FD`, and :g:`EK` to :g:`KC`; and equally :g:`AB` to :g:`B` (that is, weight :g:`C` to :g:`B`) is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`C` to :g:`E` to :g:`B`, 
namely of the straight lines :g:`EK` to :g:`KC`, and :g:`BF` to :g:`FE`; therefore :g:`AE` to :g:`AC` is compounded of the ratios :g:`CF` to :g:`FD`, :g:`EK` to :g:`KC`, :g:`AD` to :g:`AB`, :g:`EK` to :g:`KC`, and :g:`BF` to :g:`FE`. 
Similarly :g:`AD` to :g:`AB` is compounded of the ratios :g:`AD` to :g:`DE`, :g:`DE` to :g:`BC`, and :g:`BC` to :g:`BA`, or of the same in perturbed order, namely of the ratios :g:`AD` to :g:`DE`, :g:`CB` to :g:`BA`, and :g:`ED` to :g:`CB`; 
and the ratio :g:`CK` to :g:`KE` is that which is compounded of the ratios :g:`AD` to :g:`DE`, and :g:`CB` to :g:`BA`; wherefore :g:`AD` to :g:`AB` will be constituted of the ratios :g:`CK` to :g:`KE`, and :g:`ED` to :g:`CB`; 
therefore the ratio :g:`AE` to :g:`AC` is compounded of the ratios :g:`CF` to :g:`FD`, :g:`EK` to :g:`KC`, :g:`CK` to :g:`KE`, :g:`ED` to :g:`CB`, :g:`EK` to :g:`KC`, and :g:`BF` to :g:`FE`, 
of which the two :g:`EK` to :g:`KC` and :g:`KC` to :g:`KE` constitute a single ratio :g:`KE` to :g:`KE` of equality, which neither increases nor diminishes the composition of ratios, 
so it is necessary that :g:`AE` to :g:`AC` be compounded of the four ratios :g:`CF` to :g:`FD`, :g:`ED` to :g:`CB`, :g:`EK` to :g:`KC`, and :g:`BF` to :g:`FE`, 
or of the same with their order perturbed, namely of :g:`CF` to :g:`FD`, :g:`BF` to :g:`FE`, :g:`ED` to :g:`CB`, and :g:`EK` to :g:`KC`; 
now the former two ratios :g:`CF` to :g:`FD` and :g:`BF` to :g:`FE` are those which compound the ratio which triangle :g:`CFB` has to

.. folio:: 0035
   :page: p. 18

:g:`DFE`, since the two vertical angles at :g:`F` are equal; 
and the latter two :g:`ED` to :g:`CB`, and :g:`EK` to :g:`KC` compound the rectangle :g:`DEK` to the rectangle :g:`BCK`; therefore :g:`AE` to :g:`AC` is compounded of the ratios of triangle :g:`CFB` to triangle :g:`DFE`, and of rectangle :g:`DEK` to rectangle :g:`BCK`, which was to be shown.
