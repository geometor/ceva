Proposition XI: Side Ratio via Triangles and Rectangles
=======================================================

.. folio:: 0034
   :page: p. 17

**Proposition XI · Theorem III** (pp. 17–18, `Tab. 1, Fig. 5`)

*In Element II (triangle :g:`AEC` with concurrent cevians :g:`AK`, :g:`EB`, :g:`CD` meeting at :g:`F`), the ratio of side :g:`AE` to side :g:`AC` equals the compound ratio of triangles $\triangle CEB, \triangle DFE$ and rectangles $\overline{DE}\cdot\overline{EK}$ and $\overline{BC}\cdot\overline{CK}$.*

Ceva combines the area relations of Element II with *Lemma IV* to exhibit how metric lengths of the ambient triangle interact with the static division feet :g:`D`, :g:`K`, :g:`B`.
