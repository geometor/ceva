Proposition XIV: Dual Pyramid Theorem of Element III
====================================================

.. folio:: 0039
   :page: p. 22

**Proposition XIV · Theorem V** (`Tab. 2, Fig. 16`)

*With the same things remaining, I say that* :g:`CB` *to* :g:`BA` *is as the pyramid whose base is triangle* :g:`FEC` *and altitude* :g:`GD`, *to the pyramid whose base is triangle* :g:`AED` *and altitude* :g:`GF`.

Straight line :g:`CB` to :g:`BA`, namely weight :g:`A` to :g:`C`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`A` to :g:`F`, :g:`F` to :g:`D`, and :g:`D` to :g:`C`, namely of straight lines :g:`EF` to :g:`EA`, :g:`GD` to :g:`GF`, and :g:`EC` to :g:`ED`, 
or of the same taken in permuted order, that is, of the ratios :g:`EF` to :g:`EA`, :g:`EC` to :g:`ED`, and :g:`GD` to :g:`GF`; but from the former two is compounded the ratio of triangle :g:`FEC` to triangle :g:`DEA`, as we said in the preceding theorem; 
therefore :g:`CB` to :g:`BA` is compounded of the ratios of triangle :g:`FEC` to triangle :g:`DEA` and of straight line :g:`GD` to :g:`GF`; that is, :g:`CB` to :g:`BA` is as the pyramid whose base is triangle :g:`FEC` and altitude :g:`GD` to the pyramid whose base is triangle :g:`DEA` and altitude :g:`GF`.

**Corollary.**

*It is manifest that if* :g:`GD` *be equal to* :g:`GF`, :g:`CB` *to* :g:`BA` *will have the same ratio which triangle* :g:`CEF` *has to triangle* :g:`AED`.
