Proposition XX: Twenty Combinatorial Cases of Element V — Summary
=================================================================

.. folio:: 0044
   :page: p. 27

*Proposition XX* (pp. 27–29, `Tab. 2, Figs. 22–23`) completes the combinatorial inventory of the five planar Elements by solving all :math:`\binom{6}{3} = 20` cases of Element V, culminating in **Case XIX** (p. 29) where Ceva invokes cossic algebra (``℞`` and ``✠``, *per secundas radices*) to solve a quadratic weight equation.
