Proposition VIII: Area Proportionality in Element I — Summary
=============================================================

.. folio:: 0029

*Proposition VIII* (pp. 12–13) proves the symmetric four-triangle area proportion :math:`\triangle EAC : \triangle DAB = \triangle EFC : \triangle DFB` in Element I, showing how static lever ratios yield pure area invariants.
