Proposition XXI: Ratio Along an Interior Cevian Cut by a Chord — Summary
========================================================================

.. folio:: 0047
   :page: p. 30

*Proposition XXI* (p. 30, `Tab. 2, Fig. 24`) proves that the chord $DB$ joining two cevian feet cuts the third cevian $AK$ at $L$ in exact ratio $KL : LA = 2G : (I + H)$ — an effortless two-line static proof of the cevian harmonic configuration.
