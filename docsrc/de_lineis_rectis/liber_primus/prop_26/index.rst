Proposition XXVI: Concurrent Lines in a Double Pyramid
======================================================

.. folio:: 0051
   :page: p. 34

.. picture:: 0107.g31.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula III Fig. 31: Double opposite pyramid (bipyramid) concurrency

**Proposition XXVI · Theorem XV** (p. 34, `Tab. 3, Fig. 31`)

*Closing Book I, consider two opposite triangular pyramids sharing a common vertex :g:`H` (a double pyramid / bipyramid) with proportional edge divisions across both shells.*

Balancing opposite weights across the double cone of edges forces the three spatial transversals :g:`NK`, :g:`IM`, and :g:`LO` to concur at a single point :g:`P` lying directly upon the line :g:`GH` produced.
