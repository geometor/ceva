Proposition XVI: Base Ratio in Element III
==========================================

.. folio:: 0040
   :page: p. 23

**Proposition XVI · Theorem VII** (p. 23, `Tab. 2, Fig. 17`)

*With the same things posited, I say that* :g:`BC` *to* :g:`CA` *is as the pyramid whose base is triangle* :g:`FEB` *and altitude* :g:`GD`, *to the pyramid whose base is triangle* :g:`EAG` *and altitude* :g:`FD`.

Compounded is the ratio :g:`CB` to :g:`CA`, namely of weight :g:`A` to :g:`B`, of the ratios of weights :g:`A` to :g:`F`, :g:`F` to :g:`G`, and :g:`G` to :g:`B`, namely of straight lines :g:`EF` to :g:`EA`, :g:`DG` to :g:`DF`, and :g:`EB` to :g:`EG`,
and also from the same taken perturbedly, that is, from :g:`EF` to :g:`EA`, :g:`EB` to :g:`EG`, and :g:`DG` to :g:`DF`;
and triangle :g:`FEB` to triangle :g:`AEG` is compounded of the ratios :g:`FE` to :g:`EA` and :g:`EB` to :g:`EG` (since angle :g:`AEB` is common);
therefore :g:`BC` to :g:`CA` will be as the pyramid whose base is triangle :g:`FEB`, altitude :g:`GD`, to the pyramid whose base is triangle :g:`AEG`, and altitude :g:`DF`, which was &c.
