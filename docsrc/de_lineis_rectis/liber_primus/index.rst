Book I: Static Elements, Ceva's Theorem & 3D Equilibrium
========================================================

.. folio:: 0018
   :page: p. 1

*Liber Primus* (printed pp. 1–34, folios ``0018``–``0051``, `Tabulae I–III, Figs. 1–31`) constructs the complete static geometry of intersecting lines from two Axioms, one Postulate, five Lemmas, and **26 Propositions**. Beginning with :person:`Archimedes's <archimedes>` Law of the Lever (:ref:`Axioms & Lemmas I–II <liber-primus-axiomata-lemmata>`), Ceva establishes his five planar *Elementa*—encompassing :person:`Menelaus's <menelaus>` sector (*Prop. I*), **Ceva's interior concurrency theorem** (*Prop. II*), **analytic negative weights** ``F - A`` (*Prop. III*), the **addition sign** ``✠`` (*Prop. IV*), and the **3D skew-quadrilateral corollary** (*Prop. V*). After recording pure Euclidean parallel-line proofs from :person:`Pietro Paolo Caravaggio Jr. <caravaggio>` (*Props. VI & X*) and exhausting all combinatorial ratio cases (*Props. VI–XXI*), Book I ascends into three dimensions to prove **seven-line concurrency in a tetrahedron** (*Prop. XXIII*) and concurrency in quadrangular pyramids, frusta, and bipyramids (*Props. XXIV–XXVI*).
