Axioms, Postulate, and Lemmas I–II — Summary
============================================

.. folio:: 0018
   :page: p. 1

Ceva establishes the axiomatic foundation of his static geometry on pp. 1–2 (folios ``0018``–``0019``): uniqueness of the center of gravity (*Axioma I*), associative weight composition (*Axioma II*), free assignment of arbitrary vertex weights (*Petitio*), and :person:`Archimedes's <archimedes>` reciprocal Law of the Lever (*Lemmata I–II*, `Tab. 1, Figs. 1–3`).
