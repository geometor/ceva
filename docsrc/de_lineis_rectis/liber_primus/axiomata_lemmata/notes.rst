Axioms, Postulate, and Lemmas I–II — Notes
==========================================

.. folio:: 0018
   :page: p. 1

**Archival & Mathematical Commentary**:

* Printed pp. 1–2 (folios ``0018``–``0019``). Marginal citation on p. 1 reads ``6. Arch. æquipond.`` (:cite:`Archimedes, On the Equilibrium of Planes I.6 <archimedes:equipond>`).
* Notice how *Axioma I* (uniqueness) plus *Axioma II* (associativity) turns barycentric weight assignment into a coordinate-free projective calculus: any two distinct groupings of the same vertex masses *must* produce lines passing through the same unique center of gravity.
* Realized symbolically without floating-point arithmetic in :func:`geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.solve_static_barycentrics`.
