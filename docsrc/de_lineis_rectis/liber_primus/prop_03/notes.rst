Proposition III: Third Element (Exterior Ceva & Negative Weights) — Notes
=========================================================================

.. folio:: 0021
   :page: p. 4

**Archival & Mathematical Commentary**:

* Printed pp. 4–5 (folios ``0021``–``0022``), `Tab. 1, Fig. 6`.
* On p. 5 (folio ``0022``), Ceva explicitly distinguishes ordinary positive weights from subtractive weights written *more analytico, seu algebrico* as ``F - A`` (:term:`pondus negativum <pondus-negativum>`).
* In :mod:`geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics`, negative weights yield exact exterior concurrency centers such as the $A$-excenter $J_A = (-a : b : c) = (8, -12)$ on the Heronian $(13, 14, 15)$ triangle.
