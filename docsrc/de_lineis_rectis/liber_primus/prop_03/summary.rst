Proposition III: Third Element (Exterior Ceva & Negative Weights) — Summary
===========================================================================

.. folio:: 0021
   :page: p. 4

*Elementum III* (pp. 4–5, `Tab. 1, Fig. 6`) analyzes a triangle cut by a cevian $EB$ and a transversal $FD$ meeting at $G$. In the landmark **p. 5 Scholium**, Ceva introduces signed/subtractive weights (``F - A``, ``D - C``), laying the conceptual foundation of homogeneous barycentric coordinates 149 years before :person:`Möbius <moebius>` (1827).
