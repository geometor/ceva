**Proposition XVII · Problem X** (pp. 23–25, `Tab. 2, Figs. 18–19` & `Tab. 1, Fig. 7`)

*In the Fourth Element (two crossed diagonals :g:`AF`, :g:`EC` meeting at :g:`D` with a third line :g:`BG` through :g:`D` joining opposite bases :g:`AC`, :g:`EF`), given any three independent segment ratios among the five lines, to determine the remaining two.*

Ceva solves all **10 combinatorial cases** systematically using four vertex weights (`I, H, K, L`) and his addition sign ``✠``.

**Corollary (p. 25 — Projective Equivalence of Elements III and IV)**: Ceva proves a remarkable structural unification: if the upper triangle $\triangle ACD$ of Element IV is folded or projected across the intersection point :g:`D`, Element IV transforms directly into Element III! Thus both configurations share the exact same underlying static equations.
