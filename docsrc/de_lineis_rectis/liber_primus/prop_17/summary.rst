Proposition XVII: Ten Cases of Element IV & Folding Equivalence — Summary
=========================================================================

.. folio:: 0040
   :page: p. 23

*Proposition XVII* (pp. 23–25, `Tab. 2, Figs. 18–19`) resolves all 10 inverse ratio cases of Element IV and proves in its p. 25 *Corollarium* that Elements III and IV are projectively equivalent under reflection/folding through the concurrency point $D$.
