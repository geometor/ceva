Proposition XXIV: Five Concurrent Lines in a Quadrangular Pyramid
=================================================================

.. folio:: 0049
   :page: p. 32

.. picture:: 0107.g29.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula III Fig. 29: Quadrangular pyramid five-line concurrency

**Proposition XXIV · Theorem XIII** (pp. 32–33, `Tab. 3, Fig. 29`)

*In a quadrangular pyramid with apex :g:`E` over quadrilateral base :g:`ABCD`, suspend five weights at the five vertices. Let :g:`S` be the base center of gravity, and let pairs of opposite edges and triangular faces be balanced at their respective centers :g:`L, R, N, I, Q, M, H, P`.*

Then **five** spatial levers—:g:`ES`, :g:`LR`, :g:`NI`, :g:`QM`, and :g:`HP`—all concur simultaneously at the unique five-weight center of gravity :g:`T` inside the pyramid.
