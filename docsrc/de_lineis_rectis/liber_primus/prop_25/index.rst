Proposition XXV: Concurrent Lines in a Pyramidal Frustum
========================================================

.. folio:: 0050
   :page: p. 33

.. picture:: 0107.g30.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula III Fig. 30: Truncated triangular pyramid (frustum) concurrency

**Proposition XXV · Theorem XIV** (pp. 33–34, `Tab. 3, Fig. 30`)

*In a truncated triangular pyramid (frustum) bounded by two triangular bases and three lateral quadrilateral faces, assign six compatible static weights to the six vertices.*

Grouping opposite pairs of faces and edges yields three spatial lines :g:`HL`, :g:`IG`, and :g:`MO` passing simultaneously through the unique six-weight spatial center of gravity (denoted by the cossic supplement glyph ``℞`` on p. 34).
