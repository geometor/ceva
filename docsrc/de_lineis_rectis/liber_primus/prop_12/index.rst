Proposition XII: Ten Combinatorial Cases of Element III
=======================================================

.. folio:: 0035
   :page: p. 18

.. picture:: 0105.g15.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula II Fig. 15: Element III combinatorial cases

**Proposition XII · Problem IX** (`Tab. 2, Fig. 15`)

*Having set forth the figure of the Third Element, out of the five ratios of which we treated in that very element, any three being given, let it be required to make manifest the remaining two.*

Of this problem there are ten cases; for the number five is resolved into ten binary pairs; since multiplying 5 by 4 makes 20, half of which is 10.

Let first be given the three ratios :g:`AF` to :g:`FE` as 3 to 4, :g:`BG` to :g:`GE` of equality, 
and :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as 5 to 4; we must investigate the remaining two, that is, :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` and :g:`AB` to :g:`BC`; and because in this case only :g:`AC` must not be parallel to :g:`FD`, let us draw from point :g:`A` the parallel :g:`AHI`.

Since :g:`BG` to :g:`GH` is compounded of the ratio :g:`BG` to :g:`GE` and :g:`GE` to :g:`GH`, and :g:`GE` to :g:`GH` is as :g:`EF` to :g:`FA`, :g:`BG` to :g:`GH` will be compounded of the ratios :g:`BG` to :g:`GE` and :g:`EF` to :g:`FA`, 
namely of the ratios 4 to 4 and 4 to 3; and therefore :g:`BG` to :g:`GH` will be as 4 to 3; and by dividing, :g:`BH` to :g:`HG` as 1 to 3; but :g:`HG` to :g:`HE` is as 3 to 7; 
therefore by equality :g:`BH` to :g:`HE` will be as 1 to 7. Again, :g:`CD` to :g:`DI` is compounded of the proportions :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` and :g:`DE` to :g:`DI`; but :g:`ED` to :g:`DI` is as :g:`EF` to :g:`FA`; 
therefore :g:`CD` to :g:`DI` is compounded of the ratios :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` and :g:`EF` to :g:`FA`, that is, of the ratios 5 to 4 and 4 to 3; :g:`CD` to :g:`DI` is as 5 to 3; and by dividing, :g:`CI` to :g:`ID` as 2 to 3; 
and :g:`ID` to :g:`IE`, that is, :g:`AF` to :g:`FE`, is as 3 to 7; therefore by equality, as :g:`CI` to :g:`IE`, so 2 to 7; since therefore in the figure of the First Element, whose vertex is :g:`C` and center :g:`H`, 
the two ratios :g:`CI` to :g:`IE` as 2 to 7 and :g:`BH` to :g:`HE` as 1 to 7 are given, the remaining two also become clear, that is, :g:`IH` to :g:`HA` as 7 to 9 and :g:`CB` to :g:`BA` of equality; 
and as :g:`IH` to :g:`HA`, so :g:`DG` to :g:`GF`; therefore we have given the remaining two ratios :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` as 9 to 7, and :g:`CB` to :g:`BA` as 1 to 1, namely of equality.

II. In the lower figure of the same Third Element let there be given three ratios :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as 7 to 4; :g:`CB` to :g:`BA` as 2 to 4; and :g:`DG` to :g:`GF` as 5 to 11; 
it is necessary to lay open the remaining two ratios :g:`AF` to :g:`FE` and :g:`EG` to :g:`GB`.

The proportion :g:`EA` to :g:`EF`, namely of weight :g:`F` to :g:`A`, is compounded of the proportions of weights :g:`F` to :g:`D`, :g:`D` to :g:`C`, and :g:`C` to :g:`A`, 
namely of straight lines :g:`DG` to :g:`GF`, :g:`EC` to :g:`ED`, and :g:`AB` to :g:`BC`; that is, of the ratios 5 to 11 to 4 to 2; wherefore :g:`EA` to :g:`EF` is as 5 to 2, and by dividing, :g:`AF` to :g:`FE` will be as 3 to 2.

Again :g:`EB` to :g:`EG`, that is, weight :g:`G` to :g:`B`, is compounded of the proportions of weights :g:`G` to :g:`D`, :g:`D` to :g:`C`, and :g:`C` to :g:`B`, namely of straight lines :g:`DF` to :g:`FG`, :g:`EC` to :g:`ED`, and :g:`AB` to :g:`AC`; 
that is, of the ratios 16 to 11 to 4 to 6; thus :g:`EB` to :g:`EG` is as 16 to 6, and by dividing :g:`BG` to :g:`GE` will be as 10 to 6.

III. But if the three given ratios be :g:`AF` to :g:`FE` as 7 to 4, :g:`AB` to :g:`BC` as 2 to 4, and :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` as 5 to 11, in the very same way we shall show :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as 3 to 2, and :g:`BG` to :g:`GE` as 5 to 3.

IV. Let there be given three ratios :g:`BG` to :g:`GE` as 8 to 15; :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as 5 to 7; and :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` as 12 to 11; we must lay open the remaining two, that is, :g:`AF` to :g:`FE` and :g:`AB` to :g:`BC` (`Tab. 1, Fig. 6`).

Straight line :g:`AC` to :g:`AB`, namely weight :g:`B` to :g:`C`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`B` to :g:`G`, :g:`G` to :g:`F`, and :g:`F` to :g:`C`, namely of straight lines :g:`EG` to :g:`EB`, :g:`FD` to :g:`FG`, and :g:`CE` to :g:`ED`; 
that is, of the ratios 15 to 23 to 12 to 7; therefore :g:`AC` to :g:`AB` is as 15 to 7, and by dividing, :g:`CB` to :g:`BA` is as 8 to 7. 
Now having given the three proportions :g:`ED` to :g:`DC` as 7 to 5; :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` as 12 to 11; and :g:`CB` to :g:`BA` as 8 to 7; 
in the manner which we used in the first part of the second case of this problem, we shall find :g:`AF` to :g:`FE` to be as 3 to 8.

V. But if the known ratios shall be :g:`BG` to :g:`GE` as 8 to 15; 
:g:`AF` to :g:`FE` as 5 to 7; and :g:`DG` to :g:`GF` as 12 to 11; we shall similarly show :g:`AB` to :g:`BC` to be as 8 to 7; and :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as 3 to 8.

VI. Having the three ratios :g:`EG` to :g:`GB` of equality, :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` as 7 to 3; and :g:`AB` to :g:`BC` as 3 to 2, we must find the remaining two :g:`AF` to :g:`FE` and :g:`CD` to :g:`DE`. 
Compounded is :g:`EC` to :g:`ED`, namely weight :g:`D` to :g:`C`, of the ratios of weights :g:`D` to :g:`G`, :g:`G` to :g:`B`, and :g:`B` to :g:`C`, that is, of the ratios 7 to 10 to 5 to 3; 
therefore :g:`EC` to :g:`ED` is as 7 to 3, and by dividing :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as 4 to 3; thus having given the three ratios :g:`BG` to :g:`GE` of equality, :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as 4 to 3; and :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` as

   :page: p. 20

7 to 3; from the first part of the second case will also be given the ratio :g:`AF` to :g:`FE`, and it will be as 1 to 2.

VII. Let there be given three ratios :g:`AF` to :g:`FE` as 1 to 6; :g:`ED` to :g:`DC` as 5 to 4; 
and :g:`AB` to :g:`BC` as 5 to 6; we must make known the remaining two, namely :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` and :g:`EG` to :g:`GB`.
Compounded is :g:`FG` to :g:`GD`, namely weight :g:`D` to :g:`F`, of the ratios of heavy bodies :g:`D` to :g:`C`, :g:`C` to :g:`A`, and :g:`A` to :g:`F`, 
namely of straight lines :g:`EC` to :g:`ED`, :g:`AB` to :g:`BC`, and :g:`EF` to :g:`EA`; that is, of the ratios 9 to 5 to 6 to 7, wherefore :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` is as 9 to 7.

Then :g:`EB` to :g:`EG`, that is, weight :g:`G` to :g:`B`, is compounded of the ratios of heavy bodies :g:`G` to :g:`D`, :g:`D` to :g:`C`, and :g:`C` to :g:`B`, namely of straight lines :g:`FD` to :g:`FG`, :g:`EC` to :g:`ED`, and :g:`AB` to :g:`AC`; 
that is, of the ratios 16 to 9 to 5 to 11; therefore :g:`EB` to :g:`EG` is as 16 to 11; and by dividing, :g:`BG` to :g:`GE` will be as 5 to 11.

VIII. Having the three ratios :g:`AF` to :g:`FE`, again as 1 to 6, :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` as 9 to 7; :g:`ED` to :g:`DC` as 5 to 4; we must investigate the remaining two, namely :g:`AB` to :g:`BC` and :g:`EG` to :g:`GB`. 
Compounded is :g:`AB` to :g:`BC`, that is, heavy body :g:`C` to :g:`A`, of the ratios of heavy bodies :g:`C` to :g:`D`, :g:`D` to :g:`F`, and :g:`F` to :g:`A`, namely of straight lines :g:`ED` to :g:`EC`, :g:`FG` to :g:`GD`, and :g:`EA` to :g:`FE`; 
that is, of the ratios 5 to 9 to 7 to 6; therefore :g:`AB` to :g:`BC` is as 5 to 6. 
Next, since weight ``F - A`` to weight ``D - C`` is compounded of the ratios of weights ``F - A`` to :g:`F`, :g:`F` to :g:`D`, and :g:`D` to ``D - C``, namely of straight lines :g:`AF` to :g:`AE`, :g:`DG` to :g:`GF`, and :g:`CE` to :g:`DC`; 
that is, of the ratios 1 to 7 to 9 to 4; ``F - A`` to ``D - C`` will be as 1 to 4; and by compounding, :g:`E` to ``D - C`` will be as 5 to 4; 
and because ``D - C`` to :g:`G` is compounded of the ratios of heavy bodies ``D - C`` to :g:`D` and :g:`D` to :g:`G`, that is, of straight lines :g:`DC` to :g:`CE` and :g:`FG` to :g:`FD`; 
that is, of the ratios 4 to 9 to 16; ``D - C`` to :g:`G` will be as 4 to 16; but previously :g:`E` to ``D - C`` was as 5 to 4; 
therefore by equality :g:`E` to :g:`G`, that is, :g:`BG` to :g:`BE`, will be as 5 to 16; and by converting, and thence dividing, :g:`EG` to :g:`GB` will be as 11 to 5, which &c.

IX. Let there be proposed three ratios :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as 4 to 5; :g:`EG` to :g:`GB` as 11 to 5; and :g:`AB` to :g:`BC` as 5 to 6; and let it be required to search out the remaining two. 
Compounded is :g:`AF` to :g:`FE`, namely weight ``F - A`` to :g:`A`, of the ratios of heavy bodies ``F - A`` to :g:`E`, :g:`E` to :g:`B`, and :g:`B` to :g:`A`, 
and because :g:`E` to ``D - C`` is constituted of the ratios of heavy bodies :g:`E` to :g:`B`, :g:`B` to :g:`C`, and :g:`C` to ``D - C``, namely of straight lines :g:`BG` to :g:`GE`, :g:`AC` to :g:`AB`, and :g:`ED` to :g:`DC`; 
that is, of the ratios of the numbers 5 to 11 to 5 to 4; weight :g:`E` to ``D - C`` will be as 5 to 4; but by conversion of ratio, and thence

.. folio:: 0038
   :page: p. 21

by converting, ``F - A`` to :g:`E` will be as 1 to 5; but the ratios of weights :g:`E` to :g:`B` and :g:`B` to :g:`A`, namely of straight lines :g:`BG` to :g:`GE` and :g:`AC` to :g:`AB`, are in sequence as 5 to 11 to 6; 
therefore by equality :g:`AF` to :g:`FE` will be as 1 to 6.

Similarly, because :g:`FD` to :g:`GF`, that is, heavy body :g:`G` to :g:`D`, is compounded of the ratios of heavy bodies :g:`G` to :g:`B`, :g:`B` to :g:`C`, and :g:`C` to :g:`D`, 
namely of straight lines :g:`EB` to :g:`EG`, :g:`AC` to :g:`AB`, :g:`ED` to :g:`EC`; or of the ratios 16 to 11 to 5 to 9; :g:`FD` to :g:`GF` will be as 16 to 9; and by dividing, :g:`DG` to :g:`GF` will be as 7 to 9.

X. Finally, if there be given three ratios :g:`AF` to :g:`FE` as 4 to 5; :g:`EG` to :g:`GB` as 11 to 5; and :g:`CB` to :g:`BA` as 5 to 6; 
by the same reasoning we shall make manifest :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as 1 to 6; and :g:`FG` to :g:`GD` as 7 to 9, which &c.
