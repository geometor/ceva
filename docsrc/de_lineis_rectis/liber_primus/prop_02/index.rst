Proposition II: Second Element (Interior Ceva Concurrency)
==========================================================

.. folio:: 0020
   :page: p. 3

.. picture:: 0103.g5.svg
   :alt: Calibrated interactive SVG of Ceva Book I Prop. II Elementum II (Tab. I Fig. 5)

.. picture:: 0103.g5.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula I Fig. 5: Elementum II Interior Ceva Theorem

**Proposition II · Problem II · Element II**

*Let there be a triangle* :g:`EAC <AEC>`, *and from its angles let lines* :g:`EF <EB>`, :g:`AF <AK>`, :g:`CF <CD>` *be drawn to the same point* :g:`F` *within the triangle, which being produced from* :g:`F` *meet the sides in order at the points* :g:`B`, :g:`K`, :g:`D`;
*it is proposed to suspend heavy bodies* :g:`I`, :g:`G`, :g:`H` *from the aforesaid angles* :g:`E`, :g:`A`, :g:`C` *so that weight* :g:`G` *to* :g:`I` *is as* $ED$ *to* $DA$; *the same* :g:`G` *to* :g:`H` *as* $CB$ *to* $BA$; :g:`H` *to* :g:`I` *as* $EK$ *to* $KC$;
*weight* :g:`I` *to the two* :g:`G`, :g:`H` *as* $BF$ *to* $FE$; *weight* :g:`H` *to the two* :g:`I`, :g:`G` *as* $DF$ *to* $FC$; *and finally, as the single* :g:`G` *to the two* :g:`I`, :g:`H`, *so* $KF$ *to* $FA$.

(`Tab. 1, Fig. 5`) Let it be made as $CB$ to $BA$, so weight :g:`G` to :g:`H`, and as $ED$ to $DA$, so the same weight :g:`G` to :g:`I`.

Since therefore the figure $EDBACF$ is that of the First Element, and $CB$ is to $BA$ as :g:`G` to :g:`H`, and
the straight line $ED$ to $DA$ as :g:`G` to :g:`I`, also $DF$ to $FC$ will be as weight :g:`H` to the two weights :g:`I`, :g:`G`, and likewise $BF$ to $FE$ as weight :g:`I` to the two :g:`G`, :g:`H`.

(*Corol. Prop. 1, Lem. 1*) Moreover, because :g:`F` is the center of gravity in which is the weight of the heavy bodies :g:`I`, :g:`G`, :g:`H`, the produced line :g:`AF <AK>` will pass through :g:`K`, the center of the remaining weight :g:`I`, :g:`H`,

.. folio:: 0021
   :page: p. 4

(*Lem. 2*) therefore as weight :g:`I` to :g:`H`, so $CK$ to $KE$.

Again, because :g:`AK` is a certain balance-beam at whose extremities :g:`A`, :g:`K` are the weights :g:`G` and :g:`I`, :g:`H`, (*Ax. 2, Lem. 2*)
the center of gravity of the aforesaid weights :g:`I`, :g:`G`, :g:`H` will lie on the same balance-beam :g:`AK`; and since this center is unique and lies on both balance-beams :g:`DC <CD>` and :g:`EB`, as we showed above, it will necessarily be at :g:`F`;
therefore it will be as $KF$ to $FA$, so weight :g:`G` to the two :g:`I`, :g:`H` together, which was to be done.

**Scholium.** *When this same figure is set forth again, we shall signify the aggregate of weights* :g:`I`, :g:`H` *by the letter* :g:`K`, *for in* :g:`K` *is the center of gravity, and therefore the weight of the heavy bodies* :g:`I`, :g:`H`.
