Proposition II: Second Element (Interior Ceva Concurrency)
==========================================================

.. folio:: 0020
   :page: p. 3

.. picture:: 0103.g5.svg
   :alt: Calibrated interactive SVG of Ceva Book I Prop. II Elementum II (Tab. I Fig. 5)

.. picture:: 0103.g5.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula I Fig. 5: Elementum II Interior Ceva Theorem

**Proposition II · Problem II · Element II (Ceva's Theorem)** (`Tab. 1, Fig. 5`)

*In triangle :g:`AEC`, let straight lines :g:`EB` and :g:`CD` intersect inside the triangle at :g:`F`, and let a third straight line :g:`AK` be drawn from vertex :g:`A` through :g:`F` to meet base :g:`EC` at :g:`K`. To determine the division of :g:`EC` and the ratios of all six boundary segments.*

Suspend weights :g:`G` at :g:`A`, :g:`I` at :g:`E`, and :g:`H` at :g:`C` just as in Element I so that :g:`D` balances :g:`G` and :g:`I`, and :g:`B` balances :g:`G` and :g:`H`. Then the unique center of gravity of the three-weight system lies at :g:`F`.

Now group the base weights :g:`I` and :g:`H` first at their lever fulcrum on :g:`EC`. By *Axiom I–II*, the straight line joining vertex :g:`A` to that fulcrum must pass through the global center of gravity :g:`F`—hence that fulcrum is precisely :g:`K`! Consequently:

$$\overline{EK} : \overline{KC} = H : I, \qquad \overline{AF} : \overline{FK} = (I + H) : G.$$

Multiplying the three cyclic perimeter ratios eliminates the weights $G, H, I$ identically, giving **Ceva's Theorem**:

$$\frac{\overline{AD}}{\overline{DE}} \cdot \frac{\overline{EK}}{\overline{KC}} \cdot \frac{\overline{CB}}{\overline{BA}} = \frac{I}{G} \cdot \frac{H}{I} \cdot \frac{G}{H} = 1.$$

**Scholium**: Notice that knowledge of any two perimeter ratios determines the third immediately, as well as the internal division of all three concurrent :term:`cevians <ceviana>`.
