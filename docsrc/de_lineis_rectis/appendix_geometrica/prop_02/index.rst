Proposition II: Rectilinear Quadrature of Circular Lunes
========================================================

   :page: p. 63

   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula VIII Fig. 69: Circular lune GK quadrature

.. picture:: 0117.g70.png

**Proposition II · Theorem II** (`Tab. 8, Figs. 69–70`)

*If from the center of a circle a perpendicular be raised to its diameter, and from that point of it which is also on the circumference lines be drawn to the ends of the same diameter, and in the opposite semicircle an arc be made whose radii are the said lines:*
 *Moreover, any other point being taken, not the center of the circle, on the same perpendicular, let a straight line be drawn from it to one end of the diameter, with which as radius toward the parts of the said opposite semicircle let a circular line be drawn,*
 *or be so cut that it has to the semicircumference of the same circle the same ratio as the square of the radius of the supposed circle to that of the straight line which we drew from the assumed point;*
 *and likewise from the same point let a line be drawn to the other extremity of the arc made: by these straight lines a certain rectilinear space will be produced, and together with those two arcs another mixed curvilinear space, which will be equal to the former.*

Let there be circle :g:`ABC`, whose diameter is :g:`AC` and its center :g:`D`, from which let a line be erected at right angles above :g:`AC`; then from point :g:`B`, which ought to lie on the intersection of the circumference, let :g:`BA`, :g:`BC` be drawn,
and with center :g:`B` and radius either of :g:`BA`, :g:`BC` let arc :g:`CGA` be described; and any other point :g:`E` being taken, let :g:`EA` be joined, at whose radius, with center made at :g:`E`, let us understand arc :g:`AKCH` to be described,
which has to the semicircumference of the same circle the same ratio as the square on :g:`DA` to the square on :g:`EA`, and finally :g:`EH` being joined, I say that the rectilinear space :g:`ABFE` is equal to either space, namely to lune :g:`GK` and to part :g:`FCH` of the circle.
But first it must be shown that the spaces themselves are formed. Let :g:`CE` be joined; and because we took points :g:`B` and :g:`E` on the perpendicular :g:`DBE` erected from the center to diameter :g:`AC`,
it is manifest that the said arcs :g:`AGC`, :g:`AKCH` pass through the two ends :g:`C` and :g:`A` of the said diameter; next, because triangle :g:`DEA` is right-angled at :g:`D`, and in the first case side :g:`DA` is greater than side :g:`DE`, while in the second less than it,
the square on :g:`EA` will have to the square on :g:`AD` in the first case a lesser ratio, but in the second a greater ratio, than angle :g:`BDA` to angle :g:`DEA`, namely than two right angles to

angle :g:`CEA`;
nay, than the semicircumference of the circle of radius :g:`EA` to arc :g:`CKA` of the same circle; yet as the same square :g:`AE` is to :g:`AD` itself, so is the same semicircumference of the circle of radius :g:`EA` to arc :g:`HKA` of the same circle;
therefore in the first case the same semicircumference will have to arc :g:`HKA` a lesser ratio, but in the second a greater ratio, than to arc :g:`CKA`; and thus in the first case arc :g:`HKA` will be greater, but in the second less than :g:`CKA` itself,
and it is therefore established that in the former point :g:`H` falls outside, and in the latter inside circle :g:`ABC`; which being so, line :g:`CB` will be cut by :g:`HFE` itself, and thus the proposed spaces will be formed. With this premised, let us now show what was proposed.


Because straight line :g:`BA` is double in square of :g:`DA` itself, quadrant :g:`BCGA` will be double quadrant :g:`BDA`; yet semicircle :g:`CBA` is also double the same quadrant :g:`BDA`; therefore quadrant :g:`BCGA` is equal to semicircle :g:`CBA`;
yet because square :g:`DA` to square :g:`AE`, namely semicircle :g:`CBA` to the semicircle of radius :g:`AE`, has the same ratio as arc :g:`HKA` to the semicircumference of the circle of the same arc, namely as sector :g:`EHKA` to the semicircle of the same sector,
semicircle :g:`CBA`, namely quadrant :g:`BCGA`, will be equal to sector :g:`EHKA`; and if the common space be taken away, :g:`FBAE` itself, which is rectilinear, will remain equal to both lune :g:`GK` and space :g:`FHC`, Q.E.D.
