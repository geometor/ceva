Proposition XV: Finite Volume of an Infinite Hyperbolic Solid — Summary
=======================================================================

.. folio:: 0096
   :page: p. 79

*Appendix Prop. XV* (pp. 79–80, `Tab. 10, Fig. 82`) proves—via :person:`Torricelli's <torricelli>` cylindrical-shell indivisibles (:cite:`De sphaera I.5 <torricelli:1644:opera>`)—that an infinitely wide hyperbolic solid of revolution has an exact **finite volume** equal to a right cylinder.
