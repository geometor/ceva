Proposition XV: Finite Volume of an Infinite Hyperbolic Solid — Notes
=====================================================================

.. folio:: 0096
   :page: p. 79

**Archival Notes**:

* Printed pp. 79–80 (folios ``0096``–``0097``), `Tab. 10, Fig. 82`.
* One of the gems of seventeenth-century Italian calculus before Newton and Leibniz: Ceva extends Torricelli's celebrated finite-volume infinite trumpet (*Solidum Hyperbolicum Acutum*, 1644) to the symmetric four-branch hyperbolic solid.
* **Cylindrical-Shell Invariance**: Because the equilateral hyperbola $xy = k$ keeps the lateral surface area $2\pi x \cdot (2y) = 4\pi k$ constant for every coaxial cylindrical shell, each shell equals a fixed circle of radius :g:`HN` ($= \sqrt{2k}$), yielding a finite right cylinder.
