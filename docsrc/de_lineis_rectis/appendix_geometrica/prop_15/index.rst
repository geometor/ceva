Proposition XV: Finite Volume of an Infinite Hyperbolic Solid
=============================================================

.. folio:: 0096
   :page: p. 79

.. picture:: 0121.g82.svg
   :alt: Calibrated interactive SVG of Ceva's Infinite Hyperbolic Solid of Finite Volume (Tab. X Fig. 82)

.. picture:: 0121.g82.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula X Fig. 82: Infinitely extended hyperbolic solid equal to finite cylinder

**Proposition XV · Theorem XV**

*An infinitely wide hyperbolic round solid is equal to the cylinder whose opposite bases are common, together with another right cylinder whose altitude is the semi-axis of the solid, while the radius of its base is a line equal to the mean proportional between the whole axis and its half of the opposite sections which are called conjugate, and those are the ones which are seen in the same plane passed through the axis of the solid.*

Let there be hyperbola :g:`EB`, and let its asymptotes :g:`ED`, :g:`DC` contain a right angle :g:`EDC` (margin: *Tab. 10, Fig. 82*), and having taken any point :g:`B` on hyperbola :g:`EB`, from it let :g:`BC` be drawn parallel to :g:`DE`;
and opposite to figure :g:`EBCDE` of infinite length toward :g:`E` let there be conceived :g:`EDFGE` similarly equal to the aforesaid, so that line :g:`FDC` is a single straight line and the figure composed of both is :g:`EBCFGE` endlessly long;
then let this composite figure be revolved about axis :g:`FC` so that an infinitely wide round solid is formed, and let its section through the axis be figure :g:`EBIKLGE`. Now because to hyperbolas :g:`GE`, :g:`EB`, :g:`IK`, :g:`LK` the asymptotes :g:`FDC`, :g:`KDE` are common,
the said four hyperbolas so constituted will be opposite sections, which are called conjugate, and their two conjugate axes will be equal to each other. Therefore let one of them be :g:`MDH`, and from point :g:`H` having drawn line :g:`HSN` parallel to asymptote :g:`EK`,
let :g:`NM` be joined, which will be parallel to straight line :g:`DS`, namely to the other asymptote :g:`FC` (for :g:`MH` is to :g:`HD` as :g:`NH` is to :g:`HS`), and since angle :g:`EDC` of rectangle :g:`HSD` is bisected by its diagonal :g:`DH`, rectangle :g:`HSD` will be a square,
which since it is about diagonal :g:`MH` of the other rectangle :g:`HNM`, this too will be a square; and its side :g:`HN` will be a mean proportional between the whole axis :g:`MH` and its half :g:`HD`.
I say that the entire solid :g:`EFKC` infinitely extended on the sides :g:`EK` is equal to a right cylinder whose base is equal to the circle about radius :g:`HN`, and whose axis is

straight line :g:`DC`, together with cylinder :g:`GI`, whose axis is :g:`FC`. Let there be conceived any number of cylindrical surfaces :g:`BGLI`, :g:`HM`, :g:`QO`, about the same axis :g:`FC` and within the infinitely extended solid :g:`EFKC`;
and because rectangles :g:`DCI`, :g:`DRN`, :g:`D✠P` are equal to one another (margin: *Conics II.22*), their quadruples too, namely rectangles :g:`LIB`, :g:`MNH`, :g:`OPQ`, will be equal to one another. Now because cylindrical surface :g:`GBIL` is to the circle whose radius is :g:`XZ`
as rectangle :g:`LIB` is to square :g:`XZ` (margin: *Evangelista Torricelli, De sphaera I.5, etc.*), namely to rectangular square :g:`MNH`, which spaces are equal, the said cylindrical surface :g:`GLIB` will be equal to the circle whose radius is :g:`XZ`;
by the same reasoning cylindrical surface :g:`MH` will be equal to the circle whose radius is :g:`NH`; and likewise surface :g:`QO` will be equal to the circle whose radius is :g:`TV`; and this always holds wherever points :g:`INP` be taken.
Therefore, since all cylindrical surfaces :g:`GLIB`, :g:`HM`, :g:`QO`, etc. are equal to all circles whose radii are :g:`XZ`, :g:`NH`, :g:`TV`, etc., it is evident that the entire infinitely wide round solid :g:`EFKC` is equal to a right cylinder whose altitude is :g:`DC`, namely the semi-axis of the solid,
and whose base is the circle whose radius :g:`SR`, or :g:`NM`, is a mean proportional between the whole axis :g:`HM` and its half :g:`MD`, etc., together with cylinder :g:`GLIB`, about axis :g:`FC`, which was to be demonstrated.
