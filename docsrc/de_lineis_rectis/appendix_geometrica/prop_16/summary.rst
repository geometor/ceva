Proposition XVI: Center of Gravity of an Infinite Hyperbolic Plane — Summary
============================================================================

.. folio:: 0097
   :page: p. 80

*Appendix Prop. XVI* (pp. 80–81, `Tab. 10, Fig. 83`) combines Prop. XV with :person:`Torricelli's <torricelli>` Pappus-Guldin moment lemma (*pulcro lem. 31*) to pinpoint the finite center of gravity :math:`D` of an infinitely long hyperbolic plane region.
