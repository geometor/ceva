Proposition XVI: Center of Gravity of an Infinite Hyperbolic Plane
==================================================================

.. folio:: 0097
   :page: p. 80

.. picture:: 0121.g83.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula X Fig. 83: Center of gravity D of infinitely long hyperbolic plane BECMA

**Proposition XVI · Theorem XVI** (pp. 80–81, `Tab. 10, Fig. 83`)

*Even though the hyperbolic half-section :g:`BECMA` extends to **infinite length** toward asymptote :g:`C` (with rectangle :g:`AF` removed), Ceva proves—invoking :person:`Torricelli's <torricelli>` "beautiful Lemma 31" (*ex pulcro lemm. 31 Torricellij in dimens. parabola*) and the finite revolution volume of Proposition XV—that the static moment of the infinite plane balances the finite rectangle :g:`BE` around axis :g:`AE`, isolating its exact finite **center of gravity** at the intersection :g:`D` of :g:`AE` and symmetry diameter :g:`GC`!*
