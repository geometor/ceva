Proposition VIII: Ratio of Oblique Cylindrical Hooves
=====================================================

.. folio:: 0087
   :page: p. 70

.. picture:: 0119.g75.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula IX Fig. 75: Three oblique planes cutting a semicylinder along ED

**Proposition VIII · Theorem VIII** (pp. 70–71, `Tab. 9, Fig. 75`)

*If three planes pass through the same straight line :g:`ED` lying in the axial parallelogram of a semicylinder, cutting the semicylinder in three semi-ellipses or semicircles :g:`EBD`, :g:`EMD`, :g:`EGD`, then their three vertices :g:`B`, :g:`M`, :g:`G` lie along a single cylinder generator, and the volumes of the enclosed cylindrical hooves stand directly in the linear ratio of the intercepted generator segments:*

$$\text{Portion}(EBD, EGD) : \text{Portion}(EBD, EMD) = \overline{BG} : \overline{BM}.$$
