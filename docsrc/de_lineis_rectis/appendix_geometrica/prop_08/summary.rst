Proposition VIII: Ratio of Oblique Cylindrical Hooves — Summary
===============================================================

.. folio:: 0087
   :page: p. 70

*Appendix Prop. VIII* (pp. 70–71, `Tab. 9, Fig. 75`) applies Prop. VII to three coaxial oblique planes cutting a semicylinder, proving that the cylindrical hoof volumes scale linearly with their vertex spacing $GB : BM$.
