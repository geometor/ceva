Proposition III: Twelfth-Semicircle Arcs from Squared Side Ratios
=================================================================

.. folio:: 0081
   :page: p. 64

**Proposition III · Theorem III** (pp. 64–65, `Tab. 8, Figs. 69–70`)

*When the squared segments satisfy $\overline{DA}^2 : \overline{AE}^2 = 3 : 4$ (or $1 : 4$), the subtended circular arc :g:`CH` equals exactly one-twelfth ($15^\circ$) of the semicircle—linking rational quadrance ratios directly to constructible sub-multiples of the circle.*
