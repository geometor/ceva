Ten Folding Copperplate Plates (Tabulae I–X, Figs. 1–85) — Summary
==================================================================

.. folio:: 0103
   :page: Tabula I (Figs. 1–12)

Complete archival gallery and concordance of all **10 folding copperplate plates** (`Tabulae I–X`, folios ``0103``–``0122``) housing all **85 geometric figures** of Ceva (1678), together with the volume's trailing endpapers and back cover (``0123``–``0126``).
