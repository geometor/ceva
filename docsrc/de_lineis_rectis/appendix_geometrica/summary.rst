Appendix Geometrica — Summary
=============================

.. folio:: 0078
   :page: p. 61

The *Appendix Geometrica* and backmatter (folios ``0078``–``0126``) comprise 19 atomic leaves:

* **Circle Quadratures (`Props. I–VI`, pp. 61–67)**: Ptolemaic right-triangle bounds, circular lunes of Hippocrates, and semihexagon quadrature (`Tab. 8, Figs. 68–73`).
* **Cavalieri Volumes (`Props. VII–XII`, pp. 67–77)**: Cylindrical hoof (:math:`2/3` prism), oblique hooves, hyperbolic annulus (:math:`2/3` cylinder), and hyperbolic conoids (`Tab. 9–10, Figs. 74–79`).
* **Centroids & Infinite Solids (`Props. XIII–XVI`, pp. 77–81)**: Hemisphere :math:`5:3`, paraboloid :math:`2:1`, finite-volume infinite hyperbolic solid, and infinite-plane centroid (`Tab. 10, Figs. 80–83`).
* **Critique, Apparatus & Plates (`pp. 81–83` + backmatter)**: Sphere-surface indivisibles paradox (`Fig. 84`), Errata & Printer's Note on ``✠`` (``0100``), Monza Colophon (``0101``–``0102``), and all ten folding copperplates (`Tabulae I–X`, ``0103``–``0126``).
