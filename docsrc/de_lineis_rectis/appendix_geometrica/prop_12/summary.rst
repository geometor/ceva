Proposition XII: Hyperbolic Conoid Inscribed in a Conical Frustum — Summary
===========================================================================

.. folio:: 0093
   :page: p. 76

*Appendix Prop. XII* (pp. 76–77, `Tab. 10, Fig. 79`) expresses the volume of a hyperbolic conoid circumscribed by a tangent conical frustum simply as :math:`\text{Frustum} - \text{Inverted Cone}`.
