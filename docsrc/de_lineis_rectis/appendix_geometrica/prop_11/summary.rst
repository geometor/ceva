Proposition XI: Volume of a Hyperbolic Conoid — Summary
=======================================================

.. folio:: 0092
   :page: p. 75

*Appendix Prop. XI* (pp. 75–76, `Tab. 10, Fig. 78`) computes the exact volume of a **hyperbolic conoid** as its asymptotic cone minus an apical cone and a cylinder, using :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` (*Conics* II.10).
