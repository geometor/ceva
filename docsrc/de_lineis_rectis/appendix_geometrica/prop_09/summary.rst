Proposition IX: General Oblique Cylindrical Portions via Prisms and Pyramid — Summary
=====================================================================================

.. folio:: 0088
   :page: p. 71

*Appendix Prop. IX* (pp. 71–73, `Tab. 9, Fig. 76`) generalizes the cylindrical hoof quadrature to off-center oblique sections, reducing the sum of two curved cylindrical portions to an explicit polyhedron of prisms and pyramids.
