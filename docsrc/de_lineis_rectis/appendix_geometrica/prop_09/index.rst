Proposition IX: General Oblique Cylindrical Portions via Prisms and Pyramid
===========================================================================

.. folio:: 0088
   :page: p. 71

.. picture:: 0119.g76.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula IX Fig. 76: General oblique cylindrical sections above center V

**Proposition IX · Theorem IX** (pp. 71–73, `Tab. 9, Fig. 76`)

*When two oblique planes divide a cylinder along a chord :g:`AD` offset above the center :g:`V` (rather than passing through the center), Ceva proves by triangle-slice Cavalieri decomposition—explicitly writing algebraic sums and differences with ``✠`` and ``-`` on p. 72—that the two complementary cylindrical portions :g:`L98E5G` and :g:`L7E5G` equal an explicit rectilinear solid composed of triangular prisms minus four times pyramid :g:`MKXV`.*
