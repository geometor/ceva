**Proposition IX · Theorem IX** (pp. 71–73, `Tab. 9, Fig. 76`)

*It is to be shown in this following figure that the prism whose opposite bases are triangles* :g:`AI3`, :g:`DF6`, *together with that prism whose opposite bases are triangles* :g:`LYI`, :g:`EFZ`, *minus* 
*four times the pyramid whose base is* :g:`KXM` *and vertex* :g:`V`, *with that prism likewise removed whose base is triangle* :g:`AL9` *and one side is straight line* :g:`AD`, *is equal to the two portions of the cylinder* :g:`L98ESG` *and* :g:`L7ESG`.

Let a cylinder be divided by two planes so that sections :g:`A98D`, :g:`AGD` are segments of ellipses or circles (margin: *Tab. 9, Fig. 76*), which cut each other along straight line :g:`AD` above center :g:`V` of circle or ellipse :g:`AGD`; 
and having bisected :g:`AD` at :g:`T`, let :g:`TV` be joined, which we extend on both sides so that it meets circle or ellipse :g:`AGD` at points :g:`C`, :g:`G`; thence having taken :g:`GS` equal to :g:`CT`, through points :g:`V`, :g:`S`, and :g:`G` let parallels to straight line :g:`AD` be drawn, 
such as :g:`VM℞`, :g:`LSE`, and :g:`HG`, which produced on both sides meet the two joined lines :g:`AL`, :g:`DE` extended at points :g:`I`, and :g:`F`; then draw :g:`G8` a side of the cylinder, and from points :g:`I`, :g:`F` lines :g:`I3`, :g:`F6` parallel to the same side :g:`G8`, meeting 
the line passing through :g:`8` parallel to :g:`IGF` at points :g:`3`, :g:`6`: moreover having joined lines :g:`3A`, :g:`6D`, :g:`8T`, let us extend through lines :g:`3I`, :g:`IA` a plane cutting the portion of the cylinder intercepted between the proposed segments, and let it make on its convex surface, lying before our eyes, 
line :g:`9L`. And since :g:`3I` is parallel to side :g:`G8` of the cylinder, indeed to the axis of the same cylinder, and line :g:`ILA` is parallel to :g:`GC`, since

plane :g:`C8G` extended through the axis is parallel to the plane through :g:`3I`, :g:`IA`; straight line :g:`9L` will be a side of the cylinder, and therefore parallel to line :g:`G8`; thence having drawn from center :g:`V` line :g:`VK`, namely the diagonal of the parallelogram contained by lines :g:`℞V`, :g:`VG` 
in angle :g:`℞VG`, let line :g:`KX` be drawn in the plane of triangle :g:`AI3` parallel to :g:`3I`, and :g:`MX`, :g:`LY` parallel to the same :g:`3A`; and let :g:`MX`, :g:`KX` meet at point :g:`X`; and let line :g:`Y7Z` be parallel to :g:`IF`. These being supposed, let us conceive a prism 
whose opposite bases are triangles :g:`AI3`, :g:`DF6`; and likewise another having opposite bases triangles :g:`LYI`, :g:`EFZ`; thence a pyramid whose base is triangle :g:`MKX` and vertex point :g:`V`. I say that the aforesaid two prisms, minus the composite of four times the said pyramid and the prism 
whose base is triangle :g:`AL9` and altitude the same as the aforesaid two prisms have, is equal to the two portions of the cylinder :g:`L98ESG`, :g:`L7ESG`.

Since square :g:`IA` is equal to squares :g:`AL`, :g:`LI` together with twice rectangle :g:`ALI`, square :g:`IA` will be equal to square :g:`AL` together with twice rectangle :g:`AIL`, minus square :g:`IL`; but twice square :g:`MK` is equal to twice rectangle :g:`AIL`; 
therefore square :g:`IA` will be equal to square :g:`AL` together with two squares :g:`MK` - :g:`LI`; on that account triangle :g:`A3I` of the larger prism will be equal to triangle :g:`A9L` of the cylinder portion, together with twice triangle :g:`MKX` of the pyramid, minus triangle :g:`LYI` 
of the smaller prism (for these triangles are mutually similar and similarly described upon the sides of the said squares). Moreover, because twice rectangle :g:`BHP`, that is twice square :g:`ON`, surpasses twice rectangle :g:`RPH` by twice rectangle :g:`QHP` and square :g:`PH` 
likewise taken twice, and this excess is equal to square :g:`QH` - :g:`QP` ✠ :g:`PH`; twice square :g:`NO` will be greater than twice rectangle :g:`RPH` by square :g:`QH` - :g:`QP` ✠ :g:`PH`, or, which is the same, 
twice square :g:`NO` - :g:`QH` ✠ :g:`QP` - :g:`PH` will be equal to twice rectangle :g:`RPH`; and having added in common squares :g:`RP`, :g:`PH`, square :g:`RH` will be equal to twice square :g:`NO` together with squares :g:`QP`, :g:`RP` - :g:`QH`: 
for adding square :g:`PH` to - :g:`PH` leaves nothing. Wherefore also triangle :g:`R4H` of the larger prism will be equal to the triangles similar to themselves and similarly described upon the sides of the said squares as bases, namely to twice the triangle 
whose base is :g:`NO` of the aforesaid pyramid, together with that whose base is :g:`RP` of the cylinder portion inscribed in the larger prism, at the same time

with that triangle constructed on base :g:`QP` of the cylinder portion inscribed in the smaller prism, minus triangle :g:`QH2` of the same smaller prism.

Finally, because square :g:`TG` is equal to itself together with square :g:`SG` minus the same, triangle :g:`TG8` of the larger prism 
will be equal to the same triangle :g:`TG8` of the larger cylinder portion together with triangle :g:`SG7` of the smaller cylinder portion, minus the same triangle :g:`SG7`, inasmuch as it belongs to the smaller prism; since therefore all triangles :g:`AI3`, :g:`RH4`, :g:`TG8`, etc. 
of the larger half-prism are equal to twice all the triangles of pyramid :g:`MXKV`, namely :g:`MKX` and those whose bases are :g:`NO`, etc., together with all the triangles, namely :g:`AL9`, and those whose bases are :g:`RP`, :g:`TG`, etc. of the larger semiportion 
of the cylinder inscribed in the aforesaid larger half-prism, together with all the triangles, namely :g:`SG7`, and those whose bases are :g:`QP`, etc. of the smaller semiportion of the cylinder inscribed in the smaller half-prism :g:`LYG`, yet minus all the triangles :g:`LIY`, :g:`QH2`, :g:`SG7`, etc. 
of the said smaller half-prism; the half-prism whose opposite bases are triangles :g:`AI3`, :g:`TG8` will be equal to twice the pyramid whose base is triangle :g:`MKX` and vertex :g:`V`, together with the two semiportions of the cylinder (of which the larger is that which lies 
between the two triangles :g:`AL9`, :g:`TG8`; while the other is contained by the smaller half-prism :g:`TLG`), minus that same half-prism. And if this smaller half-prism be added in common, while that whose base is triangle :g:`AL9` in the same altitude in which the two 
said half-prisms stand be taken away, there will remain the prism whose base is trapezium :g:`L93I` at the same aforesaid altitude, which together with the smaller half-prism will be equal to twice the said pyramid together with the two semiportions of the cylinder, of which 
the larger lies inscribed beneath the said quadrangular half-prism, and the other beneath the smaller; and their doubles, that is prism :g:`93IL6` together with prism :g:`YLIZ`, will be equal to four times the pyramid whose base is triangle :g:`MKX` and vertex :g:`V`, 
together with the two portions of the cylinder :g:`L98ESG`, :g:`L7ESG`; if therefore four times the said pyramid be taken away in common, the said two portions of the cylinder will be equal to the rectilinear solid which remains when the aforesaid quadruple is removed, Q.E.D.
