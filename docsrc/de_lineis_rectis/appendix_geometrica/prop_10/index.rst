Proposition X: Hyperbolic Annulus as Two-Thirds of Asymptotic Cylinder
======================================================================

.. folio:: 0091
   :page: p. 74

.. picture:: 0119.g77.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula IX Fig. 77: Hyperbolic ring/annulus around axis MN

**Proposition X · Theorem X** (pp. 74–75, `Tab. 9, Fig. 77`)

*A **hyperbolic ring (annulus)** generated by revolving two opposite hyperbola branches :g:`CBA` and :g:`EFD` around their conjugate axis :g:`MN` is **two-thirds** (*subsesquialter*, $2 : 3$) of the cylinder :g:`LH` having the same height and base circle generated by the asymptotes :g:`LGH`, :g:`KGS`!*

Citing :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` (*Conics* II.11), every horizontal circular cross-section of the hyperbolic drum equals the sum of the inner cylinder circle and the asymptotic cone circle. Integrating circular indivisibles subtracts the double cone ($\frac{1}{3}$ of cylinder $LH$), leaving the outer hyperbolic ring equal to exactly $\frac{2}{3}$ of cylinder $LH$!
