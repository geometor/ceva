Prolegomena: Title Page, Dedication & Preface
=============================================

.. folio:: 0010
   :page: Title Page

The preliminary leaves of :person:`Giovanni Ceva's <ceva>` *De Lineis Rectis Se Invicem Secantibus Statica Constructio* (:cite:`Milan: Ludovico Monza, 1678 <ceva:1678:dlr>`, folios ``0001``–``0017``) frame the entire treatise: the BNCF Florence Magliabechiano binding and title page dedicated to :person:`Duke Ferdinando Carlo Gonzaga <gonzaga>`, the dedicatory epistle signed by Ceva, and the *Prooemium* narrating how the substitution of Archimedean weights for straight lines (*substitutisque linearum vice ponderibus*) gave birth to his :term:`static construction <statica-constructio>`.
