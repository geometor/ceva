Title Page & BNCF Witness Binding — Notes
=========================================

.. folio:: 0010
   :page: Title Page

**Archival Witness**: Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Firenze, Fondo Magliabechiano, ``CFMAGL. 1.7.137`` (Internet Archive ``ita-bnc-mag-00001346-001``).

* Scans ``0001``–``0006`` document the exterior binding and edges; ``0007``–``0009`` record archival flyleaves bearing the Magliabechiano shelfmark and old manuscript index ``XI CEV.``.
* Scan ``0010`` displays the 1678 title page, floral rose vignette, and circular BNCF lily stamp; scan ``0011`` is the blank verso.
* The exact folio-to-page correspondence throughout the printed text is ``printed_page = folio_number - 17`` from folio ``0018`` (p. 1) continuously through folio ``0100`` (p. 83).
