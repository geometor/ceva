Title Page & BNCF Witness Binding — Summary
===========================================

.. folio:: 0010
   :page: Title Page

The title page of the 1678 Milan *editio princeps* announces :person:`Giovanni Ceva's <ceva>` programmatic title: *De Lineis Rectis Se Invicem Secantibus Statica Constructio*—a static construction of mutually intersecting straight lines dedicated to :person:`Duke Ferdinando Carlo Gonzaga <gonzaga>` of Mantua and printed by :person:`Ludovico Monza <monza>`.
