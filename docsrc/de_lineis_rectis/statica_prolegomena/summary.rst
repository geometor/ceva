Prolegomena — Summary
=====================

.. folio:: 0010
   :page: Title Page

The *Statica Prolegomena* (unnumbered frontmatter, scans ``0001``–``0017``) comprise three atomic sections:

1. :ref:`Title Page & BNCF Witness Binding <statica-prolegomena-frontispiece>` (``0001``–``0011``): Full bibliographic identification of the 1678 Milan editio princeps from Ludovico Monza's press.
2. :ref:`Dedication to Duke Ferdinando Carlo <statica-prolegomena-dedicatio>` (``0012``–``0014``): Patronage appeal to the Duke of Mantua, Monferrato, and Guastalla.
3. :ref:`Preface to the Reader <statica-prolegomena-prooemium>` (``0015``–``0017``): Intellectual genesis of *Statica Constructio*, honoring Archimedes, Apollonius, Pappus, Cavalieri, and Donato Rossetti.
