Statica Prolegomena · Executive Geometric Summary
=================================================

Core Theses of the Frontmatter & Methodological Prooemium (1678)
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. **The Principle of *Statica Constructio***: Rather than introducing clever auxiliary parallels or circles *ad hoc* for each incidence theorem, :person:`Giovanni Ceva <ceva>` replaces geometric straight lines with exact non-zero point masses placed at vertices. Because addition of weighted points is **commutative and associative** and the global :term:`center of gravity <centrum-gravitatis>` is **unique** (*Axioma* I), grouping base loads into composite fulcrums forces cevian lines to concur at the global center of gravity.
2. **The Epistemological Break from Cavalieri's Indivisibles**: Ceva confesses that in his youth he was obstinately absorbed in attempts at circle quadrature until reading :person:`Bonaventura Cavalieri's <cavalieri>` *Geometria Indivisibilibus Continuorum Nova* (:cite:`1635 <cavalieri:1635:indivis>`). The theory of indivisibles cured him of naive quadrature illusions, leading him to seek a systematic, mechanical foundation for linear geometry.
3. **The Typography of Mechanics**: Milanese printer :person:`Ludovico Monza <monza>` establishes a distinctive typographic convention for static operations: the :term:`obelus or dagger † <obelus-additio>` denotes additive weight combination (``A † F`` = $A + F$), while the minus sign ``-`` denotes subtractive or analytic negative weights (``F - A`` = $F - A$).
