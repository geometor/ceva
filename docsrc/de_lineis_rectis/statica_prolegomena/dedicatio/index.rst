Dedication to Duke Ferdinando Carlo
===================================

.. folio:: 0012
   :page: Dedicatio [f. ✠2r]

.. picture:: 0012.g1.png
   :alt: 1678 woodcut headpiece with winged putti and floral urn atop Ceva's Dedication

**To the Most Serene Duke of Mantua, Ferdinando Carlo**

I should fear, Most Serene Duke, to prefix so illustrious a name to this slender volume, were it not a universal right to shelter oneself and one's work under the protection of Princes. For God, Father and Governor of all lights, willed these Most Serene Suns to shine upon all, casting their beneficent radiance far across humankind.

Geometry—which, deprived of the shelter of patrons, loses all vigor and life—therefore begs Your Highness's benign gaze. Although this booklet is modest in bulk, it is born of prolonged vigil and submits its new :term:`static construction <statica-constructio>` of intersecting lines to the patronage of the House of Gonzaga.

Your Most Serene Highness's most humble servant, **Giovanni Ceva**.
