Conspectus Operis
-----------------

Published in Milan in 1678 by :person:`Ludovico Monza <monza>` when :person:`Giovanni Ceva <ceva>` was thirty years old, *De lineis rectis se invicem secantibus statica constructio* (:cite:`1678 <ceva:1678:dlr>`) transformed :person:`Archimedes' <archimedes>` Law of the Lever into a universal calculus of geometric incidence and proportion. Each unit of this **Four-Track Bilingual Book Codex** provides synchronized reading tabs (**English**, **Latin**, **Summary**, and **Notes**) wired to interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVG figures:

* **Unit I** (*Dedication, Prooemium, Printer's Note & Liber Primus Opening · pp. 1–7, Tab. 1, Figs. 1–8*): :doc:`Static Foundations, Negative Weights & Skew Quadrilaterals <unit-1-statica-foundations/unit-1-statica-foundations-en>` — Dedication to :person:`Ferdinando Carlo Gonzaga <gonzaga>`, *Prooemium* honoring :person:`Donato Rossetti <rossetti>` and defining :term:`statica constructio <statica-constructio>`, :person:`Monza's <monza>` typographic obelus ``†`` for addition (:term:`A † F <obelus-additio>`), *Axiomata I–II*, *Lemmata I–II* ($A : B = BF : FA$), *Elementum I* (Menelaus sector), *Elementum II* (interior concurrency), *Elementum III & p. 5 Scholium* (**algebraic negative weights** :term:`F - A <pondus-negativum>`), and *Elementa IV–V Corollarium* (**3D non-planar skew quadrilateral**).
