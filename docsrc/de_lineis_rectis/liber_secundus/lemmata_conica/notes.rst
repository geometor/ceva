Lemmas I–III: Polygons Circumscribed About Circles and Ellipses — Notes
=======================================================================

.. folio:: 0052
   :page: p. 35

**Archival & Symbolic Notes**:

* Printed pp. 35–37 (folios ``0052``–``0054``), `Tab. 4, Figs. 32–37`.
* Ceva's oblique-cylinder proof of *Lemma I* is one of the earliest explicit uses of affine projection in seventeenth-century geometry: because parallel projection preserves ratios of collinear segments, any tangent-ratio identity proved for the circle transfers instantaneously to every ellipse!
* Verified algebraically for general $n$-gons in :func:`geometor.ceva.polygon_tetrahedron.prove_circumscribed_polygon_lemma`.
