Proposition I: Inscribed Conic Concurrency & Converse of Ceva's Theorem
=======================================================================

.. folio:: 0055
   :page: p. 38

.. picture:: 0109.g38.svg
   :alt: Calibrated interactive SVG of Ceva Liber II Prop. I Inscribed Ellipse & Converse of Ceva (Tab. IV Fig. 38)

.. picture:: 0109.g38.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula IV Fig. 38: Triangle circumscribed about ellipse and Converse of Ceva

**Proposition I · Theorem I** (p. 38, `Tab. 4, Fig. 38`)

*If any triangle be circumscribed about a circle or an ellipse, the straight lines drawn from the points of contact to the opposite angles of the same triangle will intersect one another at the same point, and, with the circle removed, there will arise that figure which we considered in the second element.*

Let :g:`BFI` be a circle or an ellipse, about which let triangle :g:`AGC` be circumscribed, touching the aforesaid section at points :g:`F`, :g:`I`, :g:`B`, and let two joined lines :g:`AI` and :g:`CF` cut one another at :g:`E`;
I say that if :g:`GE` be joined and produced, it will pass through the remaining contact :g:`B`. For if this is not true, let it pass through :g:`H`; and since by the second element straight line :g:`CH` to :g:`HA`,
namely weight :g:`A` to :g:`C`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`A` to :g:`G` to :g:`C`, that is of straight lines :g:`GF` to :g:`FA` and :g:`CI` to :g:`IG`,
and likewise from the conic section straight line :g:`CB` to :g:`BA` is compounded of the same two ratios, it will be as :g:`CH` to :g:`HA`, so :g:`CB` to :g:`BA`, and by composition :g:`CA` to :g:`AH` as :g:`CA` to :g:`AB`;
therefore :g:`AH` will equal :g:`AB`, the whole to a part, which is absurd. It is therefore necessary that the joined line :g:`GE`, if produced, fall upon the point of contact :g:`B`.

**Corollary**


*Hence it is manifest that if the ratio* :g:`CB` *to* :g:`BA` *be compounded of the ratios* :g:`GF` *to* :g:`FA` *and* :g:`CI` *to* :g:`IG`, *and* :g:`AI`, :g:`CF`, :g:`GB` *be joined, they will meet at* :g:`E`. *For if, for example,* :g:`GB` *does not pass through* :g:`E`,
 *where* :g:`AI` *and* :g:`CF` *intersect, let* :g:`GE` *be joined and produced, and it will fall on another point* :g:`H`. *Therefore* :g:`CH` *to* :g:`HA` *will be, as has been shown by the second element, compounded of the ratios* :g:`GF` *to* :g:`FA` *and* :g:`CI` *to* :g:`IG`:
 *wherefore as* :g:`CB` *to* :g:`BA`, *so* :g:`CH` *to* :g:`HA`, *and by composition, as* :g:`CA` *to* :g:`AB`, *so* :g:`CA` *to* :g:`AH`, *which is absurd.*
