Proposition XVII: Concurrency from Three Inscribed Pairwise Tangent Circles — Summary
=====================================================================================

.. folio:: 0070
   :page: p. 53

*Book II, Proposition XVII* (p. 53, `Tab. 8, Fig. 62`) applies *Lemma VI* and *Prop. XVI* to three circles tangent pairwise to the sides of a triangle.
