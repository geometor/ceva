Proposition XX: Parallel Segments Cut by a Transversal
======================================================

.. folio:: 0075
   :page: p. 58

.. picture:: 0117.g67.svg
   :alt: Calibrated interactive SVG — Ceva 1678 Tabula IX Fig. 67: Prop. XX

.. picture:: 0117.g67.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula VIII Fig. 67: Parallel segments DC and EK cut by transversal CIB

**Proposition XX · Theorem XVII** (p. 58, `Tab. 8, Fig. 67`)

*If from the extremity of any straight line we draw two lines to the endpoints of another line parallel to the first, and then from the other extremity of the first parallel another line be drawn cutting the two former lines between the parallels:* 
*the ratio of the parallels will be compounded from two ratios, of which one is formed from the portions of the cutting line, and the other from the segment and the whole straight line intercepted between the parallels upon which the cutting line itself terminates, so that all homologous terms are coterminous.*

Let there be two parallel lines :g:`DC`, :g:`EK`, and from the same endpoint :g:`D` let us draw two lines to the endpoints of the other parallel, which are :g:`DK`, :g:`DE`; 
moreover from the other endpoint :g:`C` let another straight line :g:`CIB` be drawn cutting :g:`DK` at :g:`I` and :g:`DE` at :g:`B`. I say that the ratio of :g:`DC` to :g:`EK` is compounded from the two ratios :g:`CI` to :g:`IB` and :g:`BD` to :g:`DE`. 
Since :g:`BC` cuts :g:`DC`, one of the parallels, if produced it will also meet the other parallel :g:`KE` produced at :g:`A`. Since therefore :g:`DC` to :g:`EK` is compounded of the ratios of straight lines :g:`DC` to :g:`AE` and :g:`AE` to :g:`EK`, and as :g:`DC` to :g:`AE`, so :g:`DB` to :g:`BE` because of the parallels, :g:`DC` to :g:`EK` will be compounded of the ratios :g:`DB` to :g:`BE` and :g:`AE` to :g:`EK`. Yet in the First Element whose weights are :g:`A`, :g:`K`, :g:`D` and center :g:`B`, the ratio of straight line :g:`AE` to :g:`EK`, namely of weight :g:`K` to :g:`A`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`K` to :g:`I` to :g:`A`, namely of straight lines :g:`DI` to :g:`IK` and :g:`AB` to :g:`BI`, that is, of straight lines :g:`IC` to :g:`CA` and :g:`AB` to :g:`BI`. 
Therefore the ratio :g:`DC` to :g:`EK` will be compounded of the ratios :g:`DB` to :g:`BE`, :g:`IC` to :g:`CA`, and :g:`AB` to :g:`BI`. And since :g:`IC` to :g:`CA` arises from the ratios :g:`IC` to :g:`CB` and :g:`CB` to :g:`CA`, and as :g:`CB` to :g:`CA`, so :g:`DB` to :g:`DE`, :g:`DC` to :g:`EK` will be compounded of the ratios :g:`DB` to :g:`BE`, :g:`IC` to :g:`CB`, :g:`DB` to :g:`DE`, and :g:`AB` to :g:`BI`. Yet :g:`AB` to :g:`BI` is compounded of :g:`AB` to :g:`BC` and :g:`BC` to :g:`BI`, and :g:`AB` is to :g:`BC` as :g:`EB` to :g:`BD`; therefore the aforesaid ratio :g:`DC` to :g:`EK` will be compounded of :g:`DB` to :g:`BE`, :g:`IC` to :g:`CB`, :g:`BD` to :g:`DE`, :g:`EB` to :g:`BD`, and :g:`BC` to :g:`BI`, or of the same taken perturbedly—that is, of :g:`DB` to :g:`BE` to :g:`BD` to :g:`DE` and :g:`IC` to :g:`CB` to :g:`BI`—nay, of the two ratios :g:`BD` to :g:`DE` and :g:`IC` to :g:`BI`, which was to be proved.

**Corollary.**

*It is manifest that, given the two ratios* :g:`BD` *to* :g:`DE` *and* :g:`IC` *to* :g:`BI`, *the ratio* :g:`DC` *to* :g:`EK` *compounded from them is also given.*
