**Proposition V · Theorem V** (pp. 40–41, `Tab. 5, Fig. 41`)

*If three sides of a triangle be so divided that the ratio of the parts of one is formed from the ratios of the parts of the remaining sides; then, the points of division being joined* 
*by three straight lines, so that from them there stands a triangle inscribed in the former, whose sides we likewise partition in the same manner; and finally, from the angles of the triangle to the found second points of division* 
*we draw three other lines, these, if produced, will meet at the same point.*

Let there be triangle :g:`ADG`, whose three sides :g:`GA`, :g:`AD`, :g:`DG` are so divided at :g:`H`, :g:`C`, :g:`E` that :g:`GH` to :g:`HA` is compounded of the ratios :g:`DC` to :g:`CA`, :g:`GE` to :g:`ED`. 
Let lines :g:`HC`, :g:`CE`, :g:`EH` be joined, and let them be so cut at points :g:`N`, :g:`L`, :g:`K` that likewise :g:`EL` to :g:`LC` is compounded of the ratios :g:`HN` to :g:`NC`, and :g:`EK` to :g:`KH`. Lastly let us join lines

:g:`DL`, :g:`GK`, :g:`AN`. I say that these, when produced, cut one another at the same point. Let them therefore be produced, and let line :g:`GK` meet side :g:`DA` at :g:`B`; let :g:`DL` itself meet side :g:`AG` at :g:`I`, and lastly let straight line :g:`AN` cut :g:`DG` at :g:`F`. 
Since in the third element, whose weights are :g:`A`, :g:`C-A`, :g:`E-G`, :g:`G`, and whose center is :g:`L`, straight line :g:`GI` to :g:`IA`, that is weight :g:`A` to :g:`G`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`A` to :g:`C` to :g:`E` to :g:`G`, 
namely of straight lines :g:`CD` to :g:`DA`, :g:`EL` to :g:`LC`, and :g:`DG` to :g:`ED`; but the ratio :g:`EL` to :g:`LC` is compounded of the ratios :g:`HN` to :g:`NC`, and :g:`EK` to :g:`KH`; therefore :g:`GI` to :g:`IA` will be compounded of the ratios :g:`CD` to :g:`DA`, 
:g:`HN` to :g:`NC`, :g:`EK` to :g:`KH`, and :g:`DG` to :g:`ED`; or of the ratios :g:`CD` to :g:`CA` to :g:`AD`, :g:`HN` to :g:`NC`, :g:`EK` to :g:`KH`, :g:`DG` to :g:`GE` to :g:`ED`; but of the two ratios :g:`DC` to :g:`CA`, and :g:`GE` to :g:`ED`, is compounded :g:`HG` to :g:`AH`, 
which is compounded of the two :g:`HG` to :g:`GA` to :g:`AH`; therefore the ratio :g:`GI` to :g:`IA` will be compounded of the ratios, albeit taken in perturbed order, :g:`HG` to :g:`GA` to :g:`AH`, :g:`CA` to :g:`AD`, :g:`DG` to :g:`GE`, :g:`HN` to :g:`NC`, and :g:`EK` to :g:`KH`; 
or again of the same taken in perturbed order, namely :g:`HG` to :g:`GA`, :g:`EK` to :g:`KH`, :g:`DG` to :g:`GE`, :g:`CA` to :g:`AD`, :g:`HN` to :g:`NC`, :g:`GA` to :g:`AH`; and since in the third element, whose weights are :g:`A`, :g:`H-A`, :g:`E-D`, and :g:`D`, and whose center is :g:`K`, 
:g:`DB` to :g:`BA`, namely weight :g:`A` to :g:`D`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`A` to :g:`H` to :g:`E` to :g:`D`, or rather of straight lines :g:`HG` to :g:`GA`, :g:`EK` to :g:`KH`, and :g:`DG` to :g:`GE`; 
and likewise in the third element, whose weights are :g:`D`, :g:`C-D`, :g:`H-G`, :g:`G`, and whose center is :g:`N`, the ratio :g:`GF` to :g:`FD`, namely of weight :g:`D` to :g:`G`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`D` to :g:`C` to :g:`H` to :g:`G`, 
namely :g:`CA` to :g:`AD`, :g:`HN` to :g:`NC`, and :g:`GA` to :g:`AH`: the ratio :g:`GI` to :g:`IA`, which was compounded of the ratios :g:`HG` to :g:`GA`, :g:`EK` to :g:`KH`, :g:`DG` to :g:`GE`, :g:`CA` to :g:`AD`, :g:`HN` to :g:`NC`, and :g:`GA` to :g:`AH`, 
will therefore also be compounded of the ratios :g:`DB` to :g:`BA`, and :g:`GF` to :g:`FD`; and therefore by the corollary of Prop. I of this book, lines :g:`AN`, :g:`DL`, :g:`GK` will meet at the same point, which was &c.
