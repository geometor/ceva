Proposition VIII: Segment Ratio on a Cevian Intersecting a Chord
================================================================

.. folio:: 0061
   :page: p. 44

.. picture:: 0111.g46.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula V Fig. 46: Cevian segment ratio cut by side chord

**Proposition VIII · Theorem VIII** (p. 44, `Tab. 5, Fig. 46`)

*In triangle :g:`ACE`, let a straight line :g:`CG` from vertex :g:`C` to base :g:`AE` intersect a chord :g:`BD` joining two proportional side-points at :g:`H`. Ceva determines the exact ratio $\overline{CH} : \overline{HG}$ directly in terms of the side ratios via Element III.*
