Proposition XI: Concurrency on Three Tangents to a Parabola — Summary
=====================================================================

.. folio:: 0064
   :page: p. 47

*Book II, Proposition XI* (pp. 47–48, `Tab. 6, Fig. 50`) proves that lines from the vertices of a triangle circumscribed about a parabola to the opposite points of tangency concur at a single point :math:`H`—an early special case of Brianchon's theorem.
