Proposition XXI & Scholium: Optical Rangefinder & End of Static Construction — Notes
====================================================================================

.. folio:: 0076
   :page: p. 59

**Archival & Historical Discovery**:

* Printed pp. 59–60 (folios ``0076``–``0077``). Notice that Ceva's optical rangefinder diagram sits at the bottom-right corner of the **final copperplate plate** (`Tab. 10, Fig. 85`, scan ``0121``), extracted cleanly here as `0121.g85.png` alongside our calibrated vector schematic `0121.g85.svg`.
* Ceva's witty confession on p. 60—*"Cum enim in circulo inutiliter quadrando, hæc omnia non inutiliter sint inuenta"* ("While squaring the circle uselessly, all these were found not uselessly")—explains the exact origin of the *Appendix Geometrica* that begins on the very next page (p. 61, folio ``0078``).
