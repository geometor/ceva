**Proposition VI · Theorem VI** (pp. 41–43, `Tab. 5, Fig. 42`)

*If the sides and base of a triangle be bisected, and from the section of the base two indefinite straight lines be drawn through the divisions of the sides; then, through the vertex of the triangle, outside it, another straight line be drawn* 
*cutting those lines which we drew from the said point of the base; finally from the two points of those divisions to the angles*

.. folio:: 0059
   :page: p. 42

*adjacent to the base, and toward the same parts, two other lines be drawn, these will be parallel to one another.*

Let there be triangle :g:`DAG`, whose sides :g:`DA`, :g:`AG` and base :g:`DG` are bisected at points :g:`E`, :g:`K`, :g:`F`; and with :g:`FE`, :g:`FK` joined let them be produced indefinitely; and let straight line :g:`BAH` be drawn both ways through vertex :g:`A`, 
cutting the aforesaid indefinite lines however it may at points :g:`B`, :g:`H`, yet so that it lies wholly outside triangle :g:`AGD`, and let :g:`BD`, :g:`HG` be joined; I say that these are parallel to one another. 
For either :g:`BH` and :g:`DG` are parallel to one another, or not; if they are parallel, let :g:`EK` be joined, which will be parallel to :g:`DG`, and therefore also to straight line :g:`BHA`. Wherefore since :g:`DG` is to :g:`EK` as :g:`DA` is to :g:`AE`, 
or as :g:`AD` to :g:`DE`, or :g:`BF` to :g:`FE` by the supposed parallels; and as :g:`BF` to :g:`FE`, so :g:`BH` to :g:`EK`; :g:`DG` will have the same ratio to :g:`EK` as :g:`BH` has to the same :g:`EK`; 
and therefore :g:`DG`, :g:`BH` will be equal, and they are also parallel; hence :g:`BHGD` will be a parallelogrammic space, and so :g:`HG`, :g:`BD` will themselves also be parallel: 
But if :g:`BH`, :g:`DG` be not parallel, let them be produced until they meet at point :g:`I`. And since :g:`BI` to :g:`IH` is compounded of the ratios :g:`BI` to :g:`IA` to :g:`IH`, 
and straight line :g:`BI` to :g:`IA`, that is weight :g:`A` to :g:`B` in the first element, whose weights are :g:`B`, :g:`I`, :g:`D`, and center :g:`E`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`A` to :g:`E` to :g:`B`, 
namely of straight lines :g:`ED` to :g:`DA`, and :g:`FB` to :g:`FE`; and as :g:`FB` to :g:`FE`, that is :g:`BH` to :g:`HA`, so :g:`FH` to :g:`HK` on account of parallelogram :g:`AKFE`, 
:g:`BI` to :g:`IA` is compounded of the ratios :g:`ED` to :g:`DA`, and :g:`FH` to :g:`HK`. Likewise, since in the first element, whose weights are :g:`AIF`, and center :g:`K`, :g:`IA` to :g:`IH`, namely weight :g:`H` to :g:`A`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`H` to :g:`K` to :g:`A`, 
namely of straight lines :g:`KF` to :g:`FH`, and :g:`AG` to :g:`GK`; and as :g:`AG` to :g:`GK`, so :g:`DA` to :g:`DE`, therefore :g:`IA` to :g:`IH` is compounded of the ratios :g:`KF` to :g:`FH`, and :g:`DA` to :g:`DE`; 
for this reason the former ratio :g:`BI` to :g:`IH` will be formed from the ratios :g:`ED` to :g:`DA`, :g:`FH` to :g:`HK`, :g:`KF` to :g:`FH`, and :g:`DA` to :g:`DE`; or from the same taken in perturbed order, that is from the ratios :g:`ED` to :g:`DA` to :g:`DE`, 
:g:`KF` to :g:`FH` to :g:`HK`; and so as :g:`BI` to :g:`IH`, so :g:`KF` to :g:`HK`.

Again :g:`DI` to :g:`IG` is compounded of the ratios :g:`DI` to :g:`IF` to :g:`IG`: But in the first element, whose weights are :g:`DIB`, and center :g:`E`, 
straight line :g:`DI` to :g:`IF`, that is weight :g:`F` to :g:`D`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`F` to :g:`E` to :g:`D`, namely of straight lines :g:`EB` to :g:`BF`, or :g:`AB` to :g:`BH`; or rather :g:`KF` to :g:`FH`, and :g:`DA` to :g:`AE`, 
and likewise in the first element,

whose weights are :g:`FIA`, and center :g:`K`, straight line :g:`IF` to :g:`IG` is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`G` to :g:`K` to :g:`F`, namely of straight lines :g:`KA` to :g:`AG`, and :g:`FH` to :g:`HK`; 
therefore the former ratio :g:`DI` to :g:`IG` is compounded of the ratios of straight lines :g:`KF` to :g:`FH`, :g:`DA` to :g:`AE`, :g:`KA` to :g:`AG`, and :g:`FH` to :g:`HK`; or from the same in perturbed order, namely from the ratios :g:`KF` to :g:`FH` to :g:`HK`, 
:g:`KA` to :g:`AG`, and :g:`DA` to :g:`AE`, or :g:`AG` to :g:`KA`. Wherefore :g:`DI` to :g:`IG` will be as :g:`KF` to :g:`HK`, namely in the same ratio in which :g:`BI` was to :g:`IH`; therefore :g:`BD` will be parallel to :g:`GH`, which &c.
