============================================================================

.. folio:: 0058

*Book II, Proposition VI* (pp. 41–43, `Tab. 5, Fig. 42`) applies static balance on midpoint transversals to deduce exact parallelism ($BD \parallel HG$) from an exterior vertex ray.
