Proposition XII: Cubic Area Ratio on Three Parabola Tangents
============================================================

.. folio:: 0065
   :page: p. 48

**Proposition XII · Theorem XI** (pp. 48–49, `Tab. 6, Fig. 50`)

*In the same parabolic tangent triangle with concurrency point :g:`H`, the ratio of the two sub-triangles $\triangle FHA$ and $\triangle DHC$ equals the **triplicate (cubic)** ratio of the tangent segments :g:`FE` to :g:`ED`:*

$$\triangle FHA : \triangle DHC = \overline{FE}^3 : \overline{ED}^3.$$

**Scholium**: Notes the immediate extension to area ratios of all remaining sub-triangles around :g:`H`.
