Proposition XVIII: Equal Rectangles on Two Circles Tangent to Two Lines — Summary
=================================================================================

.. folio:: 0070
   :page: p. 53

*Book II, Proposition XVIII* (p. 53, `Tab. 7, Fig. 59`) proves an equal-rectangle power-of-a-point relation for two circles tangent to the same pair of intersecting rays.
