Book II — Summary
=================

.. folio:: 0052
   :page: p. 35

*Liber Secundus* (pp. 35–60, folios ``0052``–``0077``) spans 22 atomic leaves across four major themes:

* **Cylindrical Projection & Converse of Ceva (`pp. 35–39`)**: Lemmas I–III (Borelli, Serenus, Apollonius), Prop. I (inscribed conic concurrency & explicit Converse of Ceva's Theorem), Prop. II (circumscribed quadrilateral).
* **Nested Cevians & Conic Inscription (`pp. 39–46`)**: Props. III–IX & Lemmas IV–V (parallel chords, nested Ceva triangles, outer-vertex/midpoint concurrency, inscribing an ellipse given two tangency points).
* **Parabola, Ellipse & Circle Tangents (`pp. 47–53`)**: Props. X–XVIII & Lemma VI (three parabola tangents squared/concurrent/cubic laws, ex-conic concurrency, angle-bisector Incenter `Tab. 7, Fig. 57`, common circle tangents).
* **Reciprocal Triangles & Optical Rangefinder (`pp. 54–60`)**: Props. XIX–XXI & Scholium (15 cases of reciprocally inscribed triangles, single-station optical distance rangefinder `Tab. 10, Fig. 85`, *FINIS CONSTRUCTIONIS STATICÆ*, and Preface to Appendix).
