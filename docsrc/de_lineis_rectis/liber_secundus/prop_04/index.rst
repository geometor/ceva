Proposition IV: Proportionally Divided Quadrilateral
====================================================

.. folio:: 0057
   :page: p. 40

.. picture:: 0111.g40.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula V Fig. 40: Quadrilateral with proportionally divided opposite pairs

**Proposition IV · Theorem IV** (p. 40, `Tab. 5, Fig. 40`)

*In a quadrilateral :g:`ACEC` whose two pairs of opposite sides are divided in identical ratios at :g:`B, F` and :g:`H, D`, the two opposite joins :g:`BF` and :g:`HD` intersect at :g:`I` and satisfy the full static proportionality of Element V.*
