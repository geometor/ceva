Proposition VII: Concurrency of Outer Vertices with Inscribed Midpoints
=======================================================================

.. folio:: 0060
   :page: p. 43

.. picture:: 0111.g43.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula V Fig. 43: Outer vertices joined to midpoints of inscribed Ceva triangle

.. picture:: 0111.g45.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula V Fig. 45: Auxiliary midpoint concurrency diagram

**Proposition VII · Theorem VII** (pp. 43–44, `Tab. 5, Figs. 43 & 45`)

*In triangle :g:`BAC`, let an inscribed Ceva triangle :g:`DEF` be formed by joining the feet of three concurrent cevians. Join each outer vertex :g:`B`, :g:`A`, :g:`C` to the **midpoint** of the opposite side of the inscribed triangle :g:`DEF`. Then these three lines concur at a single point lying strictly inside the inscribed triangle!*
