Unit III · Liber Primus XXIII–XXVI & Liber Secundus (Textus Latinus, 1678, pp. 36–72)
=====================================================================================

*Folio-verified Neo-Latin primary text from Giovanni Ceva's De lineis rectis se invicem secantibus statica constructio* (:cite:`Mediolani: Ex Typographia Ludovici Montiae, 1678 <ceva:1678:dlr>`, *Liber Primus*, pp. 36–43, & *Liber Secundus*, pp. 45–72; `PDF pp. 53–89`, `Tab. 3–7, Figs. 28–61`).

.. rst-class:: latin-source

1. Liber Primus, Propositio XXIII — Mira Concordia Septem Linearum in Pyramide (pp. 36–39)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. rst-class:: latin-source

**Propositio XXIII. Theorema** (`Tab. 3, Fig. 28`): *In omni pyramide triangulari (seu tetraedro)* :g:`ABCD <tetABCD>` *, positis quatuor ponderibus in ipsius angulis* :g:`A`, :g:`B`, :g:`C`, :g:`D` *, quatuor rectæ ex singulis verticibus ad centra gravitatis basium oppositarum ductæ, simul cum tribus rectis jungentibus centra gravitatis oppositorum laterum, omnes numero septem in uno eodemque puncto* :g:`O` *concurrunt.*

Appendantur enim in verticibus :g:`A`, :g:`B`, :g:`C`, :g:`D` pondera quæcumque finita; sintque :g:`G_A`, :g:`G_B`, :g:`G_C`, :g:`G_D` centra gravitatis trium ponderum in singulis planis triangularibus oppositis consistentium, ac :g:`E_AB`, :g:`E_CD`, :g:`E_AC`, :g:`E_BD`, :g:`E_AD`, :g:`E_BC` centra gravitatis binorum ponderum in sex pyramidis costis. Quoniam totius systematis quatuor ponderum ``A † B † C † D`` unicum dumtaxat est in spatio :term:`centrum gravitatis <centrum-gravitatis>` :g:`O`, necesse est tam quatuor rectas :g:`AG_A`, :g:`BG_B <AG_B>`, :g:`CG_C <AG_C>`, :g:`DG_D <AG_D>`, quam tres rectas :g:`B_AB_CD`, :g:`B_AC_BD`, :g:`B_AD_BC` oppositarum costarum centra jungentes, **mira quadam concordia** (:term:`septem linearum concursus <concordia-septem-linearum>`) in ipso unico puncto :g:`O` se mutuo intersecare!

2. Liber Secundus, Lemma I — De Polygonis Circulo & Ellipsi Circumscriptis (pp. 45–47)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. rst-class:: latin-source

**Lemma I** (`Tab. 4, Figs. 32–35`): *Si circulo vel ellipsi circumscribatur polygonum quotcumque laterum, ratio composita ex rationibus omnium segmentorum in punctis contactuum alternatim sumptorum est ratio æqualitatis.*

In circulo :g:`circleC` res statim patet ex :person:`Euclide <euclid>` (:just:`III.36`), cum binæ contingentes ex eodem polygoni angulo ad circulum ductæ sint inter se æquales. Ut autem idem in quavis **ellipsi** :g:`ellipseE` evincatur, concipiatur super circulo :g:`circleC` cylindrus obliquè sectus plano ellipsim :g:`ellipseE` exhibente, iuxta doctrinam :person:`Sereni <serenus>` (:cite:`1566 <serenus:cylindri>`) ac clarissimi :person:`Ioannis Alphonsi Borelli <borelli>` (:cite:`1661 <borelli:1661:apollonius>`). Quoniam rectæ :g:`projCyl` per contactus cylindri lateribus æquidistantes secant circumscripti polygoni latera in eadem prorsus proportione (:term:`sectio cylindrica <sectio-cylindrica>`), :term:`ratio composita <ratio-composita>` segmentorum circa ellipsim manet pariter ratio æqualitatis!

3. Liber Secundus, Propositio I & Corollarium — Punctum Contactus & Isotomicum (pp. 47–50)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. rst-class:: latin-source

**Propositio I. Theorema** (`Tab. 4, Figs. 36–37`): *Si triangulum* :g:`ABC` *circulo* :g:`incircle` *aut ellipsi cuicumque circumscribatur, rectæ* :g:`AD` *,* :g:`BE` *,* :g:`CF` *ex angulis ad puncta contactuum* :g:`D` *,* :g:`E` *,* :g:`F` *ductæ in unum punctum* :g:`X(7) <X7>` *concurrunt.*

**Corollarium** (:term:`Coniugatum Isotomicum <coniugatum-isotomicum>`): Quod si in eodem triangulo :g:`ABC` sumantur in lateribus puncta :g:`D* <D_iso>`, :g:`E* <E_iso>`, :g:`F* <F_iso>` ab oppositis terminis æqualiter distantia—ita scilicet ut sit $BD^*$ æqualis ipsi $DC$ (:g:`BD_eq`), $CE^*$ ipsi $EA$ (:g:`CE_eq`), et $AF^*$ ipsi $FB$ (:g:`AF_eq`)—quoniam invertendo singulas rationes ratio composita adhuc manet æqualitatis, etiam novæ tres rectæ :g:`AD* <AD_iso>`, :g:`BE* <BE_iso>`, :g:`CF* <CF_iso>` in alio unico puncto :g:`X(8) <X8>` infallibiliter concurrent!

4. Liber Secundus, Propositiones II–XIV — De Polygonis Imparis Laterum Numeri (pp. 50–72)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. rst-class:: latin-source

In **Pentagono** :g:`pentagon`, **Heptagono**, cæterisque figuris multilateris **imparis laterum numeri** ($2m + 1$, `Tab. 4–7, Figs. 38–61`), si rectæ (:g:`cev0`..:g:`cev4`) ex omnibus angulis (:g:`V0`..:g:`V4`) per unum commune punctum :g:`P` eductæ opposita latera secent in (:g:`Q0`..:g:`Q4`), appensis in singulis verticibus idoneis ponderibus staticis, ratio composita omnium segmentorum ordine cyclico sumptorum semper est ratio æqualitatis.
