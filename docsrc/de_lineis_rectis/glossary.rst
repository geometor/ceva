.. _ceva-glossary:

Latin & Mathematical Glossary
=============================

Lexicon of the **16** foundational Neo-Latin, typographic, projective, and symbolic-geometry terms across *De lineis rectis se invicem secantibus statica constructio* (1678) and ``geometor.ceva``.

Static & Algebraic Terminology (*Liber Primus*)
-----------------------------------------------

.. _term-statica-constructio:

* **statica constructio (Static Construction)** — Ceva's methodological principle (*Prooemium*): proving theorems on mutually intersecting straight lines purely by placing hypothetical Archimedean weights at vertices without drawing auxiliary Euclidean circles or construction lines.

.. _term-centrum-gravitatis:

* **centrum gravitatis (Center of Gravity / Barycenter)** — The unique equilibrium point $P = \frac{u A + v B + w C}{u + v + w}$ of a system of point masses (*Axiomata* I–II & *Petitio*), invariant under associative grouping of partial fulcrums.

.. _term-momentum-ponderis:

* **momentum ponderis (Static Moment / Lever Law)** — Archimedes' inverse-proportion rule (*Lemmata* I–II): weights $u$ at $A$ and $v$ at $B$ balance around fulcrum $F$ on $AB$ iff $u \cdot \overline{AF} = v \cdot \overline{FB}$ ($A : B = BF : FA$).

.. _term-pondus-negativum:

* **pondus subtractivum / F - A (Algebraic Negative Weight)** — Ceva's analytic innovation (*Liber Primus, Elementum III & p. 5 Scholium*: *"pondus verò L more analytico, seu algebrico ita scribemus $F - A$, hoc est $F$ minus $A$"*), assigning a subtractive/upward weight to capture exterior concurrency 149 years before Möbius (1827).

.. _term-obelus-additio:

* **nota crucis † pro additione (A † F)** — Milanese printer :person:`Ludovico Monza's <monza>` typographic obelus/dagger ``†`` denoting algebraic addition (`A † F` $= A + F$) throughout the 1678 quarto.

.. _term-ratio-composita:

* **ratio composita (Compound Ratio)** — Sequential product of ratios $\frac{AF}{FB} \cdot \frac{BD}{DC} \cdot \frac{CE}{EA} = 1$, translated from static weight cancellation via Commandino and Clavius (*Props.* VII–VIII).

.. _term-ceviana:

* **linea ceviana (Cevian)** — Any straight line joining a vertex of a triangle, polygon, or tetrahedron to the opposite sideline or face plane.

.. _term-sectio-harmonica:

* **sectio harmonica (Projective Harmonic Range)** — Collinear quadruple $(B, C; D, D') = -1$ where $D$ is a cevian foot ($BD/DC = w/v$) and $D' = EF \cap BC$ is the dual Menelaus transversal intersection ($BD'/D'C = -w/v$).

Polygons, Conics, Spatial Statics & Indivisibles (*Liber I–II & Appendix*)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. _term-coniugatum-isotomicum:

* **coniugatum isotomicum (Isotomic Conjugate)** — Reciprocal-weight concurrency $P^* = (u^{-1} : v^{-1} : w^{-1})$ obtained by reflecting cevian feet across side midpoints (*Liber Secundus, Prop. I Corollarium*), pairing Gergonne $X(7)$ with Nagel $X(8)$.

.. _term-punctum-contactus:

* **punctum contactus / Gergonne X(7)** — Concurrency center of cevians to the contact points of an inscribed circle or ellipse, proved in Ceva's *Liber Secundus, Proposition I* (1678).

.. _term-quadrilaterum-non-planum:

* **quadrilaterum non in eodem plano (3D Skew Quadrilateral)** — Non-planar 3D cycle $ABCD$ (*Liber Primus, Elementum V Corollarium*) whose four weighted edge fulcrums are coplanar and have intersecting opposite joins at $O$.

.. _term-concordia-septem-linearum:

* **mira concordia septem linearum (7-Line Tetrahedral Equilibrium)** — Simultaneous concurrency (*Liber Primus, Prop. XXIII*) of the 4 vertex-to-opposite-face-barycenter cevians and 3 opposite-edge fulcrum joins of a weighted tetrahedron at a single 3D center $O$.

.. _term-sectio-cylindrica:

* **sectio cylindrica / projectio affinis (Cylindrical Projection)** — Oblique plane section of a circular cylinder (*Liber Secundus, Lemma I*, citing Serenus and Borelli) mapping circles to ellipses while preserving all collinear segment ratios.

.. _term-ungula-cylindrica:

* **ungula cylindrica (Cylindrical Hoof)** — Solid wedge cut from a right circular cylinder by a plane through a base diameter, proved in *Appendix Geometrica, Prop. VII* to equal $\frac{2}{3} R^2 H = \frac{2}{3}$ of its circumscribed triangular prism.

.. _term-indivisibilia-cautio:

* **indivisibilia Cavallerii & cautio superficierum** — Ceva's closing demonstration (*Appendix Geometrica*) that summing parallel chords or circles on curved surfaces without slant frustum elements ($ds = dz / \cos\alpha$) produces false surface areas.

.. _term-spread-ceva:

* **Spread Ceva & Quadrance** — Radical-free Rational Trigonometry formulation $\frac{s(\angle BAD)}{s(\angle DAC)} \cdot \frac{s(\angle CBE)}{s(\angle EBA)} \cdot \frac{s(\angle ACF)}{s(\angle FCB)} = 1$ verified over $\mathbb{Q}$ in ``geometor.ceva``.
