De lineis rectis se invicem secantibus statica constructio (1678)
=================================================================

First edition (Milan, 1678), 126 digitized scans preserved at the Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Firenze (Fondo Magliabechiano, shelfmark ``CFMAGL. 1.7.137``).

* **Publisher**: Ex Typographia Ludovici Montiae, Milan
* **Local Path**: ``resources/de_lineis_rectis/pdfs/ceva_1678_de_lineis_rectis.pdf``
* **Rights**: Public Domain (1678)
* **Origin URL**: `https://archive.org/details/ita-bnc-mag-00001346-001 <https://archive.org/details/ita-bnc-mag-00001346-001>`_
* **Holding Institution**: Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Firenze (Magliabechiano ``CFMAGL. 1.7.137``)
* **Physical Collation (126 Scans)**:
  - ``0001``–``0009``: Binding, edges, and BNCF flyleaves (``XI CEV.``)
  - ``0010``–``0017``: Title page, Dedication to Duke Ferdinando Carlo, and *Prooemium*
  - ``0018``–``0051``: *Liber Primus* (printed pp. 1–34; Axioms, Lemmas I–V, Props. I–XXVI)
  - ``0052``–``0077``: *Liber Secundus* (printed pp. 35–60; Lemmas I–VI, Props. I–XXI, Rangefinder Scholium & *FINIS CONSTRUCTIONIS STATICAE*)
  - ``0078``–``0100``: *Appendix Geometrica* (printed pp. 61–83; Props. I–XVI, Indivisibles Scholium, *FINIS*, Errata & Printer's Note on ``✠``)
  - ``0101``–``0102``: *Imprimatur* and Ludovico Monza woodcut colophon (*HINC MICAT / INDE FERIT*)
  - ``0103``–``0126``: Ten folding copperplate plates (`Tabulae I–X`, odd rectos ``0103``–``0121``, Figs. 1–85) and trailing endpapers/back cover
