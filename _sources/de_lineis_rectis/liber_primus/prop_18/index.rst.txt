Proposition XVIII: First Pyramid Ratio of Element IV
====================================================

.. folio:: 0043
   :page: p. 26

.. picture:: 0105.g20.svg
   :alt: Calibrated interactive SVG — Ceva 1678 Tabula II Fig. 20: Prop. XVIII

.. picture:: 0105.g20.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula II Fig. 20: First pyramid ratio of Element IV

**Proposition XVIII · Theorem VIII** (p. 26, `Tab. 2, Fig. 20`)

*With the same figure of the Fourth Element posited, let lines* :g:`EA`, :g:`FC` *be drawn moreover; I say that* :g:`GF` *to* :g:`GE` *is as the pyramid whose base is triangle* :g:`FDC`, *and altitude* :g:`AB`, *to the pyramid whose base is triangle* :g:`ADE` *and altitude* :g:`CB`.

Compounded is the proportion of straight line :g:`GF` to :g:`GE`, namely that of weight :g:`E` to :g:`F`, of the ratios of heavy bodies :g:`E` to :g:`C`, :g:`C` to :g:`A`, and :g:`A` to :g:`F`, namely of straight lines :g:`CD` to :g:`DE`, :g:`AB` to :g:`BC`, and :g:`FD` to :g:`DA`,
or of the same taken perturbedly, namely from :g:`CD` to :g:`DE`, :g:`FD` to :g:`DA`, and :g:`AB` to :g:`BC`, that is, from the ratios of triangle :g:`FDC` to triangle :g:`EDA`, and from straight line :g:`AB` to :g:`BC`;
but from the same ratios is compounded the pyramid whose base is triangle :g:`FDC`, and altitude :g:`AB`, to the pyramid whose base is triangle :g:`ADE` and altitude :g:`BC`; therefore as the former pyramid to the latter, so :g:`GF` to :g:`GE`, which &c.

**Corollary.**

*It is clear that, if straight line* :g:`AB` *be equal to straight line* :g:`BC`, *triangle* :g:`FDC` *is to triangle* :g:`ADE` *as straight line* :g:`GF` *to* :g:`GE`.
