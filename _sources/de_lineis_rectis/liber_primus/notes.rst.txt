Book I — Scholarly & Symbolic Notes
===================================

.. folio:: 0018
   :page: p. 1

**Archival Provenance**: Printed pp. 1–34 (folios ``0018``–``0051``), accompanied by folding copperplate plates `Tabula I` (``0103``, Figs. 1–12), `Tabula II` (``0105``, Figs. 13–24), and `Tabula III` (``0107``, Figs. 25–31).

* **Exact Symbolic Realization**: Every landmark theorem of *Liber Primus* is verified over exact :math:`\mathbb{Q}` and polynomial rings in :mod:`geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics` (interior/exterior concurrency, Caravaggio identity, harmonic Menelaus ranges, and Wildberger Spread Ceva) and :mod:`geometor.ceva.polygon_tetrahedron` (3D skew quadrilateral coplanarity and 7-line tetrahedral equilibrium).
* **1678 Errata Applied**: All marginal figure-number corrections recorded by Ceva on p. 83 (folio ``0100``) — including p. 10 ``fig. 9 -> fig. 10``, p. 10 ``fig. 10 -> fig. 12``, and p. 12 ``fig. 10 -> fig. 12`` — are documented inside the respective proposition leaves.
