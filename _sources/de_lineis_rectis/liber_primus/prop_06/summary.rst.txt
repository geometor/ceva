Lemma III & Proposition VI: Six Cases of Element I (Caravaggio Proof) — Summary
===============================================================================

.. folio:: 0024
   :page: p. 7

After preparatory *Lemma III* (p. 7), *Proposition VI* (pp. 7–10) solves all 6 combinatorial cases of Element I. On p. 10 (folio ``0027``), Ceva preserves a purely Euclidean parallel-line proof of Case I (`Tab. 1, Fig. 10`) communicated by his young Milanese colleague :person:`Pietro Paolo Caravaggio Jr. <caravaggio>`.
