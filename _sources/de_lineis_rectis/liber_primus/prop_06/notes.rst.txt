Lemma III & Proposition VI: Six Cases of Element I (Caravaggio Proof) — Notes
=============================================================================

.. folio:: 0024
   :page: p. 7

**Archival & Errata Notes**:

* Printed pp. 7–10 (folios ``0024``–``0027``).
* **Errata Verification (p. 83, folio ``0100``)**: The 1678 printer accidentally printed ``fig. 9.`` in the margin of p. 10 for Caravaggio's proof; Ceva's Errata table explicitly corrects ``p. 10 in marg. fig. 9. -> fig. 10.``.
* Notice that Ceva praises Caravaggio Jr. as *"son of my teacher"* (*Petri Pauli filius præceptoris sui*), documenting his Milanese mathematical education under Pietro Paolo Caravaggio Sr.
