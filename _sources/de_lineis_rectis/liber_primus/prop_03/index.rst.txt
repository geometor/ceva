Proposition III: Third Element (Exterior Ceva & Negative Weights)
=================================================================

.. folio:: 0021
   :page: p. 4

.. picture:: 0103.g6.svg
   :alt: Calibrated interactive SVG of Ceva Book I Prop. III Elementum III (Tab. I Fig. 6)

.. picture:: 0103.g6.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula I Fig. 6: Elementum III and negative weight Scholium

**Proposition III · Problem III · Element III** (`Tab. 1, Fig. 6`)

*In triangle :g:`EAC`, let a straight line :g:`EB` from vertex :g:`E` to base :g:`AC` intersect a transversal :g:`FD` joining side :g:`EA` to side :g:`EC` at interior point :g:`G`. To find the static equilibrium of weights at the vertices and feet.*

Assign positive weights :g:`H` at :g:`A`, :g:`I` at :g:`E`, and :g:`K` at :g:`E` (decomposing the weight at vertex :g:`E`), balanced by weight :g:`L` at :g:`C` so that :g:`F` balances :g:`H` and :g:`I`, and :g:`D` balances :g:`K` and :g:`L`. Grouping pairwise places the global center of gravity at the intersection :g:`G` of :g:`EB` and :g:`FD`.

**Scholium (p. 5 — Analytic Negative Weights)**: When the required weight at a vertex must act in the subtracted sense to place a fulcrum externally beyond a segment, Ceva writes it in the algebraic style (:term:`pondus negativum <pondus-negativum>`):

> *"And this weight $L$, in the analytic or algebraic manner, we shall write thus: $F - A$, that is, $F$ minus $A$; and similarly $D - C$."*

By allowing subtractive weights, a single unified static calculus governs both interior and exterior concurrency configurations.
