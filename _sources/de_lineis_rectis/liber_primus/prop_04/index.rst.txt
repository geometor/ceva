Proposition IV: Fourth Element (Crossed Lines & the Sign ✠)
===========================================================

.. folio:: 0022
   :page: p. 5

.. picture:: 0103.g7.svg
   :alt: Calibrated interactive SVG of Ceva Book I Prop. IV Elementum IV (Tab. I Fig. 7)

.. picture:: 0103.g7.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula I Fig. 7: Elementum IV crossed bowtie configuration

**Proposition IV · Problem IV · Element IV** (`Tab. 1, Fig. 7`)

*Let two straight lines :g:`AF` and :g:`EC` cross at :g:`D`, joined by opposing bases :g:`AC` and :g:`EF`, and let a third straight line :g:`BG` pass through :g:`D` meeting :g:`AC` at :g:`B` and :g:`EF` at :g:`G`. To establish the static equilibrium of weights at the four corners :g:`A`, :g:`C`, :g:`E`, :g:`F`.*

Suspend four weights :g:`I` at :g:`A`, :g:`H` at :g:`C`, :g:`K` at :g:`E`, and :g:`L` at :g:`F` such that :g:`D` balances both diagonal pairs (:g:`I` with :g:`L` along :g:`AF`, and :g:`K` with :g:`H` along :g:`EC`). Then :g:`D` is the global center of gravity of all four weights. Regrouping along the bases :g:`AC` and :g:`EF`, the fulcrums :g:`B` (of :g:`I` and :g:`H`) and :g:`G` (of :g:`K` and :g:`L`) must lie collinear with :g:`D`, yielding:

$$\overline{BD} : \overline{DG} = (K + L) : (I + H).$$

**Scholium (Introduction of the Addition Symbol ``✠``)**: Here on p. 5 Ceva explicitly introduces his typographic addition symbol (:term:`obelus additionis <obelus-additio>`): *"We write ``A ✠ F``, that is, A plus F."* Because printer :person:`Ludovico Monza <monza>` lacked a standard algebraic ``+`` sort (as noted in the p. 83 Errata note, folio ``0100``), he set the ornate Maltese/patonce cross ``✠`` throughout the treatise.
