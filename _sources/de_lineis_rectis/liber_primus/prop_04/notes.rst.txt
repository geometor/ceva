Proposition IV: Fourth Element (Crossed Lines & the Sign ✠) — Notes
===================================================================

.. folio:: 0022
   :page: p. 5

**Archival & Typographical Note**:

* Printed p. 5 (folio ``0022``), `Tab. 1, Fig. 7`.
* Direct visual inspection of folio ``0022`` confirms that **both** the negative-weight Scholium (``F - A``, end of Prop. III) and the addition-symbol Scholium (``A ✠ F, hoc est A plus F``, end of Prop. IV) appear on **printed p. 5**!
* In Book I Prop. XVII Corollary (p. 25, folio ``0042``), Ceva demonstrates that Element IV and Element III are projectively equivalent by folding/unfolding the crossed plane.
