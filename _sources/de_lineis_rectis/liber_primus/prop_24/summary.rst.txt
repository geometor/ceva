Proposition XXIV: Five Concurrent Lines in a Quadrangular Pyramid — Summary
===========================================================================

.. folio:: 0049
   :page: p. 32

*Proposition XXIV* (pp. 32–33, `Tab. 3, Fig. 29`) extends 3D static concurrency from the tetrahedron to a **quadrangular pyramid**, proving that five spatial levers joining complementary vertex-subsets concur at a single center $T$.
