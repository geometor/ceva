Proposition XXIV: Five Concurrent Lines in a Quadrangular Pyramid
=================================================================

.. folio:: 0049
   :page: p. 32

.. picture:: 0107.g29.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula III Fig. 29: Quadrangular pyramid five-line concurrency

**Proposition XXIV · Theorem XIII**

*Let there be a pyramid whose vertex is* :g:`E`, *and whose base is any quadrilateral* :g:`CBAD`; *and within triangle* :g:`BEC`, *three straight lines being drawn intersecting one another at the same point* :g:`N`, *such as are the lines* :g:`BNK`, :g:`CNO`, :g:`ENP`, *so that the figure of the Second Element is made;* 
*let* :g:`AO`, :g:`DK` *be joined, and in triangles* :g:`BEA`, :g:`CED` *let two figures of the same Element be completed,* :g:`BOEFARQ` *and* :g:`CKEGDML`; *and with lines* :g:`AG`, :g:`DF` *joined intersecting each other at* :g:`H`, *let* :g:`EH` *be produced to* :g:`I`, *and let two lines* :g:`IP`, :g:`RM` *be joined* 
*so that they meet each other at point* :g:`S`. *Finally, let the lines* :g:`ES`, :g:`QM`, :g:`HP`, :g:`LR`, :g:`NI` *be conceived within the pyramid; I say that these are cut at the same point, such as* :g:`T`. *Now to demonstrate this, let us suspend, as was done in the preceding,* 
*from the angles of the same pyramid five weights ✠, V, X, Z, ℞, of which any two to the remaining three, or two to two, or two to one,* 
*or one to one, or one to four, will be reciprocally among themselves as the lengths of those balance-beams from which they hang.*

(`Tab. 3, Fig. 29`) Let it be made as :g:`DG` to :g:`GE`, so weight ℞ to ✠; as :g:`CK` to :g:`KE`, so ℞ to :g:`Z`; 
and as :g:`AF` to :g:`FE`, so ℞ to :g:`V`. Now since in each of the four figures of the Second Element which on the surface of the pyramid, the base excepted,

.. folio:: 0050
   :page: p. 33

we have constructed, we have adapted three weights according to the laws of the Second Element, :g:`BR` will be to :g:`RA` as weight :g:`V` to ✠; :g:`CP` to :g:`PB` as ✠ to :g:`Z`; and :g:`DM` to :g:`MC` as :g:`Z` to :g:`X`.
Wherefore since :g:`DI` to :g:`IA`, that is, weight :g:`V` to :g:`X`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`V` to ✠, ✠ to :g:`Z`, and :g:`Z` to :g:`X`, namely of straight lines :g:`BR` to :g:`RA`, :g:`CP` to :g:`PB`, and :g:`DM` to :g:`MC`,
:g:`ARBPCMDIAS` will be a figure of the Fifth Element, whose heavy bodies are :g:`V`, ✠, :g:`Z`, :g:`X`; thus since their center is at :g:`S`, and heavy body ℞ is at :g:`E`, on line :g:`ES` will be the center of gravity of all the heavy bodies :g:`V`, ✠, :g:`Z`, :g:`X`, ℞.
Again, because at :g:`R` is the center of heavy bodies :g:`V`, ✠, and at :g:`L` the center of heavy bodies ℞, :g:`Z`, :g:`X`, the center of all heavy bodies :g:`V`, ✠, :g:`Z`, :g:`X`, ℞ will likewise be on balance-beam :g:`RL` in the same former position;
and since this is unique, and by the same reason exists likewise on balance-beams :g:`NI`, :g:`QM`, :g:`HP`, it is necessary that all the said lines :g:`ES`, :g:`LR`, :g:`NI`, :g:`QM`, :g:`HP` intersect one another at the same point, such as :g:`T`;
and since this is at once the center of the same balance-beams, it is established that :g:`RT` to :g:`TL` is as the three heavy bodies :g:`Z`, ℞, :g:`X` to the two :g:`V`, ✠; :g:`IT` to :g:`TN` as the three heavy bodies ✠, ℞, :g:`Z` to the two :g:`V`, :g:`X`;
:g:`MT` to :g:`TQ` as the three ✠, :g:`V`, ℞ to the two :g:`Z`, :g:`X`; :g:`HT` to :g:`TP` as the two ✠, :g:`Z` to the three :g:`V`, ℞, :g:`X`; and finally :g:`ET` to :g:`TS` as the four :g:`V`, ✠, :g:`Z`, :g:`X` to ℞;
and the remaining ratios are manifest from the Second and Fifth Elements; therefore the proposition is clear.
