Proposition VII & Lemma IV: Pyramid Ratios and Compound Proportions — Summary
=============================================================================

.. folio:: 0027
   :page: p. 10

*Proposition VII* (pp. 10–11, `Tab. 1, Fig. 12`) converts the static weight ratios of Element I into pure Euclidean area/solid ratios citing :person:`Commandino <commandino>` on Euclid VI.23, while *Lemma IV* (pp. 11–12, citing :person:`Clavius <clavius>` on VIII.19) establishes the permutation rules for :term:`compound ratios <ratio-composita>`.
