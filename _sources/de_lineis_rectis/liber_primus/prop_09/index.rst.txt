Proposition IX: Fifteen Combinatorial Cases of Element II
=========================================================

.. folio:: 0030
   :page: p. 13

**Proposition IX · Problem VII** (pp. 13–15, `Tab. 1, Fig. 5`)

*In the Second Element (interior Ceva triangle :g:`AEC` with concurrent cevians :g:`AK`, :g:`EB`, :g:`CD` meeting at :g:`F`), given any two of the six segment ratios (three along the perimeter sides and three along the interior cevians), to find the remaining four ratios.*

Because there are $\binom{6}{2} = 15$ distinct pairs of given ratios among $(AD:DE)$, $(EK:KC)$, $(CB:BA)$, $(AF:FK)$, $(EF:FB)$, and $(CF:FD)$, Ceva works through all **15 cases** methodically, reducing each pair to the three vertex weights $G, H, I$ and reading off every remaining segment ratio by inspection.
