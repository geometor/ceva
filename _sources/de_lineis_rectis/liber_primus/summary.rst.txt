Book I — Summary
================

.. folio:: 0018
   :page: p. 1

*Liber Primus* (pp. 1–34, folios ``0018``–``0051``) develops the full static calculus of intersecting lines across 27 atomic sections:

* **Foundations (`pp. 1–2`)**: Axioms I–II, Postulate, and Archimedean Lever Lemmas I–II (`Tab. 1, Figs. 1–3`).
* **The Five Elements (`pp. 2–7`)**: Prop. I (Menelaus sector), Prop. II (Ceva interior concurrency), Prop. III (exterior negative weights ``F - A``), Prop. IV (crossed lines & ``✠``), Prop. V (planar & 3D skew quadrilateral).
* **Combinatorial & Euclidean Resolution (`pp. 7–30`)**: Props. VI–XXI (with Lemmas III–V, Caravaggio Jr.'s pure geometric proofs on pp. 10 & 15, Commandino/Clavius compound ratios, and Case XIX cossic algebra on p. 29).
* **3D Solid Statics (`pp. 30–34`)**: Props. XXII–XXVI (`Tab. 3, Figs. 25–31`), crowning with the 7-line tetrahedron concurrency theorem (*Prop. XXIII*).
