Proposition XII: Ten Combinatorial Cases of Element III
=======================================================

.. folio:: 0035
   :page: p. 18

.. picture:: 0105.g15.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula II Fig. 15: Element III combinatorial cases

**Proposition XII · Problem IX** (pp. 18–21, `Tab. 2, Fig. 15` & `Tab. 1, Fig. 6`)

*In the Third Element (triangle :g:`EAC` with cevian :g:`EB` cutting transversal :g:`FD` at :g:`G`), given any three independent segment ratios among the five divided lines, to determine the remaining ratios.*

Using signed and combined vertex weights, Ceva works systematically through all **10 combinatorial cases** ($\binom{5}{2} = 10$), exhibiting both positive and negative weight differences (`F - A`, `D - C`) where appropriate.
