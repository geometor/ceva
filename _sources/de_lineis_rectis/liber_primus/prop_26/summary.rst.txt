Proposition XXVI: Concurrent Lines in a Double Pyramid — Summary
================================================================

.. folio:: 0051
   :page: p. 34

*Proposition XXVI* (p. 34, `Tab. 3, Fig. 31`) concludes *Liber Primus* with a 3D **double-pyramid** concurrency theorem: $NK$, $IM$, and $LO$ meet at one point $P$ on the axis $GH$ produced.
