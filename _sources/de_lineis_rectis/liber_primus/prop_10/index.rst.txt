Proposition X & Lemma V: Third Perimeter Ratio (Caravaggio Proof)
=================================================================

   :page: p. 15

.. picture:: 0105.g14.png

**Proposition X · Problem VIII** (pp. 15–17, `Tab. 1, Fig. 5`): *In Element II, given the ratio of :g:`DA` to :g:`AB` and the ratio of :g:`DE` to :g:`BC`, to determine the third base division :g:`CK` to :g:`KE`.*

On p. 15 (folio ``0032``), Ceva again preserves a pure Euclidean proof by :person:`Pietro Paolo Caravaggio Jr. <caravaggio>` demonstrating the Ceva ratio product directly via parallel lines without weights, before continuing with his own static resolution.

**Lemma V** (p. 16, `Tab. 2, Figs. 13–14`): Constructs auxiliary parallel intersections across a triangle to isolate the compound ratio required when mixed non-adjacent side segments are given as data.
