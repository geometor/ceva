Proposition XXV: Concurrent Lines in a Pyramidal Frustum
========================================================

.. folio:: 0050
   :page: p. 33

.. picture:: 0107.g30.svg
   :alt: Calibrated interactive SVG — Ceva 1678 Tabula III Fig. 30: Prop. XXV

.. picture:: 0107.g30.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula III Fig. 30: Truncated triangular pyramid (frustum) concurrency

**Proposition XXV · Theorem XIV** (pp. 33–34, `Tab. 3, Fig. 30`)

*Let there be a pyramid whose vertex is* :g:`M`, *and whose base is triangle* :g:`AEC`; *and let* ✠H *be drawn, cutting* :g:`EM` *at* ✠ *and* :g:`AM` *at* :g:`H`; *from any point* :g:`K` *taken at pleasure on the same, let us join two lines* :g:`KH`, K✠;
*let line* :g:`MF` *cut* H✠ *at* :g:`G`; *likewise let* :g:`MB` *cut* :g:`HK` *at* :g:`I`, *and* :g:`MD` *cut* K✠ *at* :g:`L`; *and with* :g:`FB`, :g:`AD` *joined meeting each other at* :g:`O`, *let straight lines* :g:`HL`, :g:`IG`, :g:`MO` *be drawn. I say that these intersect one another as at* ℞; *and the heavy bodies suspended from the angles will preserve, as in the preceding, that harmony of ratios.*

(`Tab. 3, Fig. 30`) Let it be made as :g:`CB` to :g:`BA`, so weight :g:`P` to :g:`Q`; as :g:`ED` to :g:`DC`, so :g:`Q` to :g:`R`; and as :g:`AF` to :g:`FE`, so :g:`R` to :g:`S`; this posited, point :g:`O` will be the center of gravity of weights :g:`S`, :g:`P`, :g:`Q`, :g:`R`.
Next, as :g:`MK` to :g:`KC`, so let weight :g:`Q` to :g:`V` be made, and as M✠ to ✠E, so :g:`R` to :g:`X`; and similarly point :g:`L` will be the center of gravity of heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`Q`, :g:`V`, :g:`X`;
finally, as :g:`MH` to :g:`HA`, so let weight :g:`S` to :g:`Z` and :g:`P` to :g:`T` be placed, and :g:`G` will likewise be the center of gravity of heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`X`, :g:`Z`, :g:`S`,
:g:`I` of heavy bodies :g:`P`, :g:`T`, :g:`V`, :g:`Q`, and :g:`H` of heavy bodies :g:`S`, :g:`Z`, :g:`T`, :g:`P`; wherefore it is clear that on each of the balance-beams :g:`HL`, :g:`IG`, :g:`MO` exists the center of gravity of all suspended heavy bodies,
which since it is unique, it is necessary

.. folio:: 0051
   :page: p. 34

that they intersect one another at the same point, such as ℞; and of this kind is that center also of the same balance-beams; therefore the proposition is established.
