==================================================================

.. folio:: 0020

**Archival & Mathematical Commentary**:

* Printed pp. 3–4 (folios ``0020``–``0021``), `Tab. 1, Fig. 5`.
* Although rediscovered independently by :person:`Ceva <ceva>` in 1678 via Archimedean statics, the same theorem had been proved six centuries earlier in Zaragoza by :person:`Yūsuf al-Muʾtaman ibn Hūd <mutaman>` (*Kitāb al-Istikmāl*, c. 1080; :cite:`Hogendijk 1995 <hogendijk:1995:mutaman>`).
* Verified universally over polynomial rings in :func:`geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.prove_ceva_barycentric_identity` and constructively in :func:`geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.construct_ceva_concurrency`.
