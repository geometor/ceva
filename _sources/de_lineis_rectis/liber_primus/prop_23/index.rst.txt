Proposition XXIII: Seven Concurrent Lines in a Tetrahedron
==========================================================

**Proposition XXIII · Theorem XII**

*Let there be a pyramid whose vertex is* :g:`G`, *and whose base is triangle* :g:`AEC`; *and with the figure of the Second Element described in triangle* :g:`EGC`, *such as is* :g:`EIGLCDK`, *let us join the two lines* :g:`AI`, :g:`AL`; 
*and any point* :g:`N` *being taken on* :g:`AG`, *let us draw* :g:`NE` *cutting* :g:`AI` *at* :g:`H`, *and* :g:`NC` *cutting* :g:`AL` *at* :g:`M`; *and with lines* :g:`GHF`, :g:`GMB` *drawn moreover, let us understand* :g:`CF`, :g:`AD`, :g:`EB` *to be joined.* 
*I say first that these lines cut one another at the same point* :g:`P`; *moreover, that the lines conceived within the pyramid,* :g:`AK`, :g:`EM`, :g:`CH`, :g:`GP`, :g:`FL`, :g:`IB`, :g:`ND`, *also meet one another simultaneously at the same point* :g:`O`.

*Now that these things may clearly appear, let us as usual suspend from the angles of the same pyramid, in the reciprocal ratio of the lengths, the heavy bodies* :g:`R`, :g:`S`, :g:`Q`, :g:`T`; *this being posited, any three to the remaining one,* 
*or any two to the remaining two, or any two to one, or finally one to one, will be reciprocally among themselves with* :term:`a certain wonderful harmony <concordia-septem-linearum>` *as the lengths from which they hang.*

(`Tab. 3, Fig. 28`) Let it be made as :g:`GI` to :g:`IE`, so weight :g:`S` to :g:`Q`; as :g:`CL` to :g:`LG`, so weight :g:`Q` to :g:`T`; and as :g:`AN` to :g:`NG`, so weight :g:`Q` to :g:`R`; 
and let :g:`R` be at :g:`A`, :g:`S` at :g:`E`, :g:`T` at :g:`C`, and :g:`Q` at :g:`G`; because in the Second Element whose heavy bodies are :g:`S`, :g:`Q`, :g:`T` and center :g:`K`, :g:`GI` is to :g:`IE` as weight :g:`S` to :g:`Q`, 
and :g:`CL` to :g:`LG` as :g:`Q` to :g:`T`, it will be by Problem II also :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as :g:`S` to :g:`T`; and by the same reason in the Second Element whose heavy bodies are :g:`R`, :g:`Q`, :g:`S`, :g:`EF` will be to :g:`FA` as :g:`R` to :g:`S`. 
Likewise in the Second Element whose heavy bodies are :g:`R`, :g:`Q`, :g:`T`, and center :g:`M`, the straight line :g:`AB` will be to :g:`BC` as weight :g:`T` to :g:`R`; 
therefore since :g:`EF` to :g:`FA` is as :g:`R` to :g:`S`, :g:`CD` to :g:`DE` as :g:`S` to :g:`T`, and :g:`AB` to :g:`BC` as :g:`T` to :g:`R`, the lines :g:`EB`, :g:`AD`, :g:`CF` will intersect one another at the same point, such as :g:`P`: 
and out of them together with triangle :g:`AEC` will be formed a Second Element, whose heavy bodies are :g:`R`, :g:`S`, :g:`T`, and center :g:`P`; wherefore on line :g:`GP` will be the center of gravity of all the heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`S`, :g:`T`, :g:`Q`: 
and likewise because :g:`H` is the center of gravity of heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`S`, :g:`Q`, :g:`K` of heavy bodies :g:`S`, :g:`Q`, :g:`T`, and :g:`M` of heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`Q`, :g:`T`, 
the same center of heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`S`, :g:`T`, :g:`Q` will be on

each of the balance-beams :g:`AK`, :g:`EM`, :g:`CH`; but because this center is unique, the lines :g:`GP`, :g:`AK`, :g:`EM`, :g:`CH` will meet one another at the same point :g:`O`, 
namely at the center of gravity of all the heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`S`, :g:`T`, :g:`Q`. Next, because :g:`L` is the center of gravity of heavy bodies :g:`Q`, :g:`T`, and :g:`F` of heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`S`, point :g:`O`, inasmuch as it is the center of gravity of heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`S`, :g:`T`, :g:`Q`, 
will lie on balance-beam :g:`FL`; say the same of balance-beams :g:`IB`, :g:`DN`; therefore all the balance-beams, or lines :g:`GP`, :g:`AK`, :g:`EM`, :g:`CH`, :g:`FL`, :g:`IB`, :g:`DN`, will meet one another at the same point :g:`O`, their common center; 
and hence as :g:`CO` to :g:`OH`, so the three heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`Q`, :g:`S`, that is, weight :g:`H` to :g:`C`; as :g:`EO` to :g:`OM`, so weight :g:`M`, namely the three heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`Q`, :g:`T`, to :g:`S`; 
as :g:`AO` to :g:`OK`, so weight :g:`K`, namely the heavy bodies :g:`S`, :g:`Q`, :g:`T`, to :g:`R`; and as :g:`OG` to :g:`OP`, so weight :g:`P`, or the heavy bodies :g:`S`, :g:`T`, :g:`R`, to :g:`Q`; 
likewise as weight :g:`L` to :g:`F`, that is, the heavy bodies :g:`Q`, :g:`T` to :g:`R`, :g:`S`, so :g:`FO` to :g:`OL`; as weight :g:`N` to :g:`D`, namely the heavy bodies :g:`R`, :g:`Q` to :g:`S`, :g:`T`, so :g:`DO` to :g:`ON`; 
and as weight :g:`I` to :g:`B`, namely the heavy bodies :g:`Q`, :g:`S` to :g:`R`, :g:`T`, so :g:`BO` to :g:`OI`. And the remaining ratios become known from the Second Elements whose centers are :g:`H`, :g:`M`, :g:`K`, :g:`P`, and therefore the whole proposition is manifest.
