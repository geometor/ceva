Proposition XXII: Concurrency and Coplanarity in Skew Quadrilaterals — Summary
==============================================================================

.. folio:: 0047
   :page: p. 30

*Proposition XXII* (pp. 30–31, `Tab. 3, Figs. 25–27`) bridges planar statics and 3D solid geometry by proving spatial concurrency and coplanarity for skew quadrilaterals with proportional side divisions.
