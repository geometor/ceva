**Proposition XXII · Theorem XI**

*Let two lines* :g:`AB`, :g:`BC` *containing the angle* :g:`B` *cut two other lines* :g:`AD`, :g:`DC` *containing the angle* :g:`D` *— cut them, I say, at the points* :g:`A`, :g:`C` *— and let the lines* :g:`CB`, :g:`BA`, :g:`AD`, :g:`DC` *be so divided at the points* :g:`F`, :g:`G`, :g:`H`, :g:`E` 
*that the ratio* :g:`DE` *to* :g:`EC` *is compounded of the ratios* :g:`BF` *to* :g:`FC`, :g:`AG` *to* :g:`GB`, *and* :g:`DH` *to* :g:`HA`. *I say that the joined lines* :g:`HF`, :g:`GE` *intersect each other, say at* :g:`I`, *and therefore exist in one plane:* 
*for this to be established, it will be necessary to suspend from the points* :g:`A`, :g:`B`, :g:`C`, :g:`D` *the heavy bodies* :g:`L`, :g:`M`, :g:`N`, :g:`K`, 
*so that* :g:`GI` *to* :g:`IE` *be as the two heavy bodies* :g:`KN` *to the two* :g:`LM`; *and* :g:`HI` *to* :g:`IF` *as the two* :g:`MN` *to the two* :g:`LK`, *which we shall perform thus.*

(`Tab. 3, Figs. 25–27`) Let it be made as :g:`BF` to :g:`FC`, so heavy body :g:`N` to :g:`M`; 
as :g:`AG` to :g:`GB`, so :g:`M` to :g:`L`; and as :g:`DH` to :g:`HA`, so :g:`L` to :g:`K`. Weight :g:`N` to :g:`K` is compounded of the ratios of the heavy bodies :g:`N` to :g:`M` to :g:`L` to :g:`K`, 
namely of the ratios of the straight lines :g:`BF` to :g:`FC`, :g:`AG` to :g:`GB`, and :g:`DH` to :g:`HA`; but of the same is compounded :g:`DE` to :g:`EC`; therefore as :g:`DE` to :g:`EC`, so :g:`N` to :g:`K`. Since therefore :g:`E` is the center of balance-beam :g:`CD`, that is, of heavy bodies :g:`N`, :g:`K`, 
and :g:`G` the center of heavy bodies :g:`LM`, the center of all the heavy bodies :g:`KN`, :g:`LM` will lie on balance-beam :g:`GE`; and by the same reason, since :g:`H` is the center of heavy bodies :g:`LK`, and :g:`F` is the center of heavy bodies :g:`MN`, 
the center of all the heavy bodies :g:`LK`, :g:`MN` in the same former position will likewise lie on balance-beam :g:`HF`; and since this center is unique,

it is necessary that the balance-beams, or lines :g:`GE`, :g:`HF`, intersect each other, as at :g:`I`, 
and this will be the center of both balance-beams, and therefore as :g:`HI` to :g:`IF`, so the two heavy bodies :g:`MN` to the two :g:`LK`; similarly as the straight line :g:`GI` to :g:`IE`, so the two heavy bodies :g:`KN` to the two heavy bodies :g:`LK`, which &c.
