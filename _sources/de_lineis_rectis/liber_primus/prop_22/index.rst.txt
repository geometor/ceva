**Proposition XXII · Theorem XI** (pp. 30–31, `Tab. 3, Figs. 25–27`)

*Opening the 3D spatial climax of Book I (`Tabula III`), Ceva proves that whenever four sides of a planar or skew quadrilateral are divided by two pairs of proportional weights at the vertices, the segments :g:`HF` and :g:`GE` joining opposite division points necessarily intersect in space and lie in a common plane, with their intersection dividing each join inversely as the paired side-weights.*
