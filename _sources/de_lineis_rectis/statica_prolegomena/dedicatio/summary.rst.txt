Dedication to Duke Ferdinando Carlo — Summary
=============================================

.. folio:: 0012
   :page: Dedicatio [f. ✠2r]

In his dedicatory epistle (folios ``0012``–``0014``, signed ``✠2``), :person:`Giovanni Ceva <ceva>` places *De Lineis Rectis* under the patronage of :person:`Duke Ferdinando Carlo Gonzaga <gonzaga>` of Mantua, foreshadowing Ceva's later lifelong appointment (from 1686) as ducal mathematician and *Commissario dell'Arciducale Camera* in Mantua.
