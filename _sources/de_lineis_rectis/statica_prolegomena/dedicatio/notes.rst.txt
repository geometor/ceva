Dedication to Duke Ferdinando Carlo — Notes
===========================================

.. folio:: 0012
   :page: Dedicatio [f. ✠2r]

**Typographical & Historical Notes**:

* Folio ``0012`` opens with a baroque woodcut headpiece depicting two winged putti flanking an urn of flowers and an ornamental historiated drop-capital ``T`` (*TImerem*).
* Notice the gathering signature at the foot of folio ``0012``: ``✠ 2``, using the same distinctive patonce-cross sort ``✠`` that Monza later pressed into service as an algebraic plus sign in Book I Prop. IV (p. 5) and explained in the Printer's Note on p. 83 (folio ``0100``).
* Folio ``0014`` closes with Ceva's formal subscription (*Humillimus Seruus Ioannes Ceua*).
