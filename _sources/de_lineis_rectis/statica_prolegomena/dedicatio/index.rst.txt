Dedication to Duke Ferdinando Carlo
===================================

.. folio:: 0012
   :page: Dedicatio [f. ✠2r]

.. picture:: 0012.g1.png
   :alt: 1678 woodcut headpiece with winged putti and floral urn atop Ceva's Dedication

**To the Most Serene Duke of Mantua,** :person:`Ferdinando Carlo <gonzaga>`

I should fear, Most Serene Duke, to prefix so illustrious a name to this slender volume, were it not a universal right to shelter oneself and one's work under the protection of Princes.

For God, Father and Governor of all lights, willed these Most Serene Suns to shine upon all, that they might cast their beneficent radiance far across the common throng of

.. folio:: 0013
   :page: Dedicatio [f. ✠2v]

mortals. Now if it is lawful for anyone to entrust his goods and fortunes to so great a patronage,
how much more justly do defenseless letters claim this same privilege for themselves, especially Geometry, which, if it lacks the shelter of Maecenases, lacks all vigor and life. For polite literature and almost all the other sciences have their theaters, porticoes, and champions;
few indeed stand by Geometry's side, few keep watch for the Queen of all the sciences. Her illustrious birth avails her nothing, her dignity nothing, her benefits conferred upon mortals nothing. Her therefore I set as a suppliant before you, revered Prince,
that from this Most Serene summit of Your Highness she may claim for herself patronage and protection. Meanwhile, bravest Hero, may the Powers above preserve you safe and sound for us and for the good arts for a long time. For

.. folio:: 0014
   :page: Dedicatio [f. ✠3r]

there is no one who does not look up with wonder at your exalted character and the keenness of your great mind, this strength of soul, clemency, and the grace of your august countenance,
and who does not judge that in these is placed the greatest hope of public felicity. Farewell.

Your Most Serene Highness's

Most Humble Servant, :person:`Giovanni Ceva <ceva>`.
