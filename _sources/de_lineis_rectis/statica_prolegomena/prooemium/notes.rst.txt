Preface to the Reader — Notes
=============================

.. folio:: 0015
   :page: Prooemium [f. ✠3v]

**Intellectual Context & Sources**:

* **Classical & Galilean Lineage**: Ceva explicitly situates himself between antiquity (:person:`Archimedes <archimedes>`, :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>`, :person:`Pappus <pappus>`) and the Italian school of indivisibles (:person:`Bonaventura Cavalieri <cavalieri>`).
* **Donato Rossetti (1633–1686)**: A Livornese physicist, professor at Pisa, and later mathematician at the Savoy court in Turin; Ceva studied at the University of Pisa under Rossetti's circle during the 1670s.
* **Bibliographic Correction**: Secondary accounts sometimes place Ludovico Monza's Printer's Note on the plus glyph ``✠`` on scan ``0017``; direct visual audit confirms ``0017`` is the closing page of the *Prooemium* (*Vale.*), while the actual Printer's Note appears beneath the *Errata* table on **p. 83 (folio ``0100``)**.
