Preface to the Reader — Summary
===============================

.. folio:: 0015
   :page: Prooemium [f. ✠3v]

In the *Prooemium* (folios ``0015``–``0017``), :person:`Ceva <ceva>` narrates how failed youthful attempts to square the circle and hyperbola led him to his breakthrough method: replacing pure geometric segments with balanced Archimedean weights (*substitutisque linearum vice ponderibus*). He names his approach *Statica Constructio* and credits :person:`Donato Rossetti <rossetti>` for urging its publication.
