Preface to the Reader
=====================

.. folio:: 0015
   :page: Prooemium [f. ✠3v]

.. picture:: 0015.g1.png
   :alt: 1678 arabesque floral headpiece opening Ceva's Prooemium

Considering often amid the vicissitudes of human fortune how welcome a solace philosophy provides, I turned in youth to geometry—which surpasses other arts in both variety and nobility. Fired by the marvelous discoveries of :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>`, :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>`, and :person:`Pappus <pappus>`, I set sail hoping to discover unknown shores: attempting the quadrature of the circle and the still-untamed hyperbola.

When those ancient problems proved as relentless as the stone of Sisyphus, and even the :person:`indivisibles of Cavalieri <cavalieri>` (:cite:`1635 <cavalieri:1635:indivis>`) demanded extreme care, a simpler path opened before me: **substituting weights in place of straight lines** (*substitutisque linearum vice ponderibus*), and determining the ratios of intersecting segments directly from the uniqueness of their :term:`center of gravity <centrum-gravitatis>`.

Hence it pleased me to title this method a :term:`static construction <statica-constructio>` (*staticam constructionem*). I warmly acknowledge the encouragement of my friend :person:`Donato Rossetti <rossetti>`, who urged these investigations into print. Farewell, gentle reader.
