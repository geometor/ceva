Prolegomena — Archival & Historical Notes
=========================================

.. folio:: 0010
   :page: Title Page

**Primary Source Witness**: Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Firenze, Fondo Magliabechiano ``CFMAGL. 1.7.137`` (Internet Archive ``ita-bnc-mag-00001346-001``; 126 digitized scans).

* Multimodal visual inspection of scans ``0001``–``0017`` confirms the exact pagination formula ``printed_page = folio_number - 17`` beginning at folio ``0018`` (p. 1) and continuing unbroken to folio ``0100`` (p. 83).
* Ludovico Monza's famous note explaining the typographic substitution of ``✠`` for algebraic ``+`` appears at the end of the volume beneath the Errata table on **p. 83 (folio ``0100``)**, while Ceva's in-text introduction of ``A ✠ F`` occurs on **p. 5 (folio ``0022``)**.
