**On Straight Lines Mutually Intersecting: A Static Construction**

Dedicated to the Most Serene **Ferdinando Carlo**, Duke of Mantua, Monferrato, Guastalla, etc.

By :person:`Giovanni Ceva <ceva>` of Milan.

**Milan**, from the Printing House of :person:`Ludovico Monza <monza>`, **1678**. With the Permission of Superiors.

The Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Firenze (Magliabechiano shelfmark ``CFMAGL. 1.7.137``; handwritten spine label ``XI CEV.``) preserves the complete Milan *editio princeps* across 126 digitized scans: contemporary pasteboard binding and flyleaves (scans ``0001``–``0009``), title leaf with woodcut rose and Florentine lily library stamp (``0010``–``0011``), dedication and preface (``0012``–``0017``), 83 numbered text pages (``0018``–``0100``), *Imprimatur* and Monza colophon (``0101``–``0102``), and ten folding copperplate plates containing 85 engraved geometric diagrams (``0103``–``0122``) followed by blank endpapers (``0123``–``0126``).
