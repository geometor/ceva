Unit II · Liber Primus Props. VI–XXII (Textus Latinus, 1678, pp. 7–35)
======================================================================

*Folio-verified Neo-Latin primary text from Giovanni Ceva's De lineis rectis se invicem secantibus statica constructio* (:cite:`Mediolani: Ex Typographia Ludovici Montiae, 1678 <ceva:1678:dlr>`, *Liber Primus*, Props. VI–XXII, pp. 7–35; `PDF pp. 24–52`, `Tab. 1–3, Figs. 9–27`).

.. rst-class:: latin-source

1. Propositio VI — Demonstratio Geometrica Petri Pauli Caravaggii (pp. 7–8)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. rst-class:: latin-source

**Propositio VI. Theorema** (`Tab. 1, Fig. 10`): *Eadem, quæ supra staticè demonstravimus de tribus rectis in uno puncto concurrentibus, geometricè ac purè per lineas parallelas ostendere.*

Ne quis autem existimet veritates hasce geometricas à solis principiis mechanicis seu staticis pendere, libet hoc loco subtexere perelegantem demonstrationem purè geometricam, quam mecum humanissimè communicavit ingeniosissimus adolescens **Petrus Paulus Carauaggius Petri Pauli filius præceptoris sui** (:person:`Pietro Paolo Caravaggio Jr. <caravaggio>`).

Per verticem :g:`A` trianguli :g:`ABC` ducatur recta ipsi basi $BC$ parallela, cui occurrant productæ ex reliquis angulis secantes $CF$, $BE$ in punctis $K$ et $L$. Ob similitudinem triangulorum (:person:`Euclides <euclid>`, :just:`VI.4`), est $AF$ ad $FB$ ut $AK$ ad $BC$; itemque $CE$ ad $EA$ ut $BC$ ad $AL$; ac denique, transeunte tertia recta $AD$ per commune concursus punctum $P$, est $BD$ ad $DC$ ut $AL$ ad $AK$. Quare ex æquo perturbato, :term:`ratio composita <ratio-composita>` ex $AF$ ad $FB$, $BD$ ad $DC$, et $CE$ ad $EA$ eadem est cum ratione composita ex $AK$ ad $BC$, $BC$ ad $AL$, et $AL$ ad $AK$—hoc est, cum ratione æqualitatis!

2. Lemma IV & Propositiones VII–VIII — Ratio Composita & Theorema Menelai (pp. 8–12)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. rst-class:: latin-source

* **Lemma IV** (ex :person:`Commandino <commandino>` & :person:`Clavio <clavius>`, :cite:`1589 <clavius:1589:euclides>`): Si fuerint quotlibet magnitudines, ratio primæ ad ultimam componitur ex rationibus primæ ad secundam, secundæ ad tertiam, ac deinceps usque ad ultimam.
* **Propositio VII. Theorema** (`Tab. 1, Fig. 11`): In omni triangulo :g:`ABC`, si tres rectæ :g:`AD`, :g:`BE`, :g:`CF` ex angulis ad opposita latera (sive intra, sive extra producta) ductæ in unum punctum :g:`P` concurrant, ratio composita ex rationibus segmentorum $(AF : FB)$, $(BD : DC)$, $(CE : EA)$ est ratio æqualitatis; **et conversim**, si prædicta ratio composita fuerit æqualitatis, tres rectæ :g:`AD`, :g:`BE`, :g:`CF` aut in unum punctum :g:`P` concurrent, aut inter se æquidistantes erunt.
* **Propositio VIII. Theorema** (`Tab. 1, Fig. 12`, Theorema :person:`Menelai <menelaus>` ac :person:`Ptolemæi <ptolemy>`): Si per puncta :g:`E` et :g:`F` in duobus lateribus trianguli :g:`ABC` ducatur recta transversalis :g:`EF` occurrens tertio lateri :g:`lineBC` producto extra in :g:`D' <D_prime>`, appensis in :g:`A`, :g:`B`, :g:`C` ponderibus staticis manifestum fit rationem compositam ex $(AF : FB)$, $(BD' : D'C)$ et $(CE : EA)$ esse rationem æqualitatis—unde simul fluit :term:`sectio harmonica <sectio-harmonica>` basim :g:`lineBC` in punctis :g:`B`, :g:`D`, :g:`C`, :g:`D' <D_prime>` harmonicè dividi.

3. Propositiones IX–XXII — Concursus Linearum Notabilium Trianguli (pp. 12–35)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

.. rst-class:: latin-source

Ex hisce universalibus fontibus sponte promanant innumerabiles propemodum linearum concurrentium proprietates (`Tab. 1–3, Figs. 13–27`):

1. Si latera trianguli :g:`ABC` bifariam secentur in :g:`D`, :g:`E`, :g:`F`, positis in :g:`A`, :g:`B`, :g:`C` ponderibus æqualibus ($1 : 1 : 1$), tres medianæ concurrunt in ipso trianguli centro gravitatis :g:`X(2) <X2>`.
2. Si ex angulis demittantur perpendiculares ad latera opposita, segmenta reciproca tangentibus angulorum exhibent concursum altitudinum in uno puncto :g:`X(4) <X4>`.
3. Si anguli trianguli :g:`ABC` bifariam secentur (:just:`VI.3`), segmenta cujusque lateris proportionalia sunt reliquis duobus lateribus adjacentibus ($a : b : c$), unde tres bisecantes in centro circuli inscripti :g:`X(1) <X1>` necessario conveniunt; similiterque bisecantes duorum angulorum exteriorum cum bisecante tertii interioris ex pondere analytico ``F - A`` extra concurrunt.
