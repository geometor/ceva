Tommaso Ceva, S.J.
==================

Younger brother of Giovanni Ceva, celebrated Jesuit mathematician, humanist, and poet; long-serving professor of mathematics at the Jesuit Brera College in Milan, inventor of the mathematical trisection instrument (pantograph-like gravity compass), and author of *Opuscula mathematica* (1699).
