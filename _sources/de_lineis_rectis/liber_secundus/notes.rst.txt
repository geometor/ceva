Book II — Scholarly & Symbolic Notes
====================================

.. folio:: 0052
   :page: p. 35

**Archival Provenance**: Printed pp. 35–60 (folios ``0052``–``0077``), illustrated across `Tabulae IV–VIII` (`0109`–`0117`, Figs. 32–67) and `Tabula X` (`0121`, Fig. 85 for the Prop. XXI Scholium rangefinder).

* **Errata Applied**: Errata p. 83 corrects p. 51 (`Prop. XVI`) from ``tab. 1. fig. 5`` to ``tab. 7. fig. 57``, plus textual corrections on pp. 41, 42, 43, 44, and 46.
* **Exact Symbolic Realization**: Circumscribed-polygon invariance, isotomic Gergonne/Nagel conjugation ($X(7) \leftrightarrow X(8)$), and multilateral odd-$(2m+1)$-gon extensions are implemented without floats in :mod:`geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics` and :mod:`geometor.ceva.polygon_tetrahedron`.
