Proposition XIX: Fifteen Cases of Reciprocally Inscribed Triangles — Summary
============================================================================

.. folio:: 0071
   :page: p. 54

*Book II, Proposition XIX* (pp. 54–57, `Tab. 8, Figs. 63–66`) exhaustively solves 15 inverse ratio cases for **two reciprocally inscribed triangles**, showcasing the compositional power of Ceva's Elements on multi-triangle networks.
