**Proposition XIX · Problem III** (pp. 54–57, `Tab. 8, Figs. 63–66`)

*Given two triangles reciprocally inscribed or intersecting so that vertices of one lie upon the sides of the other (forming a hexagrammatic/star configuration of intersecting cevians), and given any pair of independent ratios among the divided sides, to determine all remaining ratios.*

Across four dense pages (pp. 54–57), Ceva systematically resolves **15 combinatorial cases** by decomposing the intertwined triangles into coupled instances of Elements I–III.
