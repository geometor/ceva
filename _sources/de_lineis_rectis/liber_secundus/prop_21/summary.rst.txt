Proposition XXI & Scholium: Optical Rangefinder & End of Static Construction — Summary
======================================================================================

.. folio:: 0076
   :page: p. 59

Closing *Liber Secundus* (pp. 59–60, folios ``0076``–``0077``), *Prop. XXI* and its *Scholium* introduce **Ceva's optical rangefinder** (`Tab. 10, Fig. 85`), followed by Ceva's celebratory epilogue (*51 ratios from any 2!*), *FINIS CONSTRUCTIONIS STATICÆ*, and his candid preface explaining that the theorems of the *Appendix Geometrica* were discovered *"while trying in vain to square the circle."*
