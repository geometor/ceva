Proposition I: Inscribed Conic Concurrency & Converse of Ceva's Theorem — Notes
===============================================================================

.. folio:: 0055
   :page: p. 38

**Archival & Mathematical Notes**:

* Printed p. 38 (folio ``0055``), `Tab. 4, Fig. 38` (final figure of Tabula IV).
* Notice the subtle historical gem revealed by direct scan inspection: while Book I Prop. II proved the *forward* direction (concurrent cevians $\implies$ ratio product $= 1$), Ceva states the explicit **Converse of Ceva's Theorem** in the **Corollary to Book II Prop. I** on p. 38 via *reductio ad absurdum* on point $H \neq B$!
* Together with isotomic conjugation (:term:`coniugatum isotomicum <coniugatum-isotomicum>`, linking Gergonne $X(7)$ to Nagel $X(8)$), this proposition is verified symbolically in :func:`geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.construct_isotomic_conjugate`.
