Proposition VI: Parallelism via Midpoints and Exterior Vertex Line
==================================================================

.. folio:: 0058
   :page: p. 41

.. picture:: 0111.g42.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula V Fig. 42: Midpoints of triangle sides and parallel segments BD || HG

**Proposition VI · Theorem VI** (pp. 41–43, `Tab. 5, Fig. 42`)

*In a triangle whose side midpoints are joined to form medial chords, let an exterior line through one vertex cut the opposite chords at :g:`I` and :g:`K`. Then the segment :g:`HG` joining the intersections stands strictly parallel to the side :g:`BD` (`BD || HG`).*
