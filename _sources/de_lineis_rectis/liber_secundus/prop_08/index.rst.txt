Proposition VIII: Segment Ratio on a Cevian Intersecting a Chord
================================================================

.. folio:: 0061
   :page: p. 44

.. picture:: 0111.g46.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula V Fig. 46: Cevian segment ratio cut by side chord

**Proposition VIII · Theorem VIII** (p. 44, `Tab. 5, Fig. 46`)

*If, as above, three sides of a triangle be divided; and two points of division be joined by one straight line, which cuts another line drawn from an angle of the same triangle, yet not from that to which the joined line is subtended,* 
*so will that line be divided by this one that its segments are compounded of two ratios, one of which is from the parts of the untouched side,* 
*while the other consists of the portions of that line which is the segment of the other side intercepted between the divided line and the angle to which the remaining side of the triangle is subtended.*

Let there be triangle :g:`ACF`, 
whose three sides :g:`AF`, :g:`FC`, :g:`CA` are divided in order at :g:`G`, :g:`D`, :g:`B`, so that the ratio :g:`AG` to :g:`GF` is compounded of the two ratios :g:`CD` to :g:`DF`, and :g:`AB` to :g:`BC`, and let :g:`GD` joined cut :g:`AE` drawn at :g:`H`; 
I say that :g:`EH` to :g:`HA` is compounded of the ratios :g:`CB` to :g:`BA`, and :g:`ED` to :g:`DC`. For in the figure of the first element, whose weights are :g:`A`, :g:`F`, :g:`D`, and center :g:`H`, 
the ratio of straight line :g:`EH` to :g:`HA`, namely of weight :g:`A` to :g:`E`, is compounded of the ratios of weights :g:`A` to :g:`F` to :g:`E`, namely of straight lines :g:`FG` to :g:`GA`, and :g:`DE` to :g:`DF`; 
but the ratio of straight line :g:`FG` to :g:`GA` is compounded (by supposition) of the ratios :g:`CB` to :g:`BA`, and :g:`FD` to :g:`DC`, therefore the aforesaid ratio :g:`EH` to :g:`HA` will be compounded of the ratios :g:`CB` to :g:`BA`, :g:`FD` to :g:`DC`, and :g:`DE` to :g:`DF`, 
or of the same taken in perturbed order :g:`CB` to :g:`BA`, :g:`DE` to :g:`DF` to :g:`DC`; that is, of the proposed ratios :g:`CB` to :g:`BA`, and :g:`ED` to :g:`DC`, which &c.
