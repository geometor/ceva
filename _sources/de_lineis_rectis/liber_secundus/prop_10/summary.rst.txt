Proposition X: Squared Segment Ratios on Three Parabola Tangents — Summary
==========================================================================

.. folio:: 0064
   :page: p. 47

*Book II, Proposition X* (p. 47, `Tab. 6, Fig. 50`) combines :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` (*Conics* III.41) with static ratios to prove the squared segment law on three tangents to a parabola.
