Proposition XVI & Lemma VI: Concurrency of Angle Bisectors (Incenter) — Summary
===============================================================================

.. folio:: 0068
   :page: p. 51

*Book II, Proposition XVI* (pp. 51–52, `Tab. 7, Fig. 57`) gives the classic three-line Ceva proof that the **angle bisectors of every triangle concur at the Incenter** $X(1)$, followed by *Lemma VI* (`Tab. 7, Figs. 58, 60, 61`) on common tangents to two circles.
