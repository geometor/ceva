**Proposition IX · Problem I** (pp. 44–45, `Tab. 5, Fig. 44`): *Given a triangle :g:`BAC` and two chosen points of tangency :g:`F` on :g:`BA` and :g:`E` on :g:`BC`, to construct the unique ellipse (or circle) inscribed in $\triangle BAC$ tangent at :g:`F` and :g:`E`.*

By *Book II Prop. I*, the third point of tangency :g:`D` on base :g:`AC` is determined immediately as the foot of the cevian :g:`BD` passing through the intersection :g:`G` of :g:`CF` and :g:`AE`! Once all three contact points :g:`D`, :g:`E`, :g:`F` are fixed, Ceva locates the center and conjugate diameters of the unique inscribed ellipse via parallel chords and midline intersections.

**Lemma IV** (p. 46, `Tab. 6, Fig. 47`) & **Lemma V** (p. 46, `Tab. 6, Figs. 48–49`): Preparatory conic-diameter lemmas for determining the center and tangency ratios of circles, ellipses, and parabolas used in Propositions X–XV.
