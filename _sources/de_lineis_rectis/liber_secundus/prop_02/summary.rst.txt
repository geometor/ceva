Proposition II: Quadrilateral Circumscribed About a Circle or Ellipse — Summary
===============================================================================

.. folio:: 0056
   :page: p. 39

*Book II, Proposition II* (p. 39) extends *Lemma I* to circumscribed quadrilaterals (circle or ellipse), proving that opposite tangency chords instantiate *Elementum V*.
