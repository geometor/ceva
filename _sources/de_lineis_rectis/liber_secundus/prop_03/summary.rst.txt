Proposition III: Proportional Side Divisions and Bisected Base — Summary
========================================================================

.. folio:: 0056
   :page: p. 39

*Book II, Proposition III* (p. 39, `Tab. 5, Fig. 39`) proves the classic trapezoid-median concurrency theorem: a parallel chord across two sides of a triangle generates crossed cevians intersecting right upon the median.
