Proposition III: Proportional Side Divisions and Bisected Base
==============================================================

.. folio:: 0056
   :page: p. 39

.. picture:: 0111.g39.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula V Fig. 39: Triangle with proportionally divided sides and bisected base

**Proposition III · Theorem III** (p. 39, `Tab. 5, Fig. 39`)

*In triangle :g:`CAE`, if two sides :g:`CA` and :g:`CE` are divided proportionally (so the joining chord :g:`GD` is parallel to base :g:`AE`) and the base :g:`AE` is bisected at its midpoint :g:`H`, then the median :g:`CH` and the two crossed cevians :g:`AD`, :g:`EG` all concur at one interior point :g:`F`.*
