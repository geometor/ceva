Proposition XIV: Concurrency on Three Tangents to a Circle or Ellipse — Summary
===============================================================================

.. folio:: 0066
   :page: p. 49

*Book II, Proposition XIV* (pp. 49–51, `Tab. 6–7, Figs. 53–56`) establishes concurrency for triangles with an externally tangent circle or ellipse (excircle / ex-conic), extending Book II Prop. I to exterior tangency configurations.
