**Proposition XIV · Theorem XII** (pp. 49–51, `Tab. 6, Figs. 53–54` & `Tab. 7, Figs. 55–56`)

*Extending the three-tangent concurrency of Proposition I to external/exscribed configurations where a circle or ellipse is tangent to one side of a triangle and the extensions of the other two sides (the excircle / ex-ellipse configuration), Ceva proves that the three lines joining vertices to opposite tangency points still concur at one point (yielding the **Nagel point** :g:`X(8)` and its conic generalizations), followed by a general **Corollary** on p. 51.*
