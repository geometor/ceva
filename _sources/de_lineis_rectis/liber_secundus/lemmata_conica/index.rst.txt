**Book II — Conic Sections, Reciprocal Triangles & Optical Mensuration**

**Lemma I** (pp. 35–36, `Tab. 4, Figs. 32–35`): *If any polygon (triangle, quadrilateral, etc.) is circumscribed about a circle or an ellipse, the product of alternating tangent segments equals the product of the complementary tangent segments.*

For a circle, equal tangents from each external vertex cancel pairwise identically; for an ellipse, Ceva invokes **parallel cylindrical projection** (:term:`sectio cylindrica <sectio-cylindrica>`, `Fig. 35`, citing :person:`Giovanni Alfonso Borelli's <borelli>` *Euclides Restitutus* / :cite:`Apollonius Conics <borelli:1661:apollonius>`): projecting a circle :g:`HIK` along the parallel generators of a cylinder onto an oblique elliptical section :g:`ABC` preserves every affine collinearity ratio along tangent lines!

**Lemma II** (pp. 36–37, `Tab. 4, Fig. 36`): *Given any ellipse, to construct a right circular cylinder whose oblique planar section is congruent to that ellipse*, citing :person:`Serenus of Antinoöpolis <serenus>` (:cite:`De Sectione Cylindri <serenus:cylindri>`).

**Lemma III** (p. 37, `Tab. 4, Fig. 37`): *Two ellipses sharing equal conjugate diameters enclosing equal angles coincide identically*, citing :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` (*Conics* I.15).
