Lemmas I–III: Polygons Circumscribed About Circles and Ellipses — Summary
=========================================================================

.. folio:: 0052
   :page: p. 35

Opening *Liber Secundus* (pp. 35–37, `Tab. 4, Figs. 32–37`), Ceva establishes the affine invariance of circumscribed polygons under oblique cylindrical projection (*Lemma I*, citing :person:`Borelli <borelli>`), constructs the generating right cylinder for any ellipse (*Lemma II*, citing :person:`Serenus <serenus>`), and verifies congruence via conjugate diameters (*Lemma III*, citing :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` I.15).
