Proposition XX: Parallel Segments Cut by a Transversal
======================================================

.. folio:: 0075
   :page: p. 58

.. picture:: 0117.g67.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula VIII Fig. 67: Parallel segments DC and EK cut by transversal CIB

**Proposition XX · Theorem XVII** (p. 58, `Tab. 8, Fig. 67`)

*Let two parallel segments :g:`DC` and :g:`EK` be joined across a baseline :g:`DE` with interior point :g:`B` on :g:`DE`, and let the sighting ray :g:`CIB` intersect the diagonal :g:`DK` at :g:`I`. Then the ratio of the baseline staff :g:`DC` to the distant unknown span :g:`EK` is compounded directly from the two measurable internal ratios $\overline{BD} : \overline{DE}$ and $\overline{IC} : \overline{BI}$!*

**Corollary**: Immediately converts this compound ratio into a numerical ruler computation when :g:`DE` and :g:`BC` are graduated with linear divisions.
