Proposition IV: Hippocrates' Lune on an Isosceles Right Triangle — Summary
==========================================================================

.. folio:: 0082
   :page: p. 65

*Appendix Prop. IV* (pp. 65–66, `Tab. 8, Fig. 71`) proves the classic quadrature of :person:`Hippocrates's <hippocrates>` Lune: the circular lune :math:`D` equals the inscribed isosceles right triangle :math:`\triangle ABC` identically.
