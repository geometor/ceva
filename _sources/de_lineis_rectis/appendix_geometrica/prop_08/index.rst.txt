Proposition VIII: Ratio of Oblique Cylindrical Hooves
=====================================================

.. folio:: 0087
   :page: p. 70

.. picture:: 0119.g75.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula IX Fig. 75: Three oblique planes cutting a semicylinder along ED

**Proposition VIII · Theorem VIII** (pp. 70–71, `Tab. 9, Fig. 75`)

*If through the same straight line lying in the plane of the parallelogram which is the base of a semicylinder three planes cutting the same semicylinder be extended, so that the sections become semi-ellipses or semicircles:* 
*the same proportion that the lines joining the vertices of the said semi-ellipses or semicircles enclosing the cylindrical portions have, the portions themselves will obtain between one another: and the said joining lines will lie on the same side of the cylinder.*

Let there be a semicylinder whose bases are semicircles :g:`ABC`, :g:`IGH` (margin: *Tab. 9, Fig. 75*); and through straight line :g:`ED` lying in parallelogram :g:`AH` let three planes be drawn, whose sections are semi-ellipses or circles :g:`EBD`, :g:`EMD`, :g:`EGD`; 
then (since the centers of the bases are :g:`K`, :g:`L`) let :g:`KL` be joined, which will be the axis of the cylinder; next from point :g:`L` having drawn perpendicular :g:`LB`, namely the conjugate semidiameter of :g:`AC`, let a plane pass through this and axis :g:`LK` 
cutting the semicylinder, so that it makes section :g:`LBGK` a parallelogram, and cuts the planes of the semi-ellipses or semicircles so that the common sections are :g:`FB`, :g:`FM`, :g:`FG`; points :g:`B`, :g:`M`, :g:`G` will be (as I shall show) the vertices of the said conic semi-sections. 
I say that the portion of the cylinder enclosed by the two semi-sections :g:`EBD`, :g:`EGD` is to the portion contained by the two :g:`EBD`, :g:`EMD` as line :g:`BG` is to :g:`BM`. Let us conceive a plane drawn parallel to axial parallelogram :g:`AH`, so that it cuts the cylinder, 
and the section of this cutting plane and parallelogram :g:`LG`, namely line :g:`ON`, will be parallel to axis :g:`LK`, and therefore also to the sides of the cylinder which are together in the cutting plane, whence it comes about that all lines drawn within 
these three parallels, namely :g:`ST`, :g:`XY`, :g:`VZ`, :g:`PQR`, are similarly divided by line :g:`ON` (for they are the common sections of the said cutting plane and of the said semi-sections) and since the said cutting plane is parallel to parallelogram :g:`AH`, lines :g:`XY`, :g:`VZ`, :g:`PR` will be parallel 
to the same :g:`ED`, while line :g:`ST` is parallel to :g:`AC`; and therefore just as :g:`TS` is bisected at :g:`N` by :g:`BL` itself, or rather by :g:`NO`, so too line :g:`PR` will be bisected at :g:`Q` by the same :g:`NO`, that is by :g:`FG` itself; 
say the same of the remaining lines :g:`XY`, :g:`PR`, wherefore it is evident that lines :g:`BF`, :g:`MF`, :g:`GF` are conjugate semidiameters to the diameter :g:`ED`

   :page: p. 71

common to semi-ellipses or semicircles :g:`EBD`, :g:`EMD`, :g:`EGD`; and therefore it is clear that points :g:`B`, :g:`M`, :g:`G` are the vertices of those same semi-sections. Therefore (to show at length what was proposed) since portion :g:`EGDB` of the cylinder is to portion :g:`EMDB` as the 
prism circumscribed about the former portion, whose base is equal to triangle :g:`BFG`, is to another prism circumscribed about the other portion having as its base a triangle equal to :g:`BFM`; and both prisms have a common altitude, namely twice the perpendicular which would be 
drawn from point :g:`E` to the plane of parallelogram :g:`LG`; the inscribed portions will be to each other as triangle :g:`BFG` is to triangle :g:`BFM`, indeed as straight line :g:`GB` is to :g:`BM`, Q.E.D.
