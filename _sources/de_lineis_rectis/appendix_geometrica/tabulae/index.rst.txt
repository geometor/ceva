**Ten Folding Copperplate Plates (Tab. I–X, Figs. 1–85)**

Ten folding copperplate plates bound at the end of the volume contain all eighty-five geometric figures of the entire work (`Tab. I`: 1–12; `Tab. II`: 13–24; `Tab. III`: 25–31; `Tab. IIII`: 32–38; `Tab. V`: 39–46; `Tab. VI`: 47–54; `Tab. VII`: 55–61; `Tab. VIII`: 62–73; `Tab. IX`: 74–77; `Tab. X`: 78–85), followed by blank leaves and the back cover:

* **Tabula I** (folio ``0103``, `Figs. 1–12`): Book I :ref:`Axioms & Lemmas I–II <liber-primus-axiomata-lemmata>` through :ref:`Prop. VIII <liber-primus-prop-08>`.
* **Tabula II** (folio ``0105``, `Figs. 13–24`): Book I :ref:`Prop. X <liber-primus-prop-10>` through :ref:`Prop. XXI <liber-primus-prop-21>`.
* **Tabula III** (folio ``0107``, `Figs. 25–31`): Book I 3D spatial theorems (:ref:`Prop. XXII <liber-primus-prop-22>` through :ref:`Prop. XXVI <liber-primus-prop-26>`).
* **Tabula IIII** (folio ``0109``, `Figs. 32–38`): Book II :ref:`Conic Lemmas I–III <liber-secundus-lemmata-conica>` and :ref:`Prop. I–II <liber-secundus-prop-01>`.
* **Tabula V** (folio ``0111``, `Figs. 39–46`): Book II :ref:`Prop. III <liber-secundus-prop-03>` through :ref:`Prop. IX <liber-secundus-prop-09>`.
* **Tabula VI** (folio ``0113``, `Figs. 47–54`): Book II :ref:`Lemmas IV–V <liber-secundus-prop-09>` and :ref:`Props. X–XIV <liber-secundus-prop-10>`.
* **Tabula VII** (folio ``0115``, `Figs. 55–61`): Book II :ref:`Prop. XIV <liber-secundus-prop-14>` through :ref:`Prop. XVIII <liber-secundus-prop-18>` (including the Incenter `Fig. 57`).
* **Tabula VIII** (folio ``0117``, `Figs. 62–73`): Book II :ref:`Props. XVII, XIX–XX <liber-secundus-prop-17>` and Appendix :ref:`Props. I–VI <appendix-geometrica-prop-01>`.
* **Tabula IX** (folio ``0119``, `Figs. 74–77`): Appendix :ref:`Props. VII–X <appendix-geometrica-prop-07>` (cylindrical hooves & hyperbolic annulus).
* **Tabula X** (folio ``0121``, `Figs. 78–85`): Appendix :ref:`Props. XI–XVI <appendix-geometrica-prop-11>`, :ref:`Indivisibles Scholium <appendix-geometrica-scholium-indivisibilia>`, and Book II :ref:`Prop. XXI Optical Rangefinder <liber-secundus-prop-21>` (`Fig. 85`).
