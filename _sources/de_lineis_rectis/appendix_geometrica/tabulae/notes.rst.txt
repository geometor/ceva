Ten Folding Copperplate Plates (Tabulae I–X, Figs. 1–85) — Notes
================================================================

.. folio:: 0103
   :page: Tabula I (Figs. 1–12)

**Archival Plate Inventory**:

* All ten full folding plates are extracted cleanly as contrast-normalized bitmaps (`0103.g0.png` through `0121.g0.png`), while every single individual figure (`fig. 1`–`fig. 85`) is extracted and paired inside its matching proposition leaf.
* Note that `Tab. X Fig. 85` (bottom-right of scan ``0121``) illustrates Ceva's single-station optical distance-measuring instrument from the *Scholium* to Book II Prop. XXI (pp. 59–60).
* In the 1678 Milan *editio princeps*, `Tabulae I–X` (`0103`–`0122`) are printed on odd rectos with blank even versos, followed by the BNCF copy's trailing endpapers and back pasteboard cover (`0123`–`0126`).
