Proposition VII: Cylindrical Hoof as Two-Thirds of Circumscribed Prism
======================================================================

.. folio:: 0084
   :page: p. 67

.. picture:: 0119.g74.svg
   :alt: Calibrated interactive SVG of Ceva Appendix Prop. VII Cylindrical Hoof / Ungula (Tab. IX Fig. 74)

.. picture:: 0119.g74.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula IX Fig. 74: Cylindrical hoof two-thirds of circumscribed triangular prism

**Proposition VII · Theorem VII — Volume of the Cylindrical Ungula (Hoof)** (pp. 67–69, `Tab. 9, Fig. 74`)

*A portion of a cylinder (:term:`ungula cylindrica <ungula-cylindrica>`) bounded by two semi-ellipses or semicircles :g:`BCA` and :g:`BDA` sharing a common secondary diameter :g:`BA`, together with the convex cylindrical surface between them, is **two-thirds** (*subsesquialtera*, $2 : 3$) of its circumscribed triangular prism :g:`QBP`!*

Beginning the Cavalieri indivisibles section of the Appendix, Ceva slices both the circumscribed prism :g:`QBP`, an inscribed double pyramid :g:`KBLE`, and the cylindrical hoof by planes parallel to the central triangle :g:`CED` (citing :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>`, *Conics* I.21: $\overline{CE}^2 : \overline{HF}^2 = \overline{AE}\cdot\overline{EB} : \overline{AF}\cdot\overline{FB}$). At every slice level, the triangular cross-section $\triangle IFM$ of the half-prism equals the sum of the pyramid slice $\triangle GFN$ and the cylindrical hoof slice $\triangle HFO$! Integrating all parallel triangles (*omnia triangula*) by :person:`Cavalieri's <cavalieri>` principle:

$$\text{Prism } QBP = \text{Ungula } CADB + 2 \cdot \text{Pyramid } KBLE = \text{Ungula } CADB + \frac{1}{3}\,\text{Prism } QBP \implies \text{Ungula} = \frac{2}{3}\,\text{Prism}!$$

**Corollary I** (p. 69): Any parallel slice $HFO$ cuts the ungula into a sub-segment equal to its circumscribed sub-prism minus the latent pyramidal frustum. **Corollary II** (p. 69): The central triangle :g:`CED` equals the base of the circumscribed triangular prism.
