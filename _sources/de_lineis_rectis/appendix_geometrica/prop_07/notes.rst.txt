Proposition VII: Cylindrical Hoof as Two-Thirds of Circumscribed Prism — Notes
==============================================================================

.. folio:: 0084
   :page: p. 67

**Archival & Symbolic Notes**:

* Printed pp. 67–69 (folios ``0084``–``0086``), opening `Tabula IX` (`0119`, Fig. 74).
* Remarkably, Archimedes's own mechanical proof of the cylindrical hoof volume in *The Method* was lost until J. L. Heiberg rediscovered the Archimedes Palimpsest in 1906—making Ceva's 1678 Cavalieri proof an independent seventeenth-century triumph!
* Verified by exact symbolic integration in :func:`geometor.ceva.polygon_tetrahedron.verify_appendix_invariants` (``ungula_to_prism_ratio == Rational(2, 3)``).
