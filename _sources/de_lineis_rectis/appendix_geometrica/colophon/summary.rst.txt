Imprimatur and Ludovico Monza Colophon — Summary
================================================

.. folio:: 0101
   :page: Imprimatur & Colophon

Folio ``0101`` (with blank verso ``0102``) preserves the Milanese ecclesiastical and senatorial *Imprimatur* approvals and the splendid Baroque eagle-and-putto woodcut device (**HINC MICAT · INDE FERIT**, monogram **LM**) of printer :person:`Ludovico Monza <monza>`.
