Imprimatur and Ludovico Monza Colophon — Notes
==============================================

.. folio:: 0101
   :page: Imprimatur & Colophon

**Archival Notes**:

* Folios ``0101`` (recto) and ``0102`` (blank verso), directly preceding the ten folding copperplate plates (`0103`–`0122`).
* **Baroque Printer's Device**: Below the ecclesiastical and civic imprimaturs stands the woodcut mark of :person:`Ludovico Monza <monza>`: a blindfolded winged putto astride a thunderbolt-grasping eagle inside a scrolled fruit cartouche bearing **HINC MICAT · INDE FERIT** and monogram **LM** beneath a cross.
