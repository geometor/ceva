Proposition II: Rectilinear Quadrature of Circular Lunes
========================================================

   :page: p. 63

.. picture:: 0117.g69.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula VIII Fig. 69: Circular lune GK quadrature

.. picture:: 0117.g70.png

**Proposition II · Theorem II** (pp. 63–64, `Tab. 8, Figs. 69–70`)

*Comparing circular sectors and inscribed triangles across two circles whose diameters satisfy rational square ratios, Ceva isolates exact rectilinear spaces equal to the circular lune :g:`GK` (`Fig. 69`) and the mixed curvilinear figure :g:`FCH` (`Fig. 70`) via Euclid XII.2 (circles stand as the squares on their diameters).*
