Proposition XII: Hyperbolic Conoid Inscribed in a Conical Frustum
=================================================================

.. folio:: 0093
   :page: p. 76

.. picture:: 0121.g79.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula X Fig. 79: Hyperbolic conoid ACE inscribed in conical frustum ABDE

**Proposition XII · Theorem XII** (pp. 76–77, `Tab. 10, Fig. 79`)

*If a conical frustum :g:`ABDE` bounded by two parallel planes circumscribes a hyperbolic conoid :g:`ACE` touching along the base circumference and vertex :g:`C`, then the conoid equals the conical frustum minus the inverted cone :g:`BAD` sharing the same base and height.*

Proved cleanly via circular slices using :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` (*Conics* III.16) and Euclid II.6.
