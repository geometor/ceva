Closing Scholium on Indivisibles, Errata & Printer's Note on ✠ — Notes
======================================================================

.. folio:: 0098
   :page: p. 81

**Archival & Methodological Milestone**:

* Printed pp. 81–83 (folios ``0098``–``0100``), `Tab. 10, Fig. 84`.
* **Mathematical Analysis of Ceva's Paradox**: Modern calculus immediately illuminates Ceva's 1678 insight: while disk volume integrates $\pi y^2\,dx$ directly along axis $x$, surface area of revolution requires the arc-length element $ds = \sqrt{1 + (dy/dx)^2}\,dx$. On a cone, $\sqrt{1 + (dy/dx)^2}$ is constant so axial slices work; on a sphere, $ds / dx = R / y$ varies continuously from $1$ to $\infty$, making naive axial circumference-summation fail!
* **Exact Location of the Printer's Note**: Multimodal scan inspection of folio ``0100`` (p. 83) establishes definitively that Monza's note (*Ad exprimendum plus more algebrico...*) stands at the foot of **p. 83**, right below the *Errata* table.
