Closing Scholium on Indivisibles, Errata & Printer's Note on ✠ — Summary
========================================================================

.. folio:: 0098
   :page: p. 81

Closing the treatise (pp. 81–83, `Tab. 10, Fig. 84`), Ceva gives a razor-sharp **counterexample to Cavalieri's indivisibles** on spherical surface area (where varying slant cosine defeats uniform axial slices, unlike on a cone), followed on p. 83 (folio ``0100``) by **F I N I S.**, the 20-line *Errata* table, and Ludovico Monza's authentic **Printer's Note** on the ``✠`` plus symbol.
