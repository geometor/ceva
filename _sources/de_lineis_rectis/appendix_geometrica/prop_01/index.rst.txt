Proposition I: Squared Sides and Opposite Angles of a Right Triangle
====================================================================

.. folio:: 0078
   :page: p. 61

.. picture:: 0117.g68.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula VIII Fig. 68: Right scalene triangle inscribed in semicircle

**Appendix Geometrica — Proposition I · Theorem I** (pp. 61–62, `Tab. 8, Fig. 68`)

*In a right-angled scalene triangle inscribed in a semicircle, the ratio of the square on the larger leg to the square on the smaller leg is greater than the ratio of the opposite acute angle to the smaller acute angle (citing :person:`Ptolemy's <ptolemy>` :cite:`Almagest <ptolemy:almagest>` chord inequality).*

Ceva establishes this fundamental inequality on circular segments and inscribed right triangles as the foundation for his lune quadratures in Propositions II–VI.
