Proposition XIII: Center of Gravity of a Hemisphere (5:3) — Summary
===================================================================

.. folio:: 0094
   :page: p. 77

*Appendix Prop. XIII* (pp. 77–78, `Tab. 10, Fig. 80`) proves by Archimedes/Cavalieri concentric slices and :person:`Luca Valerio <valerio>` (*De centro gravitatis* I.39) that a solid hemisphere's center of gravity divides its axis in exact ratio :math:`5 : 3` from the vertex.
