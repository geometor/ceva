Proposition XI: Volume of a Hyperbolic Conoid
=============================================

.. folio:: 0092
   :page: p. 75

.. picture:: 0121.g78.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula X Fig. 78: Hyperbolic conoid RFQ inside asymptotic cone ABC

**Proposition XI · Theorem XI** (pp. 75–76, `Tab. 10, Fig. 78`)

*A portion of a **hyperbolic conoid** (hyperboloid of revolution :g:`RFQ`) equals its containing asymptotic cone :g:`ABC` minus two elementary solids: (1) the small vertex cone :g:`EBD` whose axis equals the semi-transverse axis :g:`FB` of the generating hyperbola, and (2) the right cylinder :g:`EP` sharing that base and the height of the conoid.*

Citing :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` (*Conics* II.10), every parallel circular slice of the conical frustum :g:`AEDC` equals the sum of the conoid circle and the constant cylinder circle—immediately yielding the exact volume by Cavalieri's principle!
