Proposition XI: Volume of a Hyperbolic Conoid
=============================================

.. folio:: 0092
   :page: p. 75

.. picture:: 0121.g78.png
   :alt: 1678 copperplate Tabula X Fig. 78: Hyperbolic conoid RFQ inside asymptotic cone ABC

**Proposition XI · Theorem XI** (pp. 75–76, `Tab. 10, Fig. 78`)

*A portion of a hyperbolic conoid is equal to the cone containing the portion of the conoid, minus two solids: of which the one is a cone whose axis equals half the transverse side of the generating hyperbola,*
 *and its base is the section of the said containing cone touching the portion and parallel to its base; while the other solid is a cylinder whose base is that of the subtracted cone, and whose axis is the same as that of the conoid.*

Let there be a hyperbolic conoid whose section through the axis :g:`FS` is the hyperbola :g:`RFQ`; and let the section of the containing cone through the same axis be triangle :g:`ABC`; therefore the lines :g:`BA`, :g:`BC` will be asymptotes, and :g:`FB` the semi-transverse side of the same hyperbola :g:`RFQ`.
Then let there be a plane touching the conoid at :g:`F` (which will be parallel to the plane of the conoid's base), and hence the section made in cone :g:`ABC` will be a circle whose diameter is line :g:`ED` and center :g:`F`.
Finally, a cylinder having been conceived on this same base, whose parallelogram through the axis :g:`FS` is :g:`EOPD`, I say that conoid :g:`RFQ` is equal to cone :g:`ABC`, minus cone :g:`EBD` and cylinder :g:`EP`.
Let any point :g:`K` be taken on axis :g:`FS`, through which let a plane be drawn parallel to the plane touching the conoid at :g:`F`, so that it cuts the cone containing the conoid, the cylinder, and the conoid itself; and the sections will be circles whose diameters will be lines :g:`NI`, :g:`LG`, :g:`MH`.
Therefore, because (margin: *Conics* II.10) rectangle :g:`AQC`, that is square :g:`FD`, indeed :g:`SP` itself, together with square :g:`SQ`, is equal to square :g:`SC`; likewise because rectangle :g:`NHI`, that is square :g:`FD`, namely :g:`KG` itself, together with square :g:`KH`, is equal to square :g:`KI`;
and finally, since square :g:`FD` is equal to itself, it is evident that all the squares, indeed all the circles whose diameters are :g:`AC`, :g:`NI`, :g:`ED`, etc., of frustum :g:`AEDC` of the cone

.. folio:: 0093
   :page: p. 76

containing the conoid are equal to all the circles about diameters :g:`RQ`, :g:`MH`, etc. of the conoid, together with all the circles of cylinder :g:`EP`, whose diameters are :g:`OP`, :g:`LG`, :g:`ED`, etc.
Since this is so, cone :g:`ABC` will be equal to conoid :g:`RFQ`, together with cone :g:`EBD` and cylinder :g:`EP`; that is, cone :g:`ABD`, minus cone :g:`EBD` and cylinder :g:`EP`, will be equal to conoid :g:`RFQ`, which was to be demonstrated.
