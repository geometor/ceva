Proposition X: Hyperbolic Annulus as Two-Thirds of Asymptotic Cylinder — Summary
================================================================================

.. folio:: 0091
   :page: p. 74

*Appendix Prop. X* (pp. 74–75, `Tab. 9, Fig. 77`) proves via Cavalieri's circular slices and :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` (*Conics* II.11) that a revolved **hyperbolic annulus** equals exactly :math:`\frac{2}{3}` of its asymptotic cylinder.
