geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics¶

Exact static-barycentric solver and constructive proofs for Ceva’s Theorem (1678).

Models Giovanni Ceva’s De lineis rectis se invicem secantibus statica constructio (Milan, 1678) in 100% exact symbolic algebra over \(\mathbb{Q}\) and symbolic rings using sympy and geometor.model:

  • Static-Mass Solver (solve_static_barycentrics()): Assigns exact homogeneous weights \((u : v : w)\) to vertices \((A, B, C)\) (Liber Primus, Elementa I–III & Scholium p. 5 on negative weights \(F - A\)), computes the three Archimedean lever fulcrums \(D \in BC\), \(E \in CA\), \(F \in AB\), the static center of gravity \(P\), signed segment ratios, signed sub-triangle area ratios (Appendix Geometrica), and verifies \(\frac{AF}{FB}\cdot\frac{BD}{DC}\cdot\frac{CE}{EA} = 1\).

  • Canonical Rational Triangle Centers (CANONICAL_CENTERS, evaluate_canonical_centers()): Evaluates \(X(1), X(2), X(4), X(6), X(7), X(8)\) and the exterior \(A\)-excenter \(J_A\) on the Heronian \((13, 14, 15)\) triangle \(A=(5, 12), B=(0, 0), C=(14, 0)\) where every coordinate lies in \(\mathbb{Q}\).

  • Constructive ``@proposition`` Proofs:

    • construct_ceva_concurrency(): Targeted geometor.model construction of interior and exterior cevian concurrency (Liber Primus, Elementa II–III & Prop. VI).

    • construct_isotomic_conjugate(): Midpoint-reflection construction of the isotomic conjugate \((u^{-1} : v^{-1} : w^{-1})\) pairing Ceva’s contact point \(X(7)\) (Liber Secundus, Prop. I) with the Nagel point \(X(8)\).

    • construct_cevian_menelaus_harmonic(): Dual Menelaus transversal \(EF \cap BC = D'\) (Liber Primus, Elementum I & Prop. VIII) proving harmonic division \((B, C; D, D') = -1\).

    • verify_spread_ceva(): Rational Trigonometry Spread Ceva’s Theorem verified via exact rational spreads and quadrances with zero square roots.

geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.directed_ratio_collinear(p_start: sympy.geometry.Point2D, p_div: sympy.geometry.Point2D, p_end: sympy.geometry.Point2D) → sympy.Expr¶

Compute the exact directed segment ratio (p_start -> p_div) / (p_div -> p_end).

Projects collinear displacements onto p_end - p_start so no square roots are introduced. Positive when p_div lies strictly between p_start and p_end, and negative when p_div divides p_start p_end externally.

Parameters:

  • p_start – First endpoint of the directed base segment.

  • p_div – Collinear division point (lever fulcrum).

  • p_end – Second endpoint of the directed base segment.

Returns:

Exact simplified SymPy expression for the signed ratio.

Raises:

ValueError – If the three points are not collinear or p_div == p_end.

geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.solve_static_barycentrics(u: SymScalar, v: SymScalar, w: SymScalar, vertices: tuple[tuple[SymScalar, SymScalar] | sympy.geometry.Point2D, ...] = HERONIAN_VERTICES, *, name: str = 'P') → StaticBarycentricResult¶

Solve Ceva’s static equilibrium for homogeneous vertex weights (u, v, w).

Places weights u at A, v at B, and w at C. By Archimedes’ Law of the Lever (Liber Primus, Lemma I):

  • \(D = \frac{v B + w C}{v + w}\) balances \(B\) and \(C\) with \(v \cdot \overline{BD} = w \cdot \overline{DC}\),

  • \(E = \frac{w C + u A}{w + u}\) balances \(C\) and \(A\) with \(w \cdot \overline{CE} = u \cdot \overline{EA}\),

  • \(F = \frac{u A + v B}{u + v}\) balances \(A\) and \(B\) with \(u \cdot \overline{AF} = v \cdot \overline{FB}\),

and all three cevians \(AD, BE, CF\) concur at the static center of gravity \(P = \frac{u A + v B + w C}{u + v + w}\).

Parameters:

  • u – Exact weight at vertex A (integer, rational, or SymPy expression).

  • v – Exact weight at vertex B.

  • w – Exact weight at vertex C.

  • vertices – Three non-collinear vertices (A, B, C).

  • name – Label for the concurrency center.

Returns:

Populated StaticBarycentricResult with verified lever and area invariants.

Raises:

ValueError – If any pairwise or total weight sum vanishes (parallel cevian / point at infinity) or if vertices are collinear or contain Float values.

geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.prove_ceva_barycentric_identity() → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Prove Ceva’s Theorem and sub-triangle area proportions universally over symbolic coordinates.

Uses indeterminate vertex coordinates \(A=(x_A, y_A), B=(x_B, y_B), C=(x_C, y_C)\) and symbolic weights \((u, v, w)\) to verify that:

  1. \(P = \frac{u A + v B + w C}{u + v + w}\) is collinear with \((A, D)\), \((B, E)\), and \((C, F)\).

  2. \(\frac{\overline{AF}}{\overline{FB}} \cdot \frac{\overline{BD}}{\overline{DC}} \cdot \frac{\overline{CE}}{\overline{EA}} - 1 = 0\).

  3. \([\triangle PBC] : [\triangle PCA] : [\triangle PAB] = u : v : w\).

Returns:

Dictionary of simplified symbolic residuals (every residual identically 0).

Construct exact cevian concurrency in Model for weights (u, v, w).

Supports both interior concurrency (all weights same sign, e.g. Gergonne (24, 28, 21)) and exterior concurrency with negative static weights (Elementum III & p. 5 Scholium, e.g. (-14, 15, 13)). Constructs P strictly as the intersection AD ∩ BE via model.intersect and deductively demonstrates P ∈ CF and Ceva’s ratio product = 1.

Parameters:

  • u – Exact rational weight at A.

  • v – Exact rational weight at B.

  • w – Exact rational weight at C.

  • vertices – Rational vertex coordinates (A, B, C).

Returns:

Verified Model with auto_intersect=False and zero floats.

geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.construct_isotomic_conjugate(u: int | sympy.Rational = 24, v: int | sympy.Rational = 28, w: int | sympy.Rational = 21, vertices: tuple[tuple[int, int], tuple[int, int], tuple[int, int]] = HERONIAN_VERTICES) → geometor.model.Model¶

Construct the isotomic conjugate \(P' = (u^{-1}:v^{-1}:w^{-1})\) via side-midpoint reflection.

Reflects each cevian foot \(D, E, F\) of \(P = (u:v:w)\) across the midpoints \(M_A, M_B, M_C\) of \(BC, CA, AB\) to obtain \(D^*, E^*, F^*\) satisfying \(BD^* = DC\), \(CE^* = EA\), \(AF^* = FB\), and proves by Ceva’s Converse (Liber Secundus, Prop. I Corollarium) that \(AD^*, BE^*, CF^*\) concur at the isotomic conjugate \(P^*\). For \((24:28:21)\), \(P = X(7)\) (Gergonne) and \(P^* = X(8)\) (Nagel).

Parameters:

  • u – Exact non-zero rational weight at A.

  • v – Exact non-zero rational weight at B.

  • w – Exact non-zero rational weight at C.

  • vertices – Rational vertex coordinates (A, B, C).

Returns:

Verified Model proving isotomic segment equalities and concurrency.

geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.construct_cevian_menelaus_harmonic(u: int | sympy.Rational = 24, v: int | sympy.Rational = 28, w: int | sympy.Rational = 21, vertices: tuple[tuple[int, int], tuple[int, int], tuple[int, int]] = HERONIAN_VERTICES) → geometor.model.Model¶

Construct the projective harmonic conjugate D' = EF ∩ BC of a cevian foot D.

Combines Ceva’s interior concurrency (Elementum II, Prop. VII) with Ceva’s static proof of Menelaus’s Transversal Theorem (Elementum I, Prop. VIII): for any point P = (u:v:w) with v != w, the line EF joining two cevian feet meets the third side BC at D' such that (B, C; D, D') = -1.

Parameters:

  • u – Exact rational weight at A.

  • v – Exact rational weight at B (must differ from w so EF meets BC finitely).

  • w – Exact rational weight at C.

  • vertices – Rational vertex coordinates (A, B, C).

Returns:

Verified Model whose collinear points (B, C, D, D') satisfy is_harmonic(B, C, D, D') == True.

geometor.ceva.static_barycentrics.verify_spread_ceva(u: int | sympy.Rational = 14, v: int | sympy.Rational = 15, w: int | sympy.Rational = 13, vertices: tuple[tuple[int, int], tuple[int, int], tuple[int, int]] = HERONIAN_VERTICES) → SpreadCevaResult¶

Verify Rational Trigonometry Spread Ceva’s Theorem with zero square roots.

For concurrent cevians AD, BE, CF meeting at P = (u:v:w), computes the six exact vertex spreads s(BAD), s(DAC), s(CBE), s(EBA), s(ACF), s(FCB) and six segment quadrances over \(\mathbb{Q}\) and verifies:

\[\frac{s(\angle BAD)}{s(\angle DAC)} \cdot \frac{s(\angle CBE)}{s(\angle EBA)} \cdot \frac{s(\angle ACF)}{s(\angle FCB)} = 1, \qquad \frac{Q(AF)}{Q(FB)} \cdot \frac{Q(BD)}{Q(DC)} \cdot \frac{Q(CE)}{Q(EA)} = 1.\]
Parameters:

  • u – Exact rational weight at A.

  • v – Exact rational weight at B.

  • w – Exact rational weight at C.

  • vertices – Rational vertex coordinates (A, B, C).

Returns:

Verified SpreadCevaResult over \(\mathbb{Q}\).
