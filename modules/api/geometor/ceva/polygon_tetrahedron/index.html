geometor.ceva.polygon_tetrahedron¶

Multilateral polygons, circumscribed conics, 3D tetrahedra, and Appendix theorems (1678).

Formalizes Liber Primus (Props. V Coroll., XXII–XXVI), Liber Secundus (Lemma I, Props. I–XIV), and Appendix Geometrica (Props. I–XVI) of Giovanni Ceva’s De lineis rectis se invicem secantibus statica constructio (Milan, 1678) in 100% exact symbolic algebra:

  • Odd Multilateral Polygon Ceva Concurrency (verify_odd_polygon_ceva(), construct_pentagon_ceva()): Verifies over \(\mathbb{Q}\) that rays joining the \(2m + 1\) vertices of an odd polygon through a common point \(P\) divide the opposite sides in cyclic directed product \(+1\).

  • Circumscribed Conic Contact Ratio (*Liber Secundus, Lemma I*) (prove_circumscribed_polygon_lemma()): Proves symbolically that any \(n\)-gon circumscribed around a circle or ellipse (via Ceva’s Borelli–Serenus cylindrical / affine projection) divides its sides at the contact points in cyclic product \(1\).

  • 3D Tetrahedron 7-Line Static Equilibrium (*Liber Primus, Prop. XXIII*) (solve_tetrahedron_static_equilibrium()): Computes the 6 edge fulcrums, 4 face centers of gravity, and unique 3D center of gravity \(O\) for four spatial weights \((m_A, m_B, m_C, m_D)\), proving that all 4 vertex-to-face-barycenter cevians and all 3 opposite-edge fulcrum joins concur at \(O\) (“mira quadam concordia”).

  • 3D Skew Quadrilateral Concurrency (*Liber Primus, Elementum V Corollarium*) (verify_skew_quadrilateral_ceva()): Proves coplanarity and intersection of opposite-side fulcrum joins on a non-planar 3D quadrilateral.

  • Appendix Geometrica Quadratures, Cubatures & Centers of Gravity (verify_appendix_invariants()): Symbolic verification of Hippocratean lune quadrature (Prop. II), cylindrical hoof \(\frac{2}{3}\) prism cubature (Prop. VII), hemisphere \(5:3\) centroid (Prop. XIII), and paraboloid \(2:1\) centroid (Prop. XIV).

Verify Ceva’s cyclic ratio product = 1 for any odd (2m + 1)-gon.

For each vertex \(V_i\) (\(0 \le i < 2m + 1\)), projects the ray \(V_i P\) onto the opposite side line \(V_{i+m} V_{i+m+1}\) (indices modulo \(2m + 1\)) at \(Q_i\) and verifies:

\[\prod_{i=0}^{2m} \frac{\overline{V_{i+m} Q_i}}{\overline{Q_i V_{i+m+1}}} = 1.\]
Parameters:

  • vertices – Sequence of 2m + 1 planar vertices (n >= 3 odd).

  • center – Concurrency point P not lying on any side line or parallel ray.

Returns:

Verified OddPolygonCevaResult with exact rational/symbolic ratios.

geometor.ceva.polygon_tetrahedron.prove_circumscribed_polygon_lemma(n_sides: int = 5) → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Prove Ceva’s Liber Secundus, Lemma I for polygons circumscribed about circles & ellipses.

In Liber Secundus, Lemma I (tab. 4. figs. 32–35), Ceva proves that if an \(n\)-gon is circumscribed around a circle—or, by cylindrical projection citing Borelli and Serenus, around any ellipse—the contact points \(T_0, \dots, T_{n-1}\) divide the sides in cyclic product \(1\). This function verifies:

  1. Equal tangent lengths \(t_k\) from each vertex \(V_k\) to a circle give \(\prod_{k=0}^{n-1} \frac{t_k}{t_{k+1}} = 1\).

  2. Applying a general symbolic invertible affine map \(X' = M X + b\) (the planar realization of Serenus/Borelli cylindrical section mapping the circle to an arbitrary ellipse) preserves every collinear contact ratio \(\frac{V'_k T'_k}{T'_k V'_{k+1}}\) and maintains \(\prod_{k=0}^{n-1} \frac{V'_k T'_k}{T'_k V'_{k+1}} = 1\).

Parameters:

n_sides – Number of polygon sides (n_sides >= 3, odd or even).

Returns:

Dictionary of simplified symbolic residuals (all identically 0).

Solve Ceva’s 3D tetrahedron 7-line static concurrency (Liber Primus, Prop. XXIII).

Places exact weights \((m_A, m_B, m_C, m_D)\) at the four vertices \((A, B, C, D)\) of a 3D tetrahedron (tab. 3. fig. 28) and verifies that:

  1. The 4 spatial cevians \(A G_A, B G_B, C G_C, D G_D\) joining each vertex to the static center of gravity of its opposite triangular face concur at \(O\).

  2. The 3 spatial bimedians \(E_{AB} E_{CD}, E_{AC} E_{BD}, E_{AD} E_{BC}\) joining the lever fulcrums of opposite skew edges ALSO concur at the exact same point \(O\) (“mira quadam concordia”).

Parameters:

  • weights – Four exact non-zero vertex weights (m_A, m_B, m_C, m_D).

  • vertices – Four non-coplanar 3D vertices (A, B, C, D).

Returns:

Verified TetrahedronStaticResult over \(\mathbb{Q}\) (or symbolic ring).

geometor.ceva.polygon_tetrahedron.verify_appendix_invariants() → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Verify key quadrature, cubature, and centroid propositions in Appendix Geometrica.

Symbolically confirms four signature results from Printed pp. 63–83:

  1. Prop. II (Hippocratean Lune Quadrature): For an isosceles right triangle of legs \(R\) and hypotenuse \(R\sqrt{2}\), the lune bounded by the semicircle on the hypotenuse (radius \(R/\sqrt{2}\)) and the quadrant arc of radius \(R\) has exact area \(\frac{1}{2} R^2 = [\triangle ABC]\) (all \(\pi\) terms cancel).

  2. Prop. VII (Cylindrical Hoof / Ungula Cubature): A plane through a diameter of the base of a right circular cylinder (radius \(R\), altitude \(H\)) cuts off a cylindrical hoof whose volume \(\frac{2}{3} R^2 H\) equals exactly \(\frac{2}{3}\) of the circumscribed triangular prism (volume \(R^2 H\)).

  3. Prop. XIII (Hemisphere Center of Gravity): Divides the axis from vertex to base in the exact ratio \(5 : 3\) (\(\bar{z} = \frac{3}{8} R\) from the base, \(\frac{5}{8} R\) from the vertex).

  4. Prop. XIV (Parabolic Conoid Center of Gravity): Divides the axis from vertex to base in the exact ratio \(2 : 1\) (\(\bar{z} = \frac{2}{3} H\) from the vertex).

Returns:

Dictionary of exact symbolic ratios and zero-residuals.
